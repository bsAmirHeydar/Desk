# Model Disagreement and Unmodeled Driver Risk

Market behavior is not Direction authority, but it is valuable diagnostic evidence. If a well-specified causal thesis predicts front-end yields, credit, FX or the target asset should react in a particular sequence and those leaders materially fail, the brain must consider that the model is incomplete.

V21 records expected signatures **before** using the subsequent market reaction as a diagnostic. A large unexplained disagreement raises `UNMODELED_DRIVER_RISK` rather than triggering post-hoc rationalization.

Possible responses are early review, constraint, hold/no-trade and research escalation. A decision-critical unmodeled-driver state blocks exposure until a plausible missing driver or data failure is identified. The system is never allowed to say “the market is wrong” merely to protect the thesis.
