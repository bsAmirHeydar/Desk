# USDJPY Cognitive Book

USDJPY requires explicit competition among Fed/BoJ policy, real-rate differential, carry, Japanese wage/inflation dynamics, official intervention, hedging and volatility/liquidity paths.

Intervention can create a strong event-micro bearish path while the multi-day rate-differential thesis remains bullish. V21 must keep those horizons separate. Suspected intervention is not an official flow fact unless evidence supports that classification; it can still be a mechanical or model-disagreement hypothesis.

## V21.1 high-value discriminators
- Separate structural US-Japan rate/carry direction from tactical intervention and carry-unwind paths.
- Intervention may own micro/session direction while Module 89 retains an opposite multi-day/structural state.
- DXY is partially endogenous to broad USD moves; prioritize US rates, JGB yields and intervention evidence for independent roots.
- A volatility/carry-unwind path can dominate price without being relabeled as Fundamental Direction.
