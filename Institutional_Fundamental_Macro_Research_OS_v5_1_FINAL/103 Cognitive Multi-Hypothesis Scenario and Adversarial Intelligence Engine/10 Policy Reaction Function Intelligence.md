# Policy Reaction Function Intelligence

Macro releases matter through a changing policy reaction function. V21 therefore makes the reaction function an explicit state rather than an implied paragraph.

For each relevant authority estimate qualitative sensitivities to inflation, labor, growth, financial stability, financial conditions, fiscal supply interaction and currency constraints. These sensitivities are point-in-time and regime-dependent. The same payroll or CPI surprise can transmit differently when the central bank is fighting inflation, protecting employment, reacting to financial stress, or constrained by currency weakness.

The output is not a guessed Taylor-rule coefficient. It is a transparent map of which variables currently influence the expected policy path, how strongly, what evidence supports that interpretation and what observation would change it.
