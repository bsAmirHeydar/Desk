# High-Conflict Crisis and Fragmented-Market Mode

During crises, causal relationships can change rapidly and several markets may be controlled by different mechanisms. V21 permits a larger scenario set, shorter validity windows, more frequent review and explicit `FRAGMENTED` states.

The system should not force a global narrative when funding, policy, liquidation and safe-haven channels conflict. Decision-critical data gaps, broken leader relationships or unmonitorable tail risk justify no-trade even if one scenario appears attractive.
