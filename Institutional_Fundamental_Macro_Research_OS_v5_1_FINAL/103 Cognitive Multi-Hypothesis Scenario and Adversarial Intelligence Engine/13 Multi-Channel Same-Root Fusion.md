# Multi-Channel Same-Root Fusion

D1 and D3 correctly prevent one shock from becoming several independent confirmations. V21 hardens the rule: **deduplicate independence, not effects**.

A single CPI root may create a rates channel supportive of equities and a growth channel obstructive to equities. The independent-root count is still one, but both channels remain in the causal map. The root can therefore be `CONTESTED` even though it counts once statistically.

For each root store all material channels, their horizon, alignment, state, materiality and evidence grade. Only a net root with genuinely supportive active channels and no decision-material opposing channel can count as clean support. Contested roots stay contested; they are not reduced to the most severe descendant and they are not multiplied by the number of datasets.
