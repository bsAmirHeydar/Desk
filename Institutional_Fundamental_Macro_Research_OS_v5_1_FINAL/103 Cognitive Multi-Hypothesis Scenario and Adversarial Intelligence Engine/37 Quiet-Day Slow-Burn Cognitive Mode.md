# Quiet-Day and Slow-Burn Cognitive Mode

Not every day needs six dramatic scenarios. On a quiet day, the brain should remain compact and focus on persistent states, gradual expectation revisions, flows, funding, valuation, scheduled mechanics and latent catalysts.

The hypothesis set can contain a stable base case plus one or two credible challengers. Complexity is reduced, but the model-disagreement and driver-transition checks remain active so that an unscheduled shock can reopen the tournament immediately.
