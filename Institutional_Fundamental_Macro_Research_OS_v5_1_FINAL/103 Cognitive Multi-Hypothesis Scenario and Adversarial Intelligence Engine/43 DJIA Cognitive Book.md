# DJIA Cognitive Book

DJIA requires attention to cyclical growth, rates, constituent earnings and its price-weighted construction. A large move in a high-priced constituent can create index behavior that does not represent a broad macro state.

V21 therefore keeps an idiosyncratic/mechanical challenger whenever constituent concentration is material. The global reconciler should not treat a DJIA divergence from Nasdaq or S&P as a contradiction until constituent and sector mechanisms are tested.
