# Cognitive Multi-Hypothesis Scenario and Adversarial Intelligence Engine

## Mission

Module 103 is the V21 **Cognitive Hardening** layer. It does not replace D1–D4 and it is not a new directional science. Its job is to force the existing Alpha Lab sciences to think in a more realistic way when evidence is incomplete, causal paths compete, horizons disagree, regimes shift, or the market behaves in a way the current thesis did not predict.

The core change is from a mostly linear analytical sequence to a **controlled cognitive loop**:

`evidence → independent states → rival hypotheses → horizon tensor → expectation/surprise → reaction function → regime-conditioned causal graph → root/channel fusion → reflexivity → consumption/asymmetry → scenario tree → driver transition → model-disagreement check → cross-asset reconciliation → thesis builder ↔ thesis destroyer → pre-mortem → utility adjudication → rich research intent → BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE adapter → D4 learning`.

## Non-negotiable authority boundary

- Module 89 remains the sole creator of **Fundamental Direction** for the Fundamental strategy.
- Module 103 cannot flip a Fundamental direction.
- Module 103 cannot create BUY/SELL from a pre-cognitive `NO_TRADE`.
- A mechanical, forced-flow or liquidity-dislocation edge can be detected and routed, but it remains outside the Fundamental strategy unless separately researched and promoted under its own strategy contract.
- Price behavior may expose model disagreement; price behavior is not itself Direction authority.
- Scenario probabilities are qualitative bands unless an empirically calibrated estimand is explicitly authorized by D4.

## V21.1 semantic integrity hardening
V21.1 does **not** add another science layer. It closes the gap between conceptual reasoning and machine-enforced meaning: every first-class cognitive state is present or explicitly classified as not applicable/unavailable/undetermined; load-bearing evidence is lineage-referenced; material triggers are monitorable; cross-field IDs, horizons, cutoff times and directions must reconcile; and the full cognitive state is available to D4 telemetry. The design remains materiality-driven to avoid manufactured complexity.
