# NASDAQ100 Cognitive Book

Nasdaq requires simultaneous testing of rate-duration, earnings/cash-flow, AI-capex, credit, USD, dealer-options, systematic-flow, passive/rebalance and Treasury-supply hypotheses. Rate declines can be bullish in a benign disinflation regime and bearish when they signal growth panic; the regime-conditioned causal graph must decide which path is active.

The active day-trading horizon should predeclare expected leaders such as US2Y, real yields, DXY, credit spreads and volatility. Dealer/gamma or forced-flow paths can temporarily dominate execution while remaining outside Fundamental Direction authority. Extreme crowding is fragility, not an automatic short signal.

## V21.1 high-value discriminators
- **Rate relief vs growth scare:** lower yields are supportive only when earnings/credit do not simultaneously deteriorate enough to dominate the duration channel.
- **Duration vs earnings:** mega-cap earnings revisions and AI-capex expectations can dominate rate sensitivity; identify which channel leads the active horizon.
- **Mechanical path:** dealer gamma, CTA/vol-control and passive/rebalance flows may dominate the intraday path without becoming Fundamental Direction.
- **Treasury-supply interaction:** rising term premium from supply is not the same root as inflation-policy tightening; keep the mechanisms separate.
