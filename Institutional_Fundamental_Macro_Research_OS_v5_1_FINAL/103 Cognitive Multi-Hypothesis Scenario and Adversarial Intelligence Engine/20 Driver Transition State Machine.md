# Driver Transition State Machine

Dominant drivers change during the day. V21 uses `STABLE → WEAKENING → CHALLENGED → TRANSFER → TAKEOVER`, with `FRAGMENTED`, `RESET` and `UNDETERMINED` alternatives.

A transition records the current driver, strongest challenger, first detectable time, evidence, expected leader, target horizon and failure trigger. A mechanical driver can take temporary control of path without invalidating the structural thesis.

The next review schedule should accelerate during `WEAKENING`, `CHALLENGED` and `TRANSFER` states. When `TAKEOVER` occurs, the hypothesis tournament and scenario tree must be refreshed rather than merely editing the old narrative label.
