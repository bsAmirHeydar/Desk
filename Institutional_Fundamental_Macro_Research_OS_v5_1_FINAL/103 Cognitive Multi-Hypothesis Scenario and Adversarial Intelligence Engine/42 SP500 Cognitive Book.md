# SP500 Cognitive Book

S&P 500 scenario construction balances rates, broad earnings, breadth, credit, buybacks, passive flows, zero-DTE mechanics, fiscal supply and liquidity. The broad index can remain fundamentally healthy while a mechanical close or funding shock controls the session path.

Cross-sector breadth can help diagnose whether the causal story is broad or concentrated, but price breadth is confirmation/diagnostic evidence rather than a substitute for Fundamental cause. Counterfactual D4 records should distinguish saved losses from missed broad-market trends.
