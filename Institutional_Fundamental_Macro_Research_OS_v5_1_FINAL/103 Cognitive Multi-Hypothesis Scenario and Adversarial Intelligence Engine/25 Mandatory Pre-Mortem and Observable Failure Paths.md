# Mandatory Pre-Mortem and Observable Failure Paths

Before an actionable permission survives V21, assume the contemplated action loses and identify three to seven plausible failure mechanisms.

Each failure path needs an early warning, a monitorability flag and a response. Examples include rate reversal, flow exhaustion, reaction-function misread, narrative takeover, unmodeled headline, funding shock, mechanical distortion or capacity failure.

If a decision-critical failure mechanism cannot be monitored during the validity window, mark `unmonitorable_decision_critical_risk = true`. That is a legitimate reason to hold/no-trade even when the base thesis is attractive.
