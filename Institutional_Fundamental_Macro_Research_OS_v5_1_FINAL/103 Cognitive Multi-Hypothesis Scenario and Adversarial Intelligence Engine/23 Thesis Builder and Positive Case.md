# Thesis Builder and Positive Case

The Thesis Builder constructs the strongest admissible positive case for the current Fundamental strategy action. It identifies the minimum sufficient load-bearing roots, active causal channels, expected leaders, current scenario, remaining asymmetry, validity window and the exact conditions that keep the thesis alive.

It must distinguish what is observed from what is inferred and must avoid padding the thesis with contextual facts that do not change the decision. The builder is not rewarded for eloquence; it is rewarded for causal economy, falsifiability and decision relevance.
