# Confidence Calibration and No-Fake-Probability Law

V21 uses separate evidence-quality, causal-coherence, model-uncertainty and transmission-certainty states instead of presenting an unexplained `0.82 confidence` as if it were a calibrated probability.

Legacy ordinal confidence fields may remain for backward compatibility, but Production reports must label them as ordinal or evidence-quality indicators. A numeric predictive probability is allowed only when the exact event, horizon, reference class and calibration evidence are declared and D4 has authorized the estimand.
