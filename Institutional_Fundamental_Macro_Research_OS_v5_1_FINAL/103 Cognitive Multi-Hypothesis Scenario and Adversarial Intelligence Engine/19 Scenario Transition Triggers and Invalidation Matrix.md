# Scenario Transition Triggers and Invalidation Matrix

A scenario without an observable transition trigger is prose, not a decision object. V21 therefore requires machine-readable transitions.

Each transition states `from scenario → trigger → to scenario`. Triggers can be facts, rate thresholds, policy communication, cross-asset leader changes, flow reversals, funding stress, mechanics, narrative takeovers or evidence-quality failures. Technical price levels may be included only when they are execution or diagnostic observations, not Fundamental direction creators.

The system should distinguish confirmation from invalidation. Confirmation strengthens the current path; invalidation breaks a load-bearing assumption. A scenario can also lose primacy without being invalidated when a challenger gains explanatory control.
