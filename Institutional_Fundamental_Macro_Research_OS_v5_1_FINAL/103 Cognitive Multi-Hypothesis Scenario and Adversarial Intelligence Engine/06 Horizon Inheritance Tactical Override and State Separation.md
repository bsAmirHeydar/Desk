# Horizon Inheritance, Tactical Override and State Separation

Inheritance must be explicit. A long-horizon state may influence priors, expected persistence and reversal risk, but no score or direction is copied into a shorter horizon without a causal reason.

A tactical override is legitimate when a short-lived but material driver controls the path while the structural thesis remains intact. Example: multi-day USDJPY bullish carry while an official intervention creates a strongly bearish event-micro state. The system should report both states and route the trade according to the active strategy horizon rather than declaring one of them “wrong.”

Expiry matters. Tactical overrides require a reversion or transition condition. Structural states require explicit update triggers when new data, policy, earnings or balance-sheet facts can alter them.
