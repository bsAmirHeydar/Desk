# XAUUSD Cognitive Book

Gold requires explicit competition among real-yield, USD, policy, official-sector demand, physical balance, geopolitical safe-haven, funding/liquidation and COMEX-mechanical explanations. COMEX behavior is not the entire global Gold market.

The scenario tree should distinguish a durable real-yield/FX repricing path from a short-lived safe-haven spike, a funding-driven liquidation, and a physical/official-sector slow-burn when those candidates are material. Expected leaders commonly include real yields, DXY, Treasury volatility and relevant physical/ETF evidence. A price rally without the expected macro leaders raises model-disagreement risk rather than automatically validating the safe-haven story.
