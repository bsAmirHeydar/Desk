# Multi-Dimensional Consumption and Remaining Asymmetry

V11 already defines Consumption as a vector. V21 makes the live decision use the vector directly instead of collapsing it into a pseudo-precise percentage.

The production representation separates information awareness, expectation incorporation, instrument repricing, cross-asset transmission, positioning adjustment, mechanical-flow exhaustion, narrative saturation, valuation/payoff compression, time decay, catalyst expiry, flow continuation/exhaustion and reinforcement/reopening.

A 0–100 value, when used, is an **ordinal communication index** unless an explicit D4-calibrated estimand says otherwise. It is not “percent of the move consumed.”

Remaining asymmetry is a residual causal judgement based on what still has room to propagate, opposing-force load, payoff compression, crowding, path risk and reopening potential. It is never calculated as `100 - consumption`.
