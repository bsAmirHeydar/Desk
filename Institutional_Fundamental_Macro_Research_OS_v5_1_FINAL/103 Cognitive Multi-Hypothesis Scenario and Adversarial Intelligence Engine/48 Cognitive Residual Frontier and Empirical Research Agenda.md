# Cognitive Residual Frontier and Empirical Research Agenda

V21 closes the major **conceptual** gaps identified after V20, but it does not claim that every cognitive state improves trading outcomes. The following remain empirical questions for D4:

- how many independent supporting roots are needed before “broad confirmation” adds value;
- which uncertainty types are most decision-relevant by market/horizon;
- whether unmodeled-driver alerts reduce losses or merely add no-trade friction;
- which scenario-transition signals improve timing;
- whether the Thesis Destroyer catches useful failure modes;
- which interaction hypotheses survive holdout;
- whether case-based analogues add incremental value after causal state controls;
- whether complexity penalties improve or damage opportunity capture;
- which outside-strategy edges deserve independent strategy development.

No further cognitive feature should gain production authority merely because it appears intellectually attractive. V21 is a hardening release; D4 remains the judge of empirical value.
