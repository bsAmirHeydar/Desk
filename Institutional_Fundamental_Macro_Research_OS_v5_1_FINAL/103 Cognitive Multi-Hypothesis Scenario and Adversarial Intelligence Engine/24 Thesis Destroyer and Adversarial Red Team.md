# Thesis Destroyer and Adversarial Red Team

The Thesis Destroyer is mandatory and independent in role. Its job is not to produce a balanced-sounding paragraph; it actively searches for the strongest reason the current thesis could be wrong.

It must identify the strongest rival hypothesis, strongest contradictory fact, same-root double-count risks, missing-driver candidates, market-signature contradictions, regime-mapping failure, post-hoc rationalization risk, weak source dependencies and evidence that would falsify the thesis.

The red team may not invent counterarguments unsupported by the evidence universe. Its purpose is adversarial testing, not reflexive pessimism. The final adjudicator records whether the thesis survives, survives constrained, is contested, rejected or lacks sufficient evidence.
