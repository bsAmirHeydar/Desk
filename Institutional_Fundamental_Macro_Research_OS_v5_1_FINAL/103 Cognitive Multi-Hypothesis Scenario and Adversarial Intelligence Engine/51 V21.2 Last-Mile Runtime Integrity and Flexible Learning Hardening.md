# V21.2 — Last-Mile Runtime Integrity and Flexible Learning Hardening

V21.2 does **not** add a new market-science layer. It closes runtime gaps that could let a cognitively rich analysis become semantically weak, unmonitorable, or unlearnable.

## Core law

> Flexibility comes from explicit states, scoped exceptions, and conditional branches — not from silently skipping decision-material reasoning.

### What is hardened
- context-sensitive applicability: quiet/event/single-market differences remain flexible, while directional runs cannot silently mark regime/causal graph as irrelevant;
- upstream→downstream propagation invariants, with explicit evidence-backed exceptions instead of rigid universal vetoes;
- actual D1 evidence-pack resolution for load-bearing `FACT_REF`s when strict production validation is requested;
- structured triggers remain structured through Research Intent and final cognitive adjudication;
- FX policy reaction can be bilateral without forcing bilateral objects on non-FX markets;
- regime state is horizon-scoped without creating eight separate regime models;
- hypothesis root counts and critical contradictions can be derived/cross-checked rather than merely self-declared;
- D4 calibration output now conforms to its canonical schema;
- calibrated scenario probabilities must resolve to a D4 calibration record in strict production validation;
- cognitive telemetry captures compact summaries of regime, graph, reflexivity, consumption, driver transition, global reconciliation, source grades, and interaction IDs.

## What is deliberately *not* changed
- Fundamental-only direction authority;
- D3 non-voting causal modulation;
- D4 promotion governance;
- Portfolio gate remains shadow until forward evidence justifies enforcement;
- no new mega-score, no fake probability, no new science module.

## Flexibility contract
A material invariant may be explicitly excepted only with a named invariant, reason, and optional evidence refs. Exceptions are telemetry-visible and therefore forward-testable. This prevents both brittle rules and invisible bypasses.
