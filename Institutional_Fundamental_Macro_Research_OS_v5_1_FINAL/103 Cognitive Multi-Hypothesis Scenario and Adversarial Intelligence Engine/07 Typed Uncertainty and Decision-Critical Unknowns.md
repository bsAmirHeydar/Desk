# Typed Uncertainty and Decision-Critical Unknowns

V21 rejects a single opaque confidence number as the primary representation of uncertainty. It separates uncertainty into Data, Measurement, Source, Causal, Model, Timing, Regime, Narrative, Transmission, Execution and Calibration uncertainty.

The type determines the response. Missing decision-critical data can block. Causal uncertainty creates a contested hypothesis state. Model uncertainty raises unmodeled-driver risk. Timing uncertainty can delay or shorten validity. Regime uncertainty forces scenario splitting instead of a universal mapping. Execution uncertainty may block trading without invalidating the research thesis.

An uncertainty can be high but irrelevant to the current decision; materiality therefore remains essential. Conversely, one decision-critical uncertainty can dominate several low uncertainties. Do not average them.
