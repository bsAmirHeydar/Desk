# Utility-Aware Adjudication and Opportunity Cost

The brain is not optimized for direction accuracy or win rate alone. Trading decisions are asymmetric: a false permission, a missed +5R opportunity, an unnecessary delay and a small avoided loss have different economic consequences.

V21 therefore emits a qualitative decision-utility vector covering upside potential, downside severity, missed-opportunity cost, false-permission cost, delay cost, path asymmetry, gap risk, reversibility, urgency and capital optionality.

Until D4 calibrates a numerical utility estimand, the output remains qualitative. Utility can constrain or delay an existing permission; it cannot create Direction. D4 later tests whether these constraints actually improve paired policy outcomes rather than merely sounding prudent.
