# Regime Vector and State-Dependent Causal Mapping

A single regime label is too coarse. V21 uses a multi-dimensional regime vector covering growth, inflation, policy, fiscal supply, earnings, credit, volatility, funding liquidity, market liquidity, positioning, market structure and geopolitics.

Regimes can overlap. `Growth benign + inflation easing + funding stressed` is a legitimate mixed state. Mapping from facts to asset effects is conditioned on the relevant dimensions rather than a universal sign table.

Regime uncertainty is itself material. When the mapping is unstable or a structural break is plausible, the system widens the scenario set and lowers reliance on historical analogues. Repeated failures of expected causal leaders reopen the regime and hypothesis tournament instead of being explained away.
