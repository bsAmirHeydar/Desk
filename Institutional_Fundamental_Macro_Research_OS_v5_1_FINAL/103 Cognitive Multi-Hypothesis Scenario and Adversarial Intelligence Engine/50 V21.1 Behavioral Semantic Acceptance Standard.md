# V21.1 Behavioral Semantic Acceptance Standard

A cognitive pack is not certified merely because JSON parses. The acceptance suite must deliberately inject contradictions and confirm fail-closed behavior for: instrument/cutoff/horizon mismatch; invalid hypothesis/scenario references; impossible tournament states; duplicate uncertainty dimensions; Direction mismatch at the active Module 89 horizon; missing applicability records; untraceable load-bearing evidence; unmonitorable material triggers; invalid regime vocabulary; broken causal-node references; scenario permission/direction contradictions; calibrated probabilities without a calibration record; and stale review triggers.

The goal is **semantic correctness with minimum necessary complexity**, not maximal rule count.
