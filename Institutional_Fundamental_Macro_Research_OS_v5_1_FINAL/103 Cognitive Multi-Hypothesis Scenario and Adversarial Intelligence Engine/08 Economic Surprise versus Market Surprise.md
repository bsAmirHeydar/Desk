# Economic Surprise versus Market Surprise

`Actual - consensus` is not the full surprise. V21 distinguishes **economic surprise** from **market surprise**.

Economic surprise asks how the released fact differs from an official or survey expectation. Market surprise asks how the observation differs from what was already embedded in prices, positioning, options, alternative expectations and the current policy reaction function.

A CPI print can be below the survey median but above the market-implied expectation. A weak NFP headline can be offset by wages, revisions or participation. A policy statement can be unchanged in words but surprising because the market expected an easing signal.

The expectation stack therefore records official consensus, survey dispersion, market-implied pricing, whisper/alternative expectations, positioning expectations, option-implied event distributions, prior trend and revision expectations when available. If the point-in-time expectation baseline cannot be recovered, the system must use an unrecoverable/undetermined state rather than fabricate precision.
