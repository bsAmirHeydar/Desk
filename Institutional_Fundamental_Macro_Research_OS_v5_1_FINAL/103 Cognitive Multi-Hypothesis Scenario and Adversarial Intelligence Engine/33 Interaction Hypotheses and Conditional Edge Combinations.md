# Interaction Hypotheses and Conditional Edge Combinations

Some states have little value alone but become powerful together. Crowding may matter only with opposing flow and weak capacity; a positive shock may matter more with crowded shorts and short-gamma dealer exposure.

V21 permits interaction hypotheses but controls research degrees of freedom. Interactions must be predeclared, causally justified, assigned to a hypothesis family and normally limited to order three until evidence supports additional complexity.

They remain shadow until D4 validates them with dependence-aware sampling, holdout evidence and multiple-testing disclosure. The brain must not search thousands of combinations after seeing outcomes and then promote the winner.
