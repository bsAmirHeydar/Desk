# Reflexivity and Endogenous Amplification Loops

The market is not a one-way causal chain. Price, financing conditions, positioning and beliefs can feed back into the real or financial state.

Each material reflexive loop identifies the initiating fact, belief transition, behavior change, price/funding consequence, real-state or balance-sheet consequence, alternative explanation and break condition. The loop phase is emerging, self-reinforcing, saturating, exhausting, broken or negative-feedback.

Price cannot be both the only evidence and the claimed cause. At least one non-price observation or a predeclared causal sequence is required. Reflexivity can strengthen persistence while simultaneously worsening crowding and future asymmetry; both sides must remain visible.
