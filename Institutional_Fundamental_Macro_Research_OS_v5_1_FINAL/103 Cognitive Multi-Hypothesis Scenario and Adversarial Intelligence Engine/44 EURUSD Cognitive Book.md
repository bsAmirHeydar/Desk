# EURUSD Cognitive Book

EURUSD is bilateral. Every Fundamental hypothesis must compare the EUR and USD blocks: ECB versus Fed reaction functions, growth/inflation differentials, terms of trade, fiscal/political risk, funding/basis and hedging.

A Dollar move alone is not the EURUSD thesis. Fixings and forced hedging may produce an outside-strategy mechanical path. The scenario tree should retain intervention/policy-communication and relative-rate alternatives when the expected rate leaders diverge.
