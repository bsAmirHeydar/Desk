# EURUSD Cognitive Book

EURUSD is bilateral. Every Fundamental hypothesis must compare the EUR and USD blocks: ECB versus Fed reaction functions, growth/inflation differentials, terms of trade, fiscal/political risk, funding/basis and hedging.

A Dollar move alone is not the EURUSD thesis. Fixings and forced hedging may produce an outside-strategy mechanical path. The scenario tree should retain intervention/policy-communication and relative-rate alternatives when the expected rate leaders diverge.

## V21.1 high-value discriminators
- Direction is bilateral: ECB reaction function, Fed reaction function and growth/inflation differentials must be compared at the same horizon.
- DXY is partially endogenous to EURUSD and cannot count as an independent confirmation by default.
- Separate policy-differential, growth-differential, funding/basis, fixing/hedging and intervention/communication scenarios.
- If spot moves without corresponding rate-differential or funding signatures, raise unmodeled-driver risk rather than inventing a narrative.
