# Minimum Sufficient Causal Explanation and Complexity Penalty

A larger model is not automatically a better model. V21 prefers the smallest causal explanation that preserves the decision-relevant facts and rival risks.

Load-bearing roots are separated from non-load-bearing context. Additional complexity is justified only when it explains an otherwise unresolved observation, changes a scenario, changes a risk constraint, or improves a measurable forward outcome.

`OVERCOMPLEX` does not automatically mean `NO_TRADE`, but it triggers simplification and a search for hidden redundancy. Complexity must never be used to disguise contradiction or make an unfalsifiable thesis look sophisticated.
