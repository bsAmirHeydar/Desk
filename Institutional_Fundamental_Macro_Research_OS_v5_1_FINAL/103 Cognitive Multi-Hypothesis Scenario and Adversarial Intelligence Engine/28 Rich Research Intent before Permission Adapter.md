# Rich Research Intent before the Permission Adapter

`BUY / SELL / NO_TRADE` is an execution interface, not a sufficient representation of thought. V21 produces a rich Research Intent first.

The intent records strategy family, active horizon, Fundamental direction, edge state, urgency, remaining asymmetry, reversal hazard, fragility, chase policy, validity action, next review trigger, invalidation triggers, cognitive constraints and outside-strategy edges.

Only after this state exists is it compressed into the three-state permission used by downstream execution. This preserves information that is useful for humans, D4 learning and future strategy routing without complicating the EA interface.
