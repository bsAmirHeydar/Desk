# Mission, Scope and Authority Boundary

V21 addresses a specific failure mode: a very knowledgeable system can still be brittle if it locks too early onto one story, averages incompatible horizons, turns uncertainty into a fake confidence number, rationalizes after price moves, or mistakes an outside-strategy mechanical edge for a Fundamental thesis.

Module 103 therefore owns **cognitive orchestration**, not new market facts. It is authorized to organize rival explanations, expose uncertainty, construct conditional scenarios, identify model disagreement, force an adversarial challenge, and constrain or delay an existing permission when the cognitive state is decision-critically unresolved.

It is not authorized to create Direction, flip Direction, invent data, override D1 point-in-time truth, or promote unvalidated rules around D4 governance.

### One-way safety law

If the incoming Fundamental-strategy permission is `NO_TRADE`, V21 cognition may explain what would need to change and may report outside-strategy edges, but it cannot manufacture BUY/SELL. If the incoming permission is BUY/SELL, cognition may maintain, constrain, delay, or block it. The D3/D4 layers remain responsible for their own authority classes.

### Flexibility law

Flexibility means preserving several live possibilities until the evidence resolves them. It does **not** mean becoming vague. Every surviving hypothesis and scenario must have causal mechanics, horizon, expected leaders, expected signatures, confirmation triggers and invalidation triggers.
