# Expectation Distribution, Positioning and Event Pricing

Consensus is a distribution, not just a median. Wide dispersion means the same numerical miss can be less informative than a small miss against a tightly clustered expectation. Positioning also changes the payoff: a bullish surprise into already extreme long exposure can produce a weaker or even reversed path without making the fundamental surprise bearish.

When available, event options and implied distributions provide a separate view of what magnitude the market expects, but they are not perfect probability forecasts. The system records them as expectation evidence and keeps source, horizon, liquidity and model limitations.

Event analysis should always ask three separate questions: what changed economically, what changed relative to market expectation, and how much of that change was already owned or hedged.
