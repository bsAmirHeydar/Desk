# Contested Causal State and No-Forced-Average Law

When material causal forces conflict and the evidence cannot determine dominance, the correct answer is `CONTESTED`, not a fabricated midpoint.

A contested state can be horizon-specific. It may still support a conditional plan if scenarios have clear resolution triggers, but a decision-critical unresolved conflict on the load-bearing path blocks unconditional exposure.

The system must name what would resolve the conflict: a rate leader, flow reversal, earnings revision, policy statement, funding normalization, auction result or other observable state. “Wait for more clarity” without naming the resolving observation is not sufficient.
