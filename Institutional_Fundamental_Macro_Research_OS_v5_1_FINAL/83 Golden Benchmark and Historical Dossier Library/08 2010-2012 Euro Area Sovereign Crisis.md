## Executed internal reference answer

The euro-area crisis reference answer maps the sovereign-bank loop within a monetary union lacking a full fiscal union and national exchange-rate adjustment. Domestic banks held sovereign debt, sovereigns backstopped banks, and redenomination risk changed collateral and deposit behavior. Fiscal positions mattered, but current-account imbalances, competitiveness, bank funding and institutional response were also central. The OMT announcement changed tail-risk pricing through a conditional backstop.

- **Rival model:** preserve at least one mechanism that can explain the same observed move.

### Consolidated legacy note: 02 Euro Area Institutional Macro Book

> [!abstract] Country mandate
> Analyze **02 Euro Area Institutional Macro Book** through its own institutions and balance sheets rather than by importing a U.S. template.

The defining structure is a **monetary union with shared policy, national fiscal authorities, bank-based transmission, fragmentation risk, and heterogeneous inflation/growth**.

- households: income, saving, housing, debt, duration, and currency;
- firms: margins, investment, external debt, trade exposure, and refinancing;
- banks/NBFI: funding, asset quality, capital, liquidity, and foreign claims;
- government: primary balance, debt maturity, currency, investor base, and contingent liabilities;
- central bank: mandate, operating framework, reserves, intervention, and financial-stability role;
- rest of world: current account, portfolio/direct flows, external assets/liabilities, and hedging.

Build point-in-time distributions for growth, inflation, labor/income, credit, housing, fiscal impulse, policy path, external financing, and political implementation. Separate level from derivative and cyclical weakness from structural capacity.

| Market | Required decomposition |

| Local rates | policy path, inflation compensation, term premium, fiscal supply, investor base, liquidity |
| FX | relative policy, external funding, valuation, terms of trade, intervention, hedging and global beta |
| Equities | domestic/foreign revenue, margins, financial conditions, sector concentration, governance and flows |
| Sovereign credit | debt dynamics, currency mismatch, reserves, politics, liquidity and recovery |
| Banks | deposit/funding, duration, credit quality, regulation and sovereign linkage |
| Commodities | production, fiscal linkage, terms of trade, hedging and infrastructure |

- **Structural:** institutions, demographics, productive capacity, currency regime, energy/resource position.
- **Cyclical:** domestic demand, inflation, credit, earnings, fiscal and monetary conditions.
- **Tactical/swing:** policy meetings, issuance, politics, data surprises, intervention and global capital flow.
- **Daily/event:** local curve, FX, bank equities, sovereign spreads and relevant commodity markets lead.

A long/short view requires a relative counterfactual, because currency and cross-country trades price differences.
