## 1. Learning Mode

Read in this order:

1. 02 Core Doctrine
2. 01 State Expectations Price
3. 03 Discount Rate Cash Flow and Risk Premium
4. 00 Macro State MOC
5. 00 Market Pricing MOC
6. The playbook for your primary instrument
7. 01 Fundamental Bias Engine
8. 03 Daily Pre-Market Process

Do not memorize isolated correlations. Learn causal chains.

Bad learning:

> CPI up → yields up → Nasdaq down.

Better learning:

> CPI exceeded the priced distribution, the details implied persistence, the front end repriced the expected policy path higher, real yields rose, the dollar strengthened, and long-duration equities were de-rated. The causal chain was coherent.

Best learning:

> The same CPI surprise may produce a different equity response when growth is collapsing, positioning is defensive, the central bank is constrained by financial instability, or the surprise is driven by a one-off component. Correlations are conditional.

## Note Discipline

Every market statement should be classified as one of:

- **Fact** — directly observed.
- **Estimate** — model-derived or inferred.
- **Expectation** — market- or survey-implied.
- **Interpretation** — causal explanation.
- **Scenario** — conditional future path.
- **Decision** — action or permission.
- **Invalidation** — evidence that would force revision.

Never store an interpretation as if it were a fact.
