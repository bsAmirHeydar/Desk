## Relative Macro for FX

For each currency pair, compare:

- policy-rate path;
- inflation-adjusted rate differential;
- growth momentum;
- external balance;
- terms of trade;
- fiscal/sovereign risk;
- funding/safe-haven role;
- positioning.

Absolute data in one country is insufficient.

## Global Synchronization

Track whether growth, inflation, and policy cycles are:

- synchronized;
- desynchronized;
- converging;
- diverging.

Global synchronization changes:

- dollar behavior;
- commodity demand;
- cross-border capital flows;
- equity leadership;
- relative-value FX opportunities.
