## Operating principle

The prompt text is English to maximize instruction precision. The final report is Persian, right-to-left, visually consistent and intentionally simple in its main conclusions. Institutional detail, citations, the Vault Reading Ledger and methodological limitations belong in appendices rather than cluttering the executive pages.

## Mandatory boundary

These prompts are fundamental-only. They may use price and market data to measure valuation, market-implied expectations, volatility, liquidity, basis, positioning proxies and realized response. They may not use chart patterns, indicators or non-fundamental entry systems.

## Daily operating mode

The four asset prompts support daily pre-market, live-session, post-event and end-of-day profiles. Daily reports prioritize the current trading day and use the structural canon only as an inherited constraint.


## Hourly operating mode

The four asset prompts support fixed hourly snapshots plus event-driven updates. Hourly outputs separate direction, intensity, confidence, consumption, remaining pressure, persistence, reversal risk and no-material-update states.
