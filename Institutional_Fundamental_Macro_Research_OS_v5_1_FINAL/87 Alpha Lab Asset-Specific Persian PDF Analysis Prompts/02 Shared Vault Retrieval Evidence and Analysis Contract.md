# Shared Vault Retrieval Evidence and Analysis Contract

## Mandatory Vault use

The attached Obsidian Vault is the governing research system. An answer based only on memory or web research is invalid.

Before external research:

1. Open `00 HOME.md` and `01 COVERAGE MATRIX.md`.
2. Read [[82 Canonical Institutional Fundamental Research Library/00 Canonical Institutional Fundamental Research Library MOC]].
3. Read [[84 Canonical Retrieval Evidence and Version Control/02 Staged Retrieval and Context-Budget Protocol]].
4. Read [[81 Scientific QA and Certification Framework/23 Probability and Scenario Weight Taxonomy]].
5. Select one direct primary canonical monograph and the smallest causal dependency set required for the market.
6. Check [[85 Specialist Canonical Research Appendices/00 Specialist Canonical Research Appendices MOC]] for a narrower legal, sector, instrument or physical-market dependency.
7. Use legacy notes only to answer a specific residual question that the canonical notes do not resolve.
8. Record every material Vault note in a Reading Ledger and explain its causal role.

## Live mode

- Use current web research and the latest available official, exchange, regulatory, issuer or recognized institutional sources.
- Timestamp all market-sensitive facts.
- Identify stale, delayed, estimated, model-implied or unavailable data.
- Do not claim access to licensed consensus, dealer inventory, prime-broker flow or proprietary positioning unless it was genuinely available.

## Historical mode

- Lock an exact timestamp and timezone before research.
- Use only information published and available by the cutoff.
- Use first-release or contemporaneous vintages where relevant.
- Exclude later revisions, outcomes, subsequent policy information and future constituent changes from the reconstructed section.
- Place any ex-post audit in a visibly separate appendix.

## Claim taxonomy

Separate:

- observed fact;
- published estimate;
- derived measurement;
- market-implied estimate;
- model output;
- causal inference;
- scenario;
- judgment;
- unknown.

## Probability taxonomy

Every probability or weight must be labeled as one of:

- empirically calibrated probability;
- model-implied probability;
- judgmental scenario weight.

Do not present judgmental weights as calibrated probabilities.

## Evidence standard

Every load-bearing claim should identify:

- source organization;
- document, table, series, filing or release;
- publication timestamp;
- reference period;
- exact locator when available;
- transformation and units;
- interpretation and limitation;
- contradictory evidence when material.

## Fundamental-only boundary

Do not use chart patterns, technical indicators, candlestick formations or non-fundamental entry rules. Price may be used only to infer valuation, expectations, volatility, liquidity, basis, market response, positioning proxies, financing conditions and realized outcomes.

## Internal QA boundary

The report may state:

- `INTERNAL QA — FULL`
- `INTERNAL QA — CONDITIONAL`
- `INTERNAL QA — FAILED`

It may not claim independent external scientific certification unless an actual independent review exists.
