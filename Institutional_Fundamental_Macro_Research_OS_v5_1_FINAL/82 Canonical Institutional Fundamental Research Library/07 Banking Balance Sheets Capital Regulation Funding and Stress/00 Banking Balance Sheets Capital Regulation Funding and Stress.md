- **Tactical:** expectations, valuation, issuance, positioning and policy repricing over weeks.

A lower-horizon shock can change timing, distribution and confidence without automatically reversing the structural state. The conclusion must be reported separately by horizon.

## 11. Falsification, uncertainty and failure modes

- Reject conclusions that depend on an unidentified residual being treated as a structural factor.
- Cap confidence when measurement, sampling, revision or liquidity uncertainty is material.
- Separate absence of evidence from evidence of absence.
- Record the strongest contrary official or market evidence.
- State the expected sign and timing of leading indicators before observing the target outcome.
- Distinguish a wrong state estimate, wrong priced baseline, wrong causal model, wrong horizon and exogenous residual.

## 15. Deep financial-institution and credit interpretation

Financial institutions transform maturity, liquidity, credit, collateral and risk. Their behavior is constrained not only by economic capital but by accounting classification, regulatory ratios, liquidity rules, margin, ratings, covenants, investor mandates and access to secured and unsecured funding. Analysis therefore starts with a legal entity and consolidated perimeter, then reconciles assets, liabilities, off-balance-sheet commitments, hedges and contingent liquidity needs.

Bank solvency and bank liquidity are separate. Mark-to-market losses can be economically important before they are recognized in regulatory capital; stable deposits can become runnable when concentration, digital access and uninsured balances change behavior; collateral that is high quality can still generate loss when monetized under time pressure. CET1, leverage, LCR, NSFR, IRRBB, TLAC/MREL, deposit insurance and resolution regimes affect the adjustment path, but jurisdictional implementation matters more than a generic Basel label.

Credit analysis decomposes default probability, exposure at default, loss given default, liquidity premium, optionality and recovery timing. A spread can widen because expected loss rises, because risk-bearing capacity falls, because hedging demand increases or because cash bonds cheapen to derivatives. Private credit and structured finance add opacity, appraisal lag, payment-in-kind flexibility, covenant design, warehouse funding and tranche waterfalls.

NBFI analysis follows leverage and liquidity across money funds, open-end funds, hedge funds, insurers, pensions, securitization vehicles and finance companies. The central question is where margin or redemption pressure creates a forced seller and whether dealers, banks or official facilities can intermediate the flow. The same asset can be sound in hold-to-maturity value yet systemically dangerous when financed with runnable or collateral-sensitive liabilities.
