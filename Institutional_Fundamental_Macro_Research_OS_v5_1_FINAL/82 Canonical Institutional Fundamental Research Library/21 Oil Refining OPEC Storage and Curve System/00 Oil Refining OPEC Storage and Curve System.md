A lower-horizon shock can change timing, distribution and confidence without automatically reversing the structural state. The conclusion must be reported separately by horizon.

## 11. Falsification, uncertainty and failure modes

- Reject conclusions that depend on an unidentified residual being treated as a structural factor.
- Cap confidence when measurement, sampling, revision or liquidity uncertainty is material.
- Separate absence of evidence from evidence of absence.
- Record the strongest contrary official or market evidence.
- State the expected sign and timing of leading indicators before observing the target outcome.
- Distinguish a wrong state estimate, wrong priced baseline, wrong causal model, wrong horizon and exogenous residual.

## 15. Deep physical-market and commodity interpretation

A commodity is a time-and-location-specific claim on a physical system. National supply-demand totals are insufficient when quality, transport, processing, storage and deliverability bind. Analysis reconciles production, imports, exports, transformation, consumption, losses and inventory by region and specification, then maps the balance into cash differentials, calendar spreads, freight, utilization and marginal economics.

Inventories must be normalized for seasonality, working-stock requirements, capacity, ownership and accessibility. A low aggregate stock is not necessarily scarce if it is in the right location and quality; a seemingly adequate stock can be unavailable behind infrastructure bottlenecks. Forward curves reflect financing, storage, convenience yield, optionality and market power, not merely forecasts of future spot prices.

Supply responds with lags governed by geology, decline rates, crop cycles, project permitting, service capacity and producer balance sheets. Demand substitution depends on equipment, technology, policy and relative prices. For power and gas, weather ensembles, forced outages, pipeline constraints, merit order, heat rates, reserve margins and nodal congestion create nonlinear scarcity. For agriculture, planted area, yield, stocks-to-use, basis, storage and export logistics dominate.

Financial positioning can amplify price but cannot prove a physical deficit. The report must identify which physical observation would confirm the thesis and which curve or basis move is a consequence rather than independent evidence.
