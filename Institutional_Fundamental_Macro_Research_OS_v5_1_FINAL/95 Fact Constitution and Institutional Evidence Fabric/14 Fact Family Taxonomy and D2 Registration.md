# Ten registered fact families

D1 registers ten families so every production run can declare coverage consistently:

1. `MACRO_FUNDAMENTAL` — growth, inflation, labor, policy, fiscal and structural facts used by V11/V13.
2. `MARKET_IMPLIED` — rates, curves, spreads, implied distributions and other reproducible market-implied states.
3. `CORPORATE_EARNINGS_VALUATION` — issuer/index earnings, revisions, valuation and constituent fundamentals where applicable.
4. `NARRATIVE_ATTENTION` — factual attention inputs plus clearly separated V12 narrative inferences.
5. `TEMPORAL_CALENDAR` — event calendars, benchmark clocks, settlement/fixing and known timing facts used by V15.1.
6. `POSITIONING_OWNERSHIP` — registered now; dedicated science is D2-pending.
7. `ACTUAL_FLOW` — registered now; dedicated science is D2-pending.
8. `FUNDING_PLUMBING` — registered now; dedicated science is D2-pending.
9. `INSTITUTIONAL_MECHANICS` — registered now; dedicated science is D2-pending.
10. `MARKET_CAPACITY` — registered now; dedicated science is D2-pending.

Registration is not promotion. D2-pending families may contain useful existing facts, but D1 does not create a new decision authority or hidden-data inference engine for them.

## V18 transition

As of V18, Positioning/Ownership, Actual Flow, Funding/Plumbing, Institutional Mechanics and Market Capacity are no longer definition-pending. Their science authorities are Modules 96–99 and their six-market router is Module 100. They remain shadow-only for permission.
