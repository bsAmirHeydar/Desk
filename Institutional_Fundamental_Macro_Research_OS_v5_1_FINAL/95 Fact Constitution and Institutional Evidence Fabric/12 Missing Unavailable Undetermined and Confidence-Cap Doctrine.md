# Missingness is information

D1 distinguishes:

- `UNAVAILABLE`: the target is known not to be accessible/supplied for the required scope;
- `UNDETERMINED`: the system cannot establish the state;
- absent record: no attempt/result is recorded and therefore cannot be interpreted safely.

## Routing

A decision-critical unavailable/undetermined item capable of flipping the decision produces `HOLD` unless a canonical substitute route explicitly resolves the same question. Material missingness caps confidence or shortens validity. Supporting/contextual missingness is disclosed.

## No hidden imputation

Never replace unknown institutional flow, dealer inventory or unpublished participant data with a public proxy without labeling the proxy and its limits. D1 coverage receipts must reveal these substitutions.
