# V17 D1 — Truth & Evidence Foundation

D1 is the epistemic substrate of Alpha Lab. It does **not** add a second directional brain, a new trading signal, or a claim that all institutional data are public. It defines what may enter the system as evidence, what must remain inference, how every claim is time-bounded, and how a decision can be reconstructed later without look-ahead.

## Mandatory architecture

`Source / Snapshot → Fact Class → Temporal Visibility → Revision Vintage → Claim Lineage → Root Cause → Decision Materiality → Decision Evidence Pack → V16.1 Scientific Stack → Fact Coverage Receipt → Final Permission`

The chain is fail-closed only where the unresolved issue is capable of changing a live decision. Secondary or contextual uncertainty is disclosed and capped rather than being turned into an indiscriminate veto.

## Canonical authorities introduced by D1

- **Fact constitution:** what counts as observed, reported, implied, proxy, derived, inferred, assumed, unavailable or undetermined.
- **Temporal visibility:** which version of a fact was actually knowable at an analysis cutoff.
- **Vintage governance:** first releases and revisions are distinct immutable versions.
- **Source eligibility:** provenance determines whether a claim can be load-bearing.
- **Claim lineage:** derived facts and interpretations must expose their parents and method.
- **Root-cause dependency:** multiple descendants of one event are not independent votes.
- **Materiality routing:** missing evidence is handled according to its marginal ability to alter permission, Edge, validity or confidence.
- **Coverage honesty:** every production run declares what was retrieved, what was unavailable and what remains outside D1 decision authority.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

1. Direction remains `FUNDAMENTAL_ONLY`.
2. Narrative remains transmission clearance, never a substitute for factual direction.
3. Timing remains temporal clearance.
4. Donchian/ATR remain external execution mechanics only.
5. Positioning, Actual Flow, Funding/Plumbing, Institutional Mechanics and Market Capacity are registered for D2 but are not promoted to new decision authority by D1.
6. A public proxy may never be described as proprietary or direct institutional flow.
7. Historical replay may never use a revision, source or timestamp that was not demonstrably visible by the requested cutoff.
