# Residual limitations

D1 certifies an evidence constitution, not universal data acquisition or trading profitability.

## Deliberate remaining frontiers

- participant-level positioning is not uniformly public or contemporaneous;
- many “flow” datasets are estimated rather than identified actual flow;
- dealer balance-sheet, funding, collateral and internal risk data can require licensed/proprietary sources;
- options dealer exposures often depend on model assumptions and incomplete counterparty information;
- benchmark/index mechanics require venue/administrator-specific live schedules;
- market capacity is state-dependent and cannot be reduced to one static liquidity number;
- source timestamps can be revised, delayed or ambiguously archived;
- true independent model validation requires a genuinely independent model/analyst, not merely a second prompt pass.

These are inputs to D2 and D4, not reasons to blur evidence classes in D1.
