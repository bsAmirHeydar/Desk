# Institutional Fundamental Macro Research OS v6.1 — Knowledge Canon Correction

This patch corrects the interpretation of the transformation program.

## The objective

The objective is not to build software, data infrastructure or coded models. The objective is to extract, write, organize and audit the complete body of fundamental and macro-financial knowledge at institutional depth.

## What changes

- Replaces the previous implementation-oriented Module 76 with a 57-stage knowledge-completion program.
- Removes the production data-platform, production asset-engine and operational accreditation modules from the Start Here path and deletes those v6 folders when present.
- Adds a knowledge-only Ten-of-Ten scorecard.
- Adds a seven-wave scholarly release and dependency plan.
- Keeps the Vault fundamental-only and excludes technical analysis.

## What 10/10 means

Ten-of-ten is defined as complete knowledge coverage and depth: beginner explanation, institutional definition, formal treatment, competing theories, measurement, history, cross-system transmission, multihorizon interpretation, failure modes, sources and pedagogy.
