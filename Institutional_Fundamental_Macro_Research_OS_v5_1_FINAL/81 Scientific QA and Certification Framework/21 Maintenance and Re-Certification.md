# Maintenance and Re-Certification

## Re-certification triggers

- new statistical manuals or major methodology revisions;
- new regulation or market structure;
- benchmark index or contract redesign;
- major accounting-standard changes;
- new central-bank operating framework;
- structural changes in energy, technology or financial intermediation;
- repeated benchmark failure;
- discovered citation or factual error;
- model drift or regime break.

## Review frequencies

- current-data source routes: monthly or after provider changes;
- policy and market-structure notes: after material decisions;
- domain monographs: annual review;
- historical cases: after major new evidence;
- prompt and certification gates: quarterly regression test;
- full adversarial audit: annual.

## Change record

Every material revision records the changed claim, old and new source, reason, affected analyses and whether prior reports require correction.

## No permanent certification

Certification applies to a version, date and scope. A certified analysis expires when its data cutoff, causal assumptions or market structure are no longer current.
