# Probability and Scenario Weight Taxonomy

Every probability-like number must be labelled as one of three objects:

1. **Empirically calibrated probability** — derived from a declared reference class or repeated out-of-sample forecasts with calibration statistics.
2. **Model-implied probability** — generated by a named model under stated assumptions; model uncertainty is separate.
3. **Judgmental scenario weight** — an analyst allocation used for decision comparison, not a frequency claim.

Mixing the three objects is an integrity failure. Reports must state the basis, sample or model, update rule and confidence ceiling. When no defensible numeric basis exists, use ordered qualitative weights rather than fabricated precision.
