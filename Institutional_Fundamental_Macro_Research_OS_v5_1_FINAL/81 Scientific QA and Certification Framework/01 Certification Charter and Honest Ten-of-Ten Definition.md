# Certification Charter and Honest Ten-of-Ten Definition

## What ten-of-ten means

Ten-of-ten is a property of a completed analysis process, not a permanent claim that the Vault contains omniscient or final truth. Economic systems evolve, data are revised, institutions change and many causal questions remain underidentified. The appropriate institutional claim is therefore:

> The analysis is ten-of-ten when every material dimension of the specified research object has been retrieved, measured, challenged, sourced and reported to the highest defensible standard permitted by available information, while unresolved uncertainty remains visible.

Ten-of-ten never means certainty, perfect forecasting or absence of model error. It means no avoidable research omission, hidden assumption, unsupported claim, timeframe leak or invented evidence survived the process.

## Four distinct certification objects

1. **Vault coverage certification** — the relevant science, models, markets and institutional mechanisms exist in the Vault.
2. **Retrieval certification** — the analyst selected all material domains and did not rely on a single convenient narrative.
3. **Evidence certification** — each load-bearing factual or quantitative claim is traceable to a source, timestamp and transformation.
4. **Report certification** — the final synthesis handles horizons, rival models, scenarios, contradictions and unknowns without false precision.

A Vault can be broadly complete while a particular report fails because current data were unavailable. Conversely, a strong report may use external official evidence to update a stable conceptual framework. Certification must state which object is being judged.

## Non-negotiable principles

- A missing data point is not permission to fabricate a proxy.
- A proxy is not the underlying variable and must be labelled.
- A price move is not automatically evidence of the proposed cause.
- Consensus is not truth; it is a priced or surveyed distribution.
- An explanatory story without a falsifier is not a research conclusion.
- A historical reconstruction that uses later revisions is contaminated.
- A probability without a reference class, model or calibration basis is a judgmental weight, not an empirical frequency.
- A long report can still be incomplete; a concise report can be certified if the object is narrow and fully covered.

## Certification labels

- **INTERNAL QA — FULL**: all mandatory gates pass and no material unresolved veto remains.
- **INTERNAL QA — CONDITIONAL**: the process is complete but one or more explicit data limitations cap confidence or narrow the conclusion.
- **QA REJECTED — INCOMPLETE EVIDENCE**: material data, source lineage or horizon coverage is missing.
- **QA REJECTED — MODEL CONFLICT**: serious rival models remain unresolved and the conclusion is too strong.
- **QA REJECTED — TIME CONTAMINATION**: historical cutoff or vintage integrity failed.
- **QA REJECTED — SCOPE ERROR**: the wrong instrument, country, entity, contract or market structure was analyzed.

The certification verdict belongs at the beginning and end of every institutional report.
