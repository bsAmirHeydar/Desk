# Fundamental-Only Boundary and Permitted Price Uses

## Prohibited methods

The Vault does not use price patterns, chart formations, indicator crossovers, candlestick rules or visually inferred support/resistance as fundamental evidence.

## Permitted price uses

Price and market data are admissible when they measure:

- market-implied expectations;
- valuation and risk premium;
- yield curves, forward curves and basis;
- volatility and risk-neutral distributions;
- liquidity, depth, spread and transaction cost;
- positioning proxies and forced-flow effects;
- event response and causal lead-lag evidence;
- realized outcomes for attribution and calibration.

## Price is evidence, not truth

A market move can reflect new information, risk premium, hedging, funding pressure, forced liquidation or noise. The report must distinguish these possibilities.

## No execution leakage

The analysis may discuss economic payoff, horizon, liquidity, implementation constraints and exposure. It must not transform into chart-based entry instructions or claim that a price formation proves the fundamental conclusion.
