# Weighted Review Rubric and Inter-Rater Protocol

## Weighted score

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Object, cutoff and instrument integrity | 10 |
| Retrieval coverage and canonical-source use | 10 |
| Claim-level evidence and source freshness | 20 |
| Measurement and revision integrity | 10 |
| Causal architecture and rival models | 15 |
| Pricing, valuation and payoff translation | 10 |
| Multihorizon and cross-asset synthesis | 10 |
| Scenarios, tails, unknowns and falsifiers | 10 |
| Report clarity, update rules and auditability | 5 |

- **90–100:** INTERNAL QA — FULL, provided no integrity veto exists.
- **75–89:** INTERNAL QA — CONDITIONAL.
- **Below 75:** QA REJECTED.

## Reviewer protocol

Two reviewers score independently. A difference greater than eight total points or greater than three points in any 20-point dimension requires adjudication. Reviewers cite the paragraph, claim or omitted domain responsible for each deduction. Inter-rater agreement is reported; the rubric is not treated as a validated psychometric instrument until repeated use establishes reliability.
