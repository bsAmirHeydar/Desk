# Residual Frontiers and Research Agenda

## Decision purpose

Publish what V11 cannot honestly solve yet, preventing architecture completeness from being misrepresented as empirical certainty or market access.

## Governing distinctions

- Unknown is a valid state.
- Licensed data can improve observability but does not guarantee causality.
- Calibration is asset/event/horizon/regime specific.
- Structural breaks can invalidate learned mappings.
- Execution outcomes remain outside the fundamental-only engine.

## Operating method

1. Maintain a prioritized frontier register.
2. For each frontier state the missing evidence, acquisition path, cost/licence, expected value and validation design.
3. Separate architecture-complete, data-limited and empirically-unvalidated objects.
4. Retire frontiers when evidence and validation gates pass.
5. Reopen completed items after structural breaks.

## Required outputs

- `frontier_id`
- `object`
- `current_limit`
- `required_data`
- `public_proxy`
- `validation_design`
- `priority`
- `status`

## Failure modes and controls

- **Failure:** Calling a research agenda a defect-free system  
  **Control:** Certification remains conditional.
- **Failure:** Buying data without incremental-value test  
  **Control:** Require ablation.
- **Failure:** Permanent model status  
  **Control:** Monitor drift and structural breaks.

## Priority frontiers

1. Point-in-time consensus archives for intraday event reconstruction.
2. Historical policy-curve snapshots and options distributions at exact cutoffs.
3. Reliable dealer/CTA/real-money flow observability and incremental-value tests.
4. Event-family counterfactual repricing envelopes by regime.
5. Inter-rater calibration on frozen information packets.
6. State-survival and reinforcement labels without leakage.
7. Structural-break detection and model retirement.
8. Mapping fundamental asymmetry to a separate execution layer without contaminating the fundamental canon.

V11 is internally complete as an analytical architecture only when these limits are exposed. It is not an empirically universal prediction engine.
