# Information Absorption Measurement

## Decision purpose

Estimate how widely and accurately information is known and incorporated into explicit expectations before confusing awareness with completed price adjustment.

## Governing distinctions

- Publication is not universal awareness.
- Awareness is not correct interpretation.
- Consensus awareness can coexist with disagreement about persistence or reaction function.
- A leak or pre-positioned expectation can create high pre-catalyst absorption.

## Operating method

1. Identify earliest verified source time and distribution path.
2. Recover pre-event consensus, forecasts and market-implied state.
3. Assess anticipation in level, composition and probability.
4. Separate awareness from interpretation and expectation incorporation.
5. Update after clarifications, revisions or dissemination changes.

## Required outputs

- `earliest_verified_source_time`
- `awareness_band`
- `interpretation_dispersion`
- `expectation_incorporation`
- `pre_event_leak_risk`
- `timestamp_confidence`

## Failure modes and controls

- **Failure:** Timestamp equals full awareness  
  **Control:** Assess dissemination and market-hours context.
- **Failure:** Consensus mean represents full distribution  
  **Control:** Record dispersion and tails when available.
- **Failure:** Analyst knew it, therefore market knew it  
  **Control:** Use external expectation evidence or mark judgmental.
