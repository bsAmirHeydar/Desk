# Honest Ten-of-Ten Definition and Scope

## Decision purpose

Define maximum methodological maturity without claiming perfect prediction or inaccessible information. Ten-of-ten is earned through explicit ontology, auditable measurement, honest uncertainty, reproducibility, point-in-time validity and regression-safe implementation.

## Governing distinctions

- Knowledge completeness is different from data completeness.
- Internal certification is different from external scientific certification.
- A published unknown is a controlled frontier; an implicit unknown is a defect.
- A method may be 10/10 while a particular live record remains low confidence.

## Operating method

1. Name the decision object, asset and horizon before scoring.
2. Declare whether the run is ordinal, calibrated or hybrid-provenance.
3. Require a field-level evidence ledger and missing-input penalties.
4. Run causal, rival-model, point-in-time, horizon and regression gates.
5. Publish residual frontiers and failed gates.

## Required outputs

- `ten_of_ten_verdict`
- `certification_scope`
- `passed_gates`
- `failed_gates`
- `residual_frontiers`
- `data_mode`

## Failure modes and controls

- **Failure:** Treating a high-quality narrative as calibrated forecasting  
  **Control:** Require an estimand, sample contract and out-of-sample evidence before calibrated language.
- **Failure:** Hiding unknowns to preserve a high score  
  **Control:** Treat explicit unknowns and confidence caps as evidence of maturity.
- **Failure:** Equating a correct market call with a correct model  
  **Control:** Score state estimation, causal attribution and payoff separately.
