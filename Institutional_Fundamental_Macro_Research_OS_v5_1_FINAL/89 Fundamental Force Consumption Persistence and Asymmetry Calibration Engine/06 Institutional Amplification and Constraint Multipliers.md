# Institutional Amplification and Constraint Multipliers

## Decision purpose

Represent institutional constraints as conditional multipliers that can accelerate, suppress, delay or reverse transmission of a fundamental impulse.

## Governing distinctions

- Flow is not automatically fundamental information.
- A mechanical amplifier can extend travel without increasing thesis quality.
- A constraint can dominate tactically while the slow state remains unchanged.
- Direct, licensed, public-proxy and unknown states remain distinct.

## Operating method

1. Identify relevant institutional mechanisms for the asset and session.
2. Determine whether the mechanism is forced, discretionary or inferred.
3. Establish trigger, direction, capacity, timing and saturation.
4. Apply an amplifier or dampener range with provenance.
5. Separate the overlay from force and consumption ledgers.

## Required outputs

- `institutional_mechanism`
- `observability_tier`
- `amplifier_range`
- `mechanical_flow_state`
- `saturation_condition`
- `confidence_penalty`

## Failure modes and controls

- **Failure:** Dealer gamma sign asserted without data  
  **Control:** Use `PUBLIC_PROXY` or `UNAVAILABLE` and cap confidence.
- **Failure:** CTA flow treated as permanent macro signal  
  **Control:** Assign short tactical horizon and exhaustion condition.
- **Failure:** Passive flow double-counted as earnings confirmation  
  **Control:** Keep it in the overlay layer.
