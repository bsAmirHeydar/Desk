# DJIA Force, Consumption and Persistence Book
Use current point-in-time price-weighted constituents/weights. Separate broad US macro force from price-weighted constituent shocks, cyclical/industrial growth, financial/rates, health/regulation and earnings revision channels. Consumption and remaining pressure must be evaluated at both macro-root and constituent-root levels; do not borrow Nasdaq persistence assumptions.
