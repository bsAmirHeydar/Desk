# v6.3 Institutional Analysis Canon

This release corrects the objective of the Knowledge Canon program.

The Vault is designed as an institutional fundamental research intelligence base. Its success criterion is the quality and completeness of analysis it can support across objects, horizons and regimes. Educational artifacts remain available as optional validation aids, but they no longer determine the architecture or score.
