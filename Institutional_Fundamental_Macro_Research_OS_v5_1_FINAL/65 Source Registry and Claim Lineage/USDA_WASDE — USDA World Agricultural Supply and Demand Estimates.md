# USDA WASDE

## Official route

https://www.usda.gov/oce/commodity/wasde

## Institutional use

Primary global agricultural balance-sheet source. Preserve report date, crop year, revisions and country-specific assumptions.

## Required citation fields

- exact document, release, table, filing or series;
- publication timestamp and reference period;
- version, amendment or vintage;
- transformation and units;
- coverage and limitations.

## Misuse warnings

Do not cite the organization homepage for a specific quantitative claim. Do not assume the latest methodology existed at a historical cutoff.
