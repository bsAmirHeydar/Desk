https://www.sec.gov/divisions/investment/13ffaq

## Institutional use
Regulated quarterly institutional investment-manager holdings filings.

## Cadence / latency
Quarterly with filing lag

## Hard boundaries
- 13F is delayed structural ownership, not live positioning or flow.
- Reported long holdings do not reveal all shorts, derivatives, hedges or intra-quarter changes.
