# Cboe — U.S. Options Market Statistics

## Official route
https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/options/market-statistics/daily/

## Access / cadence
PUBLIC_FREE

## Institutional use
Public options volume/open-interest and market statistics for listed options.

## Point-in-time / latency
Daily/EOD for public statistics; intraday participant detail is a different product.

## Hard boundaries
- Volume/OI do not identify dealer inventory.
- GEX/dealer hedging requires explicit assumptions or direct participant data.

## Admission contract
Record publication/retrieval/reference times, exact table/file/product, units, corrections/cancellations where applicable, access class, and whether the observation is direct, aggregate, proxy or model inference. A registered source is not assumed available in a run.
