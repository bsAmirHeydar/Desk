## Historical reconstruction protocol

Freeze the contract, rulebook, filing, dataset and institutional perimeter at the cutoff. Reconstruct what a well-resourced analyst could have observed, which values were delayed or appraised, which legal outcomes remained uncertain, and which market prices embedded optionality rather than a point forecast. Later resolutions, restatements or recoveries belong only in the ex-post audit.

A current report states the live state, priced assumptions, financing and contractual constraints, next disclosures, scenarios, contradictory evidence and unknowns. A historical report freezes the admissible record at the cutoff, then separates the ex-post audit.
