## Analytical perimeter

This specialist monograph closes a gap that was previously embedded in an over-broad industry, country or market note. ## Causal sequences and scenario design

Each chain names the constrained balance sheet, trigger, mediator, leading evidence, threshold and falsifier. Base, alternative and tail scenarios state what changes the contractual cash flow, financing need, recovery, valuation or policy response.

## Historical reconstruction protocol

Freeze the contract, rulebook, filing, dataset and institutional perimeter at the cutoff. Reconstruct what a well-resourced analyst could have observed, which values were delayed or appraised, which legal outcomes remained uncertain, and which market prices embedded optionality rather than a point forecast. Later resolutions, restatements or recoveries belong only in the ex-post audit.

## Current and historical output requirements

A current report states the live state, priced assumptions, financing and contractual constraints, next disclosures, scenarios, contradictory evidence and unknowns. A historical report freezes the admissible record at the cutoff, then separates the ex-post audit. Conclusions are reported by structural, cyclical, tactical, multi-day and event horizons where meaningful.
