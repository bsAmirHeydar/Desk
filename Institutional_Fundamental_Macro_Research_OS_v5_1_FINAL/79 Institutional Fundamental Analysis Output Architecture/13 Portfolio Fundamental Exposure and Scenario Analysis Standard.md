# Portfolio Fundamental Exposure and Scenario Analysis Standard

## Objective

Identify true economic exposures rather than labels or asset names.

## Required decomposition

- growth, inflation, policy, real-rate, dollar, credit, liquidity and commodity factors;
- country, sector, duration, convexity and funding exposures;
- duplicated theses across apparently different positions;
- nonlinear and option-like payoffs;
- liquidity and margin sensitivity;
- conditional correlations under stress;
- event and calendar concentration;
- hedge basis and hedge-failure mechanisms;
- scenario P&L logic and unknown exposures.

## Output

The conclusion states which scenarios dominate loss, which exposures are intentional, which are accidental, which hedges fail together and which observations would indicate a portfolio regime transition.
