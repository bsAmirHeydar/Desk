# Asset and Market Analysis Standard

## Required layers

- legal and economic instrument definition;
- cash-flow or payoff structure;
- valuation anchors;
- dominant macro and idiosyncratic drivers;
- expectations embedded in relevant curves, spreads or prices;
- institutional investor and intermediary structure;
- liquidity, financing, basis and positioning constraints;
- historical regime behavior;
- cross-asset confirmations;
- structural, cyclical, tactical, day-horizon and event conclusions;
- scenario asymmetry and invalidation.

## Asset-specific rule

The analysis cannot stop at “macro favorable” or “macro adverse.” It must explain the exact payoff channel for the instrument, including convexity, carry, optionality, maturity, currency, basis and financing where relevant.
