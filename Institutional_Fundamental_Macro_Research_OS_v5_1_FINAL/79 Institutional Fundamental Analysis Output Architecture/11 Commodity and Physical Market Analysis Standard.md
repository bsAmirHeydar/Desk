# Commodity and Physical Market Analysis Standard

## Required architecture

- specification, quality, geography and deliverability;
- production capacity, utilization and decline dynamics;
- inventories by location and ownership;
- transport, storage, processing and bottlenecks;
- demand by end use and substitution elasticity;
- policy, sanctions, weather and geopolitical constraints;
- term structure, regional basis, processing margins and convenience yield;
- producer, consumer, merchant and financial positioning;
- marginal cost, incentive price and project pipeline;
- seasonal, cyclical and structural balances;
- event and disruption scenarios;
- links to currencies, inflation, equities, credit and fiscal accounts.

Headline supply-demand totals are insufficient without location, timing, quality and logistics.
