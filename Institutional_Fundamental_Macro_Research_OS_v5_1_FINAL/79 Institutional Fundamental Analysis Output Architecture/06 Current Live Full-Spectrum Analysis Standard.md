# Current Live Full-Spectrum Analysis Standard

## Objective

Describe the complete current state as of a stated timestamp using the latest available information, while separating release time, reference period and market pricing time.

## Required sections

- Executive state and dominant uncertainty
- Structural and cyclical backdrop
- Recent state changes and revisions
- Policy and institutional reaction functions
- Current pricing and vulnerable assumptions
- Asset-specific payoff and valuation
- Cross-asset confirmations and contradictions
- Positioning, liquidity and market constraints
- Scenario distribution and signposts
- Horizon-specific conclusions
- Confidence, invalidation, expiry and unknowns
- Claim-evidence ledger

## Current-data discipline

“Latest” never means mixing a current market price with a stale economic reference period without disclosure. Every material observation records observation time, publication time and reference period.
