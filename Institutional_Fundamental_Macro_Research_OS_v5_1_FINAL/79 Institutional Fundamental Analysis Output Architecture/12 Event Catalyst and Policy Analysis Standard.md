# Event, Catalyst and Policy Analysis Standard

## Event object

- scheduled data release;
- central-bank decision or communication;
- fiscal announcement;
- auction or issuance event;
- earnings release;
- regulatory decision;
- geopolitical or supply disruption;
- election or policy transition.

## Required analysis

1. Pre-event state and priced baseline.
2. Consensus distribution rather than median only.
3. Component-level surprise vector.
4. Relevant reaction function.
5. Primary and secondary transmission channels.
6. Markets or variables that should lead if the interpretation is correct.
7. Persistence and half-life conditions.
8. Alternative interpretations and false-confirmation risks.
9. Scenario matrix before and after the event.
10. Conditions that convert an event shock into a regime change.
