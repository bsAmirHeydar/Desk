# Cross-Asset and Cross-Domain Evidence Integration

## Confirmation hierarchy

Cross-asset evidence is used to test mechanisms, not to create a checklist of directional correlations.

Examples:

- Policy-path revision: front-end rates, OIS and relevant FX.
- Real-rate channel: inflation-linked markets, nominal curves and monetary metals.
- Growth revision: cyclicals, credit, commodities, freight and earnings expectations.
- Funding stress: repo, basis, cross-currency basis, bank liabilities and credit spreads.
- Physical commodity shock: inventories, time spreads, freight, refinery or processing margins and regional basis.

## Contradiction types

- timing mismatch;
- different horizon;
- different geographical exposure;
- hedge or positioning flow;
- measurement error;
- model failure;
- true regime change.

The analysis must classify contradiction before reducing or preserving confidence.
