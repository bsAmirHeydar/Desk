# Contradiction, Uncertainty, Confidence and Unknowns Ledger

## Claim classes

- observed fact;
- constructed measurement;
- estimate;
- causal interpretation;
- forecast;
- valuation judgment;
- scenario assumption;
- unknown.

## Confidence dimensions

Confidence is decomposed into:

- source quality;
- measurement reliability;
- model identification;
- regime stability;
- cross-domain confirmation;
- horizon appropriateness;
- archive completeness;
- sensitivity to assumptions.

## Contradiction ledger

For every material contradiction record:

- evidence;
- affected claim;
- possible reconciliation;
- alternative model;
- confidence impact;
- evidence needed to resolve it.

“Insufficient evidence” is a valid institutional output.
