# State, Expectations, Pricing Gap, Transmission and Payoff Stack

## Stack

1. **State:** observed and latent economic, financial, institutional and physical conditions.
2. **Expectations:** consensus, official projections, model distributions and market-implied beliefs.
3. **Pricing gap:** difference between plausible state distribution and priced distribution.
4. **Transmission:** channels through which revision changes discount rates, cash flows, balance sheets, funding, supply or risk premia.
5. **Payoff:** object-specific exposure to those channels.
6. **Constraints:** liquidity, leverage, positioning, regulation, logistics and institutional reaction.
7. **Conclusion:** horizon-specific distribution, asymmetry and invalidation.

## Double-counting control

Multiple indicators that represent the same latent factor are grouped. A rate move, dollar move and valuation change may be downstream manifestations of one policy-path revision and cannot be counted as independent evidence without justification.

## Payoff discipline

Economic strength is not automatically favorable to an asset. The analysis must identify whether the dominant effect is cash flow, discount rate, inflation compensation, funding, physical scarcity, currency translation or risk premium.
