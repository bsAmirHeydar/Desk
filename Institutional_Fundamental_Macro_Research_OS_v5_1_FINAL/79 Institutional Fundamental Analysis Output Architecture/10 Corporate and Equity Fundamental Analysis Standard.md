# Corporate and Equity Fundamental Analysis Standard

## Required architecture

- business model, segment economics and industry structure;
- revenue volume, price, mix and unit economics;
- margin structure and operating leverage;
- working capital, cash conversion and capital intensity;
- balance-sheet quality, funding and covenants;
- accounting quality, accruals and forensic risks;
- management incentives and capital allocation;
- competitive advantage and disruption risk;
- earnings expectations and revision distribution;
- valuation under multiple frameworks;
- macro, FX, rate, commodity and policy sensitivities;
- scenarios, path dependence and terminal assumptions;
- index, sector and ownership effects where relevant.

The output must separate business-state improvement from expectation and valuation improvement.
