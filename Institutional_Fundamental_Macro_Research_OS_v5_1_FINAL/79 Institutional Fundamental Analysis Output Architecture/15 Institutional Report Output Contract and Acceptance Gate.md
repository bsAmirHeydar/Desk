# Institutional Report Output Contract and Acceptance Gate

## Required report structure

1. Analysis object, cutoff and scope.
2. Executive conclusion by horizon.
3. Observed and estimated state.
4. Expectations and priced baseline.
5. Causal and rival-model map.
6. Valuation and payoff translation.
7. Cross-asset and institutional evidence.
8. Positioning, liquidity and constraints.
9. Scenario distribution and tails.
10. Contradictions, unknowns and data limits.
11. Confidence, invalidation and expiry.
12. Claim-evidence ledger and source locators.
13. Residual questions and update triggers.

## Acceptance gate

Reject the report if it:

- summarizes news without expectations and pricing;
- treats all horizons as one conclusion;
- uses generic relationships without regime conditions;
- omits material rival explanations;
- hides contradictions;
- cites source homepages rather than claims;
- fabricates missing current or historical data;
- produces certainty not supported by the information set;
- imports non-fundamental entry or chart methods.
