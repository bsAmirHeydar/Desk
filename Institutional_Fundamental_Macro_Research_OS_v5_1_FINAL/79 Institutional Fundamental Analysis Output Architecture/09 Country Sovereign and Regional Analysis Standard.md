# Country, Sovereign and Regional Analysis Standard

## Required architecture

- constitutional and institutional system;
- national accounts and growth composition;
- inflation formation and labor structure;
- fiscal accounts, debt stock, maturity, currency and investor base;
- monetary regime, central-bank credibility and financial repression;
- banking, household and corporate balance sheets;
- balance of payments, reserves, external debt and capital controls;
- trade, commodity and geopolitical dependencies;
- political economy and election-policy distribution;
- local market plumbing and foreign-investor constraints;
- crisis mechanisms and historical analogues;
- sovereign, currency, rates, credit and equity transmission.

A country book is incomplete if it is a list of macro indicators without institutions, balance sheets and external vulnerability.
