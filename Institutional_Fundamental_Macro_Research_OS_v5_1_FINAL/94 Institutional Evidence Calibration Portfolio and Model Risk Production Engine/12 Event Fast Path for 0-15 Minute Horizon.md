# Event Fast Path for 0–15 Minute Horizon

Full web research can consume a material fraction of a 0–15 minute state. V16 adds a pre-event fast path.

Before a scheduled major event, freeze: consensus/vintage, scenario tree, surprise dimensions, causal leader map, required cross-assets, timing/hazard windows, invalidations and source list. At release, update only first-release surprise, immediate causal transmission, data integrity and timing state.

The fast path may shorten latency but cannot skip source validation, first-release/revision separation or the Timing gate. If the pre-event pack is stale or missing, the micro horizon is `UNDETERMINED` rather than improvised.
