## Core rule
Fundamental says **what direction the causal force points**. Narrative says **whether the market is selecting/transmitting that force**. Timing says **whether the present window is usable**. No downstream layer may invent the opposite trade.

Hard blocks use decision materiality. Missing secondary/contextual information is handled with confidence caps, earlier review or disclosure rather than reflexive `NO_TRADE`.
