# Non-Voting and Non-Additive Aggregation Principle

D3 explicitly forbids patterns such as:

```text
Fundamental +2
Narrative +1
Positioning -1
Flow +1
Funding -1
= BUY
```

That construction destroys causal semantics and double-counts correlated descendants.

Instead D3 asks whether a named causal pathway is **open, constrained, delayed, or broken**. Independent supportive evidence may improve confirmation quality, but cannot numerically overwhelm a decision-critical obstruction.
