# Contradiction and Conflict Adjudication — V21 Hardened

Conflicting D2 sciences are not resolved by majority vote or arithmetic averaging.

The engine asks:
1. Are the claims independent or same-root descendants?
2. If same-root, do they represent genuinely different causal channels?
3. Are they measuring different horizons?
4. Is one observed and one model-implied?
5. Is the conflict directional, temporal, transmission, capacity or regime-related?
6. Which claim is decision-material for the active strategy horizon?
7. Does Module 103 classify the higher-level hypothesis state as dominant, contested, fragmented or model-disagreement?

A legitimate unresolved material conflict yields `CONSTRAINED`, `DELAYED`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or a cognitive `NO_TRADE/HOLD`; it never yields a fabricated midpoint.

A same-root conflict is preserved in the V21 root-channel map rather than reduced to one “winning” descendant. The root counts once for independence while all material effects remain visible.
