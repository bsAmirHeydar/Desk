# Contradiction and Conflict Adjudication

Conflicting D2 sciences are not resolved by majority vote.

The engine asks:
1. Are the claims independent or same-root?
2. Are they measuring different horizons?
3. Is one observed and one model-implied?
4. Is the conflict directional, temporal, or capacity-related?
5. Which claim is decision-material for the current horizon?

A legitimate unresolved material conflict yields `CONSTRAINED`, `DELAYED`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, not a fabricated average.
