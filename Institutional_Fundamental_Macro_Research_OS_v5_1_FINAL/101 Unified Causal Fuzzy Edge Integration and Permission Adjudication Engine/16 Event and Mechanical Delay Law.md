# Event and Mechanical Delay Law

D3 differentiates thesis invalidation from temporary non-usability.

`DELAYED` means:
- Fundamental Direction is preserved;
- research thesis may remain valid;
- current entry is blocked until a named mechanic/event resolves;
- next review trigger must be explicit.

This prevents temporary fix/expiry/auction/settlement distortion from being mislabeled as fundamental reversal.
