# Funding & Plumbing Integration Law

Funding does not carry a universal sign. D3 first identifies the market-specific causal channel.

Examples:
- restrictive funding may impair duration-risk transmission in equity indices;
- dollar funding stress may affect FX through a bilateral USD channel;
- collateral stress can initially force liquidation even in assets with longer-run safe-haven demand;
- reserve/repo balances are never direct trade signals by themselves.

Funding can therefore `SUPPORT`, create `FRICTION`, `DELAY`, or produce a `TRANSMISSION_BREAK`, but only with an explicit asset-specific channel and evidence lineage.
