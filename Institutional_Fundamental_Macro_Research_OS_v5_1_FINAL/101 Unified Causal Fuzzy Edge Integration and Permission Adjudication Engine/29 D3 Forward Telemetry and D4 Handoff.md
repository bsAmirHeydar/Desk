# D3 Forward Telemetry and D4 Handoff

D3 records every applied and rejected modifier before outcomes are known. D4 will evaluate whether each modifier improves conditional expectancy, drawdown, false-permission rate, missed-opportunity rate, edge duration and reversal prediction by instrument/regime/horizon.

No positive modifier receives permission-creation authority in V19. D4 alone may recommend selective promotion after forward evidence and governance thresholds are satisfied.
