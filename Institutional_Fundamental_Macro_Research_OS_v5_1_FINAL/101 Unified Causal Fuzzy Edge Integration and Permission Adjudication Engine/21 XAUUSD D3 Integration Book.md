# XAUUSD D3 Integration Book

Key D3 channels include real-yield/USD transmission, official-sector/ETF/physical flows, futures positioning, collateral/funding liquidation risk, COMEX/LBMA mechanics and scoped market capacity.

Do not treat COMEX-only observations as complete global gold flow/capacity. Funding stress can produce both liquidation and later safe-haven demand; the active horizon/channel must be identified before a modifier is applied.
