# Market Capacity Integration Law

Capacity is an execution feasibility gate, not a directional signal.

A capacity claim is valid only for its:
`venue × instrument × size × horizon × volatility/liquidity state`.

If intended execution exceeds supported capacity, final permission becomes `NO_TRADE` / `CAPACITY_BLOCK` even when the research edge remains valid.

Public futures depth cannot establish full OTC FX or physical/OTC gold capacity.
