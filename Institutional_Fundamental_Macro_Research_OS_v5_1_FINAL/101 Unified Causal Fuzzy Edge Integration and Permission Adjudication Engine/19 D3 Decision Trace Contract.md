# D3 Decision Trace Contract

Every V19 permission must preserve an auditable D3 trace:
- pre-D3 direction/edge/permission;
- each D2 Integration Vector;
- root deduplication;
- applied modifier rules;
- rejected modifiers and why;
- confidence caps;
- reversal/asymmetry state;
- validity action;
- final edge quality and permission;
- D4 promotion-readiness telemetry.

The trace is immutable after outcome realization.
