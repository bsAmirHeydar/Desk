# Unknown, Missing and Licensed Data Law

D3 never interprets missing proprietary data as neutral evidence.

- `LICENSED_REQUIRED` means the science cannot be fully established from the available public stack.
- `UNAVAILABLE` means a known source/data family is not available in the run.
- `UNDETERMINED` means even the state cannot be defensibly resolved.

Decision-critical missing data may block. Secondary gaps create caps and earlier review. Public proxy cannot silently become direct institutional flow or dealer inventory.
