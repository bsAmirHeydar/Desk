# Unified Causal Integration Law

Every D2 science enters Module 101 through an **Integration Vector** containing:

`science → causal_channel → alignment_to_fundamental → obstruction_class → materiality → evidence_grade → root_id → persistence → confidence`.

The engine never sums science scores.

## Causal roles
- `SUPPORT`: strengthens transmission confidence but cannot create direction.
- `FRICTION`: reduces asymmetry/validity without invalidating direction.
- `DELAY`: thesis may remain valid but current execution window is temporarily unusable.
- `TRANSMISSION_BREAK`: directional thesis exists but current market transmission is materially broken.
- `EXECUTION_BLOCK`: research edge may exist, but intended execution is infeasible.
- `NONE`: no material D3 effect.

## Precedence
1. Fact/evidence block.
2. Fundamental Direction/Force.
3. Narrative and Timing clearance.
4. D3 decision-critical unknowns.
5. Capacity execution block.
6. Mechanical delay/fragmentation.
7. Funding/flow transmission break.
8. Fragility/asymmetry/validity constraints.
9. Support/amplification labels.

Positive evidence never cancels a higher-precedence hard obstruction merely by count.
