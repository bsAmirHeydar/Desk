# Reversal Hazard and Asymmetry Law

D3 emits two orthogonal states:

`reversal_hazard = LOW | MODERATE | HIGH | EXTREME | UNKNOWN`

`asymmetry_state = FAVORABLE | NEUTRAL | DEGRADED | POOR | UNKNOWN`

Crowding, flow deceleration/opposition, funding restriction and mechanical concentration can raise reversal hazard or degrade remaining asymmetry. Supportive flow and uncrowded positioning may improve the asymmetry label, but cannot erase pre-existing consumption measured by Module 89.
