# Confidence Cap and Validity Window Law

D3 does not invent a universal confidence score. It emits named caps and a validity action.

## Confidence caps
Examples:
- `CROWDING_FRAGILITY`
- `FLOW_OPPOSITION`
- `FUNDING_RESTRICTION`
- `MECHANICAL_DISTORTION`
- `CAPACITY_UNCERTAINTY`
- `SECONDARY_D2_EVIDENCE_GAP`

## Validity actions
- `KEEP_BASE`
- `SHORTEN_MODERATE`
- `SHORTEN_SEVERE`
- `UNTIL_MECHANICAL_EVENT`
- `IMMEDIATE_REVIEW`

No D3 extension may outlive the next known decision-critical Fundamental/Narrative/Timing expiry.
