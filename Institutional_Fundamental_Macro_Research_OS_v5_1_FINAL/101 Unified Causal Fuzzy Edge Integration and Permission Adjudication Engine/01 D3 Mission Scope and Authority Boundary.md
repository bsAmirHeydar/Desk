# D3 Mission, Scope and Authority Boundary

D3 solves one problem: **how institutional state sciences alter the usability of an already-founded Fundamental edge**.

It does not ask “which science votes bullish?” It asks:
- Is the fundamental thesis transmitting through actual positioning/flow/funding/mechanics?
- Is the opportunity already crowded or fragile?
- Is financing/collateral plumbing obstructing transmission?
- Is a mechanical clock temporarily distorting the market?
- Is the intended execution size feasible in the relevant venue/horizon?

### Forbidden
- additive science scores;
- majority voting;
- D2-originated Fundamental Direction;
- direction reversal by positioning or flow;
- treating crowding as an automatic contrarian signal;
- treating funding stress as a universal bullish/bearish sign;
- treating expected mechanical flow as realized flow;
- using a capacity estimate outside its venue/size/horizon scope.

### V19 conservative promotion rule
D3 is enforced **one-way on permission creation**: it may keep or remove an existing BUY/SELL, but it cannot create new exposure when the pre-D3 permission is `NO_TRADE`. Supportive D2 evidence is recorded as confirmation/amplification and becomes a D4 forward-validation candidate.
