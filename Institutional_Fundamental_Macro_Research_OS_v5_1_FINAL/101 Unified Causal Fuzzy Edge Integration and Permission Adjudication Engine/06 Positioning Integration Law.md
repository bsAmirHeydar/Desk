# Positioning Integration Law

Positioning changes **fragility, remaining asymmetry and squeeze/liquidation sensitivity**; it does not create direction.

## Typical translations
- light/balanced positioning + active fundamental thesis: no automatic upgrade; may support favorable asymmetry.
- crowded positioning aligned with thesis: does **not** confirm direction; it consumes asymmetry and raises fragility.
- very crowded + high/extreme fragility: confidence cap, shorter validity, or conditional state.
- opposite crowding: may imply squeeze potential, but never flips direction without a separate fundamental thesis.

`CROWDING != CONTRARIAN_SIGNAL` is mandatory.
