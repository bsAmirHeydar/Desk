# Actual Flow Integration Law

Actual Flow is the strongest D2 transmission observation when it is identified, signed, persistent and independently rooted.

## Supportive
Persistent identified flow aligned with the Fundamental Direction can mark transmission as `CONFIRMED` or `AMPLIFIED`, but V19 cannot create a new BUY/SELL if the pre-D3 decision was NO_TRADE.

## Opposing
Material, persistent, identified opposing flow can create:
- `FRICTION` when absorption is high or evidence is partial;
- `TRANSMISSION_BREAK` when flow is strong, persistent, direct and independently rooted.

Model-implied CTA/dealer/vol-control flows carry lower epistemic authority than identified flow.
