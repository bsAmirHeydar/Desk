# Institutional Mechanics Integration Law

Mechanics alter the **path and clock** of an edge.

Options expiry, futures roll/settlement, benchmark fixing, auctions, index rebalance, passive execution, buyback windows and period-end processes are not Fundamental Direction.

A decision-critical mechanical window can:
- shorten validity;
- force review at a precise time;
- delay new entry;
- classify current tape as mechanically fragmented.

Expected mechanical demand is not realized flow. Once actual transactions are observed, they must migrate to the Actual Flow science with lineage preserved.
