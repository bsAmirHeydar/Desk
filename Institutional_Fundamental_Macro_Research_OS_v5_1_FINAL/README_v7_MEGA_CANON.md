This release completes the knowledge and analysis canon for Releases 02–14 in one modular Obsidian patch. The product objective is institutional analysis generation, not pedagogy or software deployment.

## What is completed

- macro accounting, growth and system state;
- inflation, labor, income and demand;
- fiscal, sovereign and monetary regimes;
- rates, Treasury, repo and inflation markets;
- banking, credit, NBFI and hidden leverage;
- global dollar, FX and emerging markets;
- corporate, equity and industry fundamentals;
- gold, energy and physical commodities;
- real assets, digital assets, derivatives and market-implied information;
- cross-asset, geopolitics and structural systems;
- country books and historical regimes;
- measurement, reconstruction and institutional synthesis;
- retrieval, source canon, output contracts and final adversarial audit.

## Boundary

The Vault is fundamental-only. Price is used as evidence of expectations, valuation, payoff, liquidity, positioning and outcome; it is not used as an independent chart-based decision doctrine. Training material is optional quality assurance and does not define the product score.
