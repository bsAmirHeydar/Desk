# Volume, Open Interest and Flow Non-Equivalence

Volume measures trading activity. Each transaction contains a buyer and a seller; raw volume alone does not reveal a market-wide net buyer.

Open-interest change measures outstanding contract count change, not the initiator's economic direction or participant identity. It can support a positioning/participation inference only with additional evidence.

Price up + volume up is not proof of institutional buying. Price impact and trade classification can be informative but remain methodology-dependent.

Production outputs must label such evidence as activity, proxy, derived or identified flow correctly.
