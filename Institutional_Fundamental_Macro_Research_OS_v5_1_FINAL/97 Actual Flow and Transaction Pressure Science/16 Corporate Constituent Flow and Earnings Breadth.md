# Corporate Constituent Flow and Earnings Breadth

## Purpose
Prevent index analysis from becoming only macro-duration analysis. The index fundamental engine must know whether constituent earnings/guidance/capital-allocation facts support or contradict the macro thesis.

## Direct public facts
SEC filings/XBRL can establish reported revenue, earnings, margins, cash flow, capex, share count, repurchase disclosures, debt/equity issuance and material corporate events.

## Licensed/derived facts
Analyst consensus, estimate revisions, revision breadth and high-frequency recommendation/target changes are licensed/derived unless a public source is explicitly admitted.

## Index synthesis
Use point-in-time weights and concentration. Separate:
- weighted earnings surprise contribution;
- weighted guidance/revision direction;
- mega-cap concentration;
- sector breadth;
- actual repurchase disclosure versus authorization;
- issuance/dilution supply.

## Authority
Constituent facts may enter Module 89 index Fundamental Direction through the existing causal/index adapter. Corporate flow/issuance observations enter D2 separately. No double counting.
