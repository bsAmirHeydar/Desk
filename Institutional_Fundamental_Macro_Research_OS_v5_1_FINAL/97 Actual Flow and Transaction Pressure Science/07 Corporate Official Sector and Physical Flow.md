# Corporate, Official-Sector and Physical Flow

Examples include issuer buybacks/issuance, central-bank intervention or reserve activity, and physical commodity movements.

Contract:
- distinguish authorization from execution;
- distinguish announced maximum from realized amount;
- distinguish reserve/holdings stock from transaction flow;
- preserve reporting lag;
- separate official confirmed intervention from market rumor/inference;
- for gold, separate ETF/futures activity from physical regional/official-sector channels.
