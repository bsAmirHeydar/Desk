# 97 — Actual Flow and Transaction Pressure Science

**Release:** V18.0 / D2  
**Authority:** `CANONICAL_SHADOW`  
**Final permission authority:** none in V18.

## Mission
Measure or honestly estimate **transactional change**: who is buying/selling/creating/redeeming/hedging/rebalancing, through what instrument, over what interval, and with what identification quality.

## Core law
`POSITION != FLOW != VOLUME != OPEN_INTEREST_CHANGE != PRICE_MOVE`

A flow claim requires a change process. If the buyer/seller cannot be identified, say so. If it is inferred, retain `MODEL_INFERENCE` or `DERIVED_FACT` lineage under Module 95.

## Flow evidence ladder
1. identified transaction/creation-redemption/official intervention fact;
2. identified participant flow aggregate;
3. signed transaction data with defensible classification;
4. derived flow proxy;
5. model-implied flow;
6. unavailable/undetermined.
