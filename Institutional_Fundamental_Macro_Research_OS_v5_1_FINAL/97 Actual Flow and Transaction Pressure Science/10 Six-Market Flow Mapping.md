# Six-Market Flow Mapping

## XAUUSD
ETF creation/redemption or holdings change with correct decomposition; futures/option transaction activity; official-sector/physical flow where directly reported; OTC spot flow commonly remains licensed/partial.

## NASDAQ100 / SP500 / DJIA
ETF/fund flows, futures/option transaction evidence, corporate buyback/issuance, passive/rebalance activity, auction data where available. Modeled systematic flows remain inference.

## EURUSD / USDJPY
Official intervention facts, public futures transactions/position changes, cross-border/hedging proxies. Comprehensive dealer/client spot flow generally requires licensed identified data; public proxies cannot be relabeled as complete FX flow.
