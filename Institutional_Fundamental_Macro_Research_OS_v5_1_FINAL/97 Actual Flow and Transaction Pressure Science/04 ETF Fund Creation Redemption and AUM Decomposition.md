# ETF/Fund Creation-Redemption and AUM Decomposition

Prefer directly reported creations/redemptions, shares outstanding or fund flow disclosures when available.

If flow is inferred from fund values, decompose at minimum:
`AUM change = market return effect + FX effect + distributions/corporate actions + net subscriptions/redemptions + residual`.

Do not equate price-driven AUM growth with investor inflow. For index ETFs also distinguish primary-market creation/redemption from secondary-market share turnover.
