# Flow Persistence, Absorption and Exhaustion

Flow science may describe:
- `ACCELERATING`
- `PERSISTENT`
- `DECELERATING`
- `REVERSING`
- `ONE_OFF`
- `UNKNOWN`

and market absorption:
- `LOW_ABSORPTION`
- `BALANCED`
- `HIGH_ABSORPTION`
- `UNKNOWN`

These states require explicit measurement windows. A large flow with little price impact can indicate absorption, but causality must remain qualified unless competing flows/liquidity are observed.
