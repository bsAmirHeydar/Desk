# Gold Physical, OTC, Location and Flow Intelligence

## Market decomposition
Gold is simultaneously a monetary asset, futures market, ETF asset, London OTC market and physical commodity. No single venue is the gold market.

## Observable public subsets
- WGC physically backed ETF holdings/flows and Gold Demand Trends;
- COMEX registered/eligible depository stocks and delivery notices;
- LBMA London vault holdings and clearing statistics;
- public official-sector reserve data where admitted;
- trade/import/export/location-premium evidence where source quality is adequate.

## Subscription/private subsets
LBMA trade-data products and institutional OTC/client liquidity/flow can improve coverage but remain access-dependent. Dealer/client intent is not inferred from aggregate London clearing or COMEX volume.

## Location-arbitrage questions
Track London/COMEX/Asia differences only with compatible units, delivery conventions, timing, FX and financing. A premium/discount is a state to explain, not automatically a directional signal.

## Hard boundary
`COMEX != LONDON OTC != ETF != PHYSICAL ASIA != GLOBAL GOLD`.
