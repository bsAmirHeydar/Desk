## Scenario and flexibility contract
V21 does not force every market state into the same number of scenarios or the same causal template. Production uses **2–6 explicit conditional paths** when material ambiguity exists. Quiet states may remain lean with a base and adverse path; event/crisis states may expand to several competitive or transition scenarios. Each path must have observable conditions, early indicators, confirmation/invalidation triggers, expected leaders, expected market signatures and transition routes. Scenario labels express conditional structure, not fabricated numerical probabilities.

## Cognitive fail-closed boundary
The new brain may preserve an existing Fundamental permission, constrain it, delay it or block it. It may not reverse Fundamental Direction and may not create BUY/SELL from a pre-cognitive NO_TRADE. Decision-critical unresolved causal conflict, decision-critical unmodeled-driver risk and unmonitorable decision-critical pre-mortem risk fail closed.
