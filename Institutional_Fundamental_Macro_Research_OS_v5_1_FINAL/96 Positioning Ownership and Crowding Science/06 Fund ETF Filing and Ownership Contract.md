# Fund, ETF, Filing and Ownership Contract

Distinguish:
- holdings/ownership snapshot;
- shares/units outstanding;
- creation/redemption activity;
- AUM change;
- market-value change;
- estimated investor flow.

`AUM_t - AUM_{t-1}` is not automatically investor flow because price, FX, distributions and corporate actions can change AUM.

Regulatory filing data inherit the filing's reporting lag and scope. They may be excellent ownership facts but weak live positioning facts.
