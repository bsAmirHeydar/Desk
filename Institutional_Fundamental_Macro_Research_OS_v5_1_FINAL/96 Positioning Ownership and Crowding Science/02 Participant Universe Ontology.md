# Participant Universe Ontology

Canonical participant labels are deliberately instrument-aware. Examples:

- `DEALER_INTERMEDIARY`
- `ASSET_MANAGER_INSTITUTIONAL`
- `LEVERAGED_FUND`
- `COMMERCIAL_HEDGER`
- `PRODUCER_MERCHANT_PROCESSOR_USER`
- `SWAP_DEALER`
- `PENSION_INSURANCE_REAL_MONEY`
- `MUTUAL_FUND_ETF`
- `CTA_TREND`
- `VOL_CONTROL`
- `RISK_PARITY`
- `PASSIVE_INDEX`
- `CORPORATE_ISSUER_BUYBACK`
- `CENTRAL_BANK_OFFICIAL_SECTOR`
- `RETAIL`
- `UNIDENTIFIED`

Never merge labels from two source taxonomies without an explicit mapping. A CFTC category, SEC filer class, ETF holder, and modeled CTA universe are not interchangeable populations.
