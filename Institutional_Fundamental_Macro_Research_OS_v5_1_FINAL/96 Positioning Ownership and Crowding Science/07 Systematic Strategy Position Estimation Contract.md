# Systematic Strategy Position Estimation

CTA trend, volatility-control, risk-parity, target-vol, passive/rebalance and similar states are `MODEL_INFERENCE` unless direct positions are identified.

Every estimate must publish:
- model family and version;
- instruments and weights;
- volatility estimator;
- trend signal definition;
- leverage/cap rules;
- rebalance assumption;
- input cutoff;
- uncertainty band;
- alternative plausible specification if decision material.

No single sell-side/public model estimate may be treated as universal industry inventory.
