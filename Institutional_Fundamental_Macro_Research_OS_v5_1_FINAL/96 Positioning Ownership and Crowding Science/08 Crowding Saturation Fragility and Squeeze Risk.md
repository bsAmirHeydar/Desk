# Crowding, Saturation, Fragility and Squeeze Risk

These are distinct states.

- **Crowding**: one-sided concentration relative to a declared reference distribution.
- **Saturation**: marginal capacity for additional same-direction positioning appears reduced.
- **Fragility**: state is sensitive to shocks, leverage, liquidity, stop/hedging mechanics or correlated exits.
- **Squeeze risk**: adverse move may force/induce position reduction or hedge acceleration.

Recommended ordinal states:
`VERY_LIGHT | LIGHT | BALANCED | CROWDED | VERY_CROWDED | UNKNOWN`

Fragility:
`LOW | MODERATE | HIGH | EXTREME | UNKNOWN`

These are state descriptors, not contrarian signals. A crowded trend may persist; the causal engine decides direction elsewhere.
