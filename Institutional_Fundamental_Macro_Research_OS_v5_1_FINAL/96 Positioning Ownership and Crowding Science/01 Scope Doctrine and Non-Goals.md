# Scope Doctrine

## Questions this science may answer
- Which participant cohorts are measurably long/short/hedged?
- Is exposure concentrated, stretched, balanced or reversing relative to its own history?
- Is the state likely structural, tactical, hedging-driven, leveraged, or uncertain?
- How much of the claim is observed versus inferred?

## Questions it may not answer in V18
- It cannot issue BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE.
- It cannot manufacture a directional root cause.
- It cannot treat price action as proof of an unseen institutional position.
- It cannot fill an unavailable participant universe with a proxy while preserving direct-data confidence.

## Production rule
Every output includes `authority_mode=CANONICAL_SHADOW`, `coverage_state`, source/vintage lineage, horizon, participant universe, instrument universe, and uncertainty.
