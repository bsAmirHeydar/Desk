# Validation and D3 Promotion Gates

Before any positioning feature can constrain or amplify the final edge:
1. point-in-time replay must be clean;
2. direct vs inferred coverage must be measured;
3. predictive/incremental value must be tested forward/out-of-sample;
4. same-root leakage must be controlled;
5. regime dependence and instability must be measured;
6. multiple-testing controls apply;
7. effect must be encoded as a bounded modifier, never new Fundamental Direction authority.

Until promotion: `SHADOW_ONLY`.
