# Subphase 01 — Fundamental Science and Ontology

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Define the object, categories, boundaries and lifecycle of institutional fundamental knowledge before any domain-specific theory is admitted.

## Prerequisites
- None; this subphase establishes its own vocabulary.

1. [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/01 Fundamental Science and Ontology/What Fundamental Analysis Is — Object, Boundary and Institutional Purpose]]

4. [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/01 Fundamental Science and Ontology/Stocks, Flows, Rates, Levels and Changes]]
5. [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/01 Fundamental Science and Ontology/Fundamental Information, Expectations, Valuation and Risk Premia]]
6. [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/01 Fundamental Science and Ontology/Fundamental Analysis Across Instruments, Sectors and Countries]]
7. [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/01 Fundamental Science and Ontology/The Fundamental Research Lifecycle]]
8. [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/01 Fundamental Science and Ontology/Boundary Cases — What Is and Is Not Fundamental Knowledge]]

## Required mastery outcomes
- Distinguish state, structure, regime, shock, stock, flow, expectation, valuation and risk premium.
- Define why and how market information can enter a fundamental-only canon.
- Map a research object across instruments, countries and horizons.
- Run a complete research lifecycle with an auditable claim structure.

## Accreditation gate
A reader passes this subphase only when they can explain the concepts in plain language, reconstruct the formal logic, compare rival views, diagnose measurement error, and apply the framework to an unfamiliar historical case without hindsight.
