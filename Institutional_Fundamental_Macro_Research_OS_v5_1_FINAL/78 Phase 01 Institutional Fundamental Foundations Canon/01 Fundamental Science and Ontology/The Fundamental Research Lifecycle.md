# The Fundamental Research Lifecycle

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Institutional quality comes from a lifecycle, not a single opinion: mandate definition, question formulation, evidence collection, model comparison, synthesis, review, archival and later re-evaluation must form an auditable loop.

## Analytical intuition

Good research is not “read news and form a view.” It begins by asking a precise question, deciding what evidence would answer it, checking alternatives, documenting uncertainty, and later reviewing what changed and what was learned.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Mandate | The domain, horizon, object, constraints and purpose of the research. |
| Research question | A specific question whose terms and possible answers can be distinguished by evidence. |
| Claim-evidence ledger | A record linking each material claim to source, transformation, inference and uncertainty. |
| Adversarial review | A structured attempt to produce the strongest rival interpretation and identify hidden assumptions. |
| Knowledge retirement | Downgrading or replacing claims when evidence, institutions or measurement systems change. |

## Mechanism map

1. Write the mandate and object.
2. Decompose the question into factual, measurement, causal, forecast and valuation components.
3. Build a source hierarchy and claim-evidence ledger.
4. Estimate state and expectations with multiple methods.
5. Compare rival models and scenario distributions.
6. Publish a bounded conclusion with confidence and expiry.
7. Review new evidence, errors and model drift; preserve the historical record.

## Formal structure and notation

### Research value

$$
RV=\Delta Decision\ Quality-Research\ Cost-Complexity\ Cost
$$

More information is valuable only if it improves a defined judgment enough to exceed time, attention and complexity costs.

### Calibration condition

$$
\Pr(Y=1\mid \hat p=p)\approx p
$$

If the research uses probabilities, long-run outcomes among statements assigned probability p should occur at roughly that frequency, subject to adequate samples and stable definitions.

## Competing interpretations and adversarial synthesis

| View | What it explains | Where it can fail |
|---|---|---|
| Fast discretionary synthesis | Integrates qualitative information quickly. | Can hide inconsistent definitions and hindsight. |
| Formal model-first research | Forces assumptions and reproducibility. | Can exclude institutional knowledge that is hard to quantify. |
| Hybrid committee process | Combines models, specialists and adversarial review. | Can produce consensus dilution and unclear accountability. |

## Historical and institutional cases

### Central-bank meeting preparation

The lifecycle begins before the meeting with priced distribution, reaction-function hypotheses and scenario mapping; the post-event review separates communication surprise from data interpretation.

### Sovereign-risk review

Fiscal numbers, debt structure, institutions, external balance and political constraints are combined, with explicit scenarios rather than a single debt-ratio threshold.

## Multihorizon interpretation

- **Structural/secular:** identify persistent institutions, stocks or constraints governing **The Fundamental Research Lifecycle**; begin with: Write the mandate and object.
- **Cyclical:** determine how the mechanism changes across expansion, slowdown, recession, recovery, inflation and disinflation; interrogate: Decompose the question into factual, measurement, causal, forecast and valuation components.
- **Tactical/multi-week:** identify which expectation, valuation or policy assumption is vulnerable; test: Build a source hierarchy and claim-evidence ledger.
- **Event/current:** define the new information, contemporaneous expectation and first observable mechanism; verify: Estimate state and expectations with multiple methods.
- Use the full inheritance and conflict standard in [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/06 Multihorizon Synthesis and Institutional Conclusions/Horizon Inheritance, Conflict Resolution and Double-Counting Control]].

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Starting with a desired conclusion.
- Collecting sources without a research question.
- Leaving important claims uncited because they seem obvious.
- Publishing point estimates without sensitivity or rival models.
- Failing to archive superseded views and the information set that supported them.

## Worked reasoning protocol

## Adversarial analytical checks and accreditation questions

1. Write a research mandate for current gold fundamentals without using directional shorthand.
2. Build a claim-evidence ledger for a central-bank reaction-function claim.
3. Conduct a pre-mortem on a sovereign-risk conclusion.
