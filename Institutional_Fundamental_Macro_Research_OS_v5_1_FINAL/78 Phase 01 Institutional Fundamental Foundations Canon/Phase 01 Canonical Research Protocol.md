# Phase 01 Canonical Research Protocol

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Provide one shared protocol for foundational research so individual monographs can remain topic-specific rather than repeating generic workflow text.

## Protocol

1. **Define the research object.** State the entity, system, contract, balance sheet, physical process or institutional rule being studied.
2. **Fix unit, horizon and information set.** Record timing, frequency, denomination, measurement convention, publication cutoff and vintage.
3. **Classify every statement.** Separate fact, measurement, estimate, accounting identity, model, causal claim, forecast, valuation judgment, mandate-specific conclusion and unknown.
4. **Reconcile accounting and physical feasibility.** Check stocks, flows, counterparties, capacity, conservation and balance-sheet closure before behavioral interpretation.
5. **Specify the causal question.** Define exposure, outcome, counterfactual, unit and horizon; draw rival mechanisms and possible confounders.
6. **Build competing models.** Steelman serious traditions, state their domains and derive evidence that would discriminate among them.
7. **Represent uncertainty.** Use probability, ranges, scenarios or partial identification consistent with evidence; avoid false point precision.
8. **Separate state from expectations and valuation.** Reconstruct what is believed or priced, then identify the research–expectation gap.
9. **Map transmission.** Explain cash-flow, discount-rate, default, scarcity, currency, funding, liquidity, political and behavioral channels.
10. **Resolve horizons.** Keep structural, cyclical, tactical and event conclusions separate; apply inheritance and double-counting controls.
11. **Write the conclusion conditionally.** State proposition, horizon, confidence, evidence quality, rival view, invalidation, expiry and insufficient-evidence conditions.
12. **Preserve auditability.** Attach claim-level citations, locators, assumption register, unknown ledger, contradiction register and revision history.
13. **Red-team before promotion.** Run a premortem, strongest rival case and historical point-in-time analogue without outcome contamination.
14. **Review after evidence changes.** Update probabilities and conclusions by rule, not by attachment to prior narrative.

## Use boundary

This protocol is a research standard. It contains no non-fundamental price-pattern, indicator or entry method. Market prices may appear only as evidence about expectations, valuation, response, liquidity, positioning, basis, volatility or realized outcome.
