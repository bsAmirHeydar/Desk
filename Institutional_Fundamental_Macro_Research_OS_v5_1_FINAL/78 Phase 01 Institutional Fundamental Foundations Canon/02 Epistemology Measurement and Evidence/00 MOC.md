# Subphase 02 — Epistemology, Measurement and Evidence

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Establish what can be known, how economic concepts are measured, how evidence is sourced and how uncertainty, incentives, revisions and falsification are governed.

## Prerequisites
- [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/01 Fundamental Science and Ontology/00 MOC]]

## Required mastery outcomes
- Label every material statement by epistemic type.
- Design a measurement contract and point-in-time evidence packet.
- Diagnose selection, revision, missingness and source-incentive bias.
- State pre-specified falsifiers and maintain an unknown register.

## Accreditation gate
A reader passes this subphase only when they can explain the concepts in plain language, reconstruct the formal logic, compare rival views, diagnose measurement error, and apply the framework to an unfamiliar historical case without hindsight.
