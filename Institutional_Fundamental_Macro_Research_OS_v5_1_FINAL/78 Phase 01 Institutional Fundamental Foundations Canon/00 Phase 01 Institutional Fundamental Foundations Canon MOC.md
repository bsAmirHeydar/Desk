# Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon

> [!abstract] Role
> Phase 01 establishes the inference, evidence and synthesis language required to produce institutional fundamental analysis. It is not a course and is not judged by teaching progression.

## Canonical institutional inference chain

```text
Object and scope
→ information set and measurement
→ accounting and institutional constraints
→ observed and latent state
→ competing causal models
→ expectations and pricing
→ valuation, payoff and transmission
→ cross-domain evidence
→ scenarios and tails
→ horizon-specific conclusion
→ contradiction, confidence, invalidation, expiry and unknowns
```

## Mandatory use in later phases

Every later domain must use Phase 01 to define:

- object and variable ontology;
- information-set boundary;
- causal estimand and rival models;
- evidence hierarchy and source lineage;
- horizon inheritance and conflict;
- scenario and uncertainty representation;
- conclusion, contradiction, invalidation and expiry.

## Quality-control artifacts

The glossary, formula atlas, cases and adversarial test remain because they expose missing definitions and incoherent synthesis. They are not the objective of the module and do not replace domain depth.

## Completion boundary

Phase 01 is complete as a foundational inference standard. It does not imply that macro accounting, rates, FX, corporate, commodity, country or portfolio domains are complete.
