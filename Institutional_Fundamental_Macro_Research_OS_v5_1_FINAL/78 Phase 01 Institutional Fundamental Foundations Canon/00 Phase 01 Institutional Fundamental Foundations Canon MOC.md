# Phase 01 — Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon

> [!abstract] Mission
> Establish the common scientific language of the entire Fundamental Knowledge Canon. No later domain may use undefined objects, mixed evidence, unexamined causal claims, incoherent probability, hidden assumptions, horizon leakage or one-model certainty.

## Why this phase exists

A large fundamental library can still be institutionally weak if its concepts change meaning across notes, evidence is not tied to claims, causality is inferred from co-movement, probabilities are rhetorical, schools are caricatured, or long-run and event-level conclusions are mixed. Phase 01 is the constitutional layer that prevents those failures.

## Canonical research doctrine

```text
Define the object, unit, horizon and information set
→ classify facts, measurements, estimates, models, judgments and unknowns
→ reconcile accounting, units, stocks and flows
→ identify causal estimand, mechanism and rival explanations
→ update probabilities and scenarios without double counting
→ condition relationships on regime, institution and horizon
→ separate state, expectations, valuation, carry and risk premium
→ state a conditional conclusion with confidence, invalidation and expiry
→ preserve sources, contradiction, dissent and revision history
```

## Foundation requirements imposed on all later phases

- Every material term has an operational definition.
- Every quantity has units, timing, denomination and vintage.
- Every important claim has a claim-level evidence and contradiction record.
- Accounting identities are separated from behavioral and causal models.
- Forecasts specify event, horizon, information set and evaluation method.
- Rival models are steelmanned and compared by discriminating evidence.
- Structural, cyclical, tactical and event conclusions remain separate but linked.
- Fundamental meaning is mapped through asset payoff and institutional transmission.
- Confidence, invalidation, expiry and insufficient-evidence outputs are mandatory.
- Historical cases freeze ex-ante information before ex-post attribution.

## Completion boundary

Phase 01 is a 10/10 foundation canon when all notes, exercises, references, links and accreditation artifacts pass the audit. This does **not** claim that later domains—macro accounting, rates, FX, corporate analysis, commodities or country books—are complete. It establishes the standard those phases must satisfy.
