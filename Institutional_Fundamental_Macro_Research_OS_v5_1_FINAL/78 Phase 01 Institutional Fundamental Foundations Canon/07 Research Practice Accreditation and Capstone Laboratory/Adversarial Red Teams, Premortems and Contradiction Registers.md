# Adversarial Red Teams, Premortems and Contradiction Registers

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Research quality improves when a designated process seeks the strongest rival explanation, failure path and disconfirming evidence before a conclusion is promoted. Adversarial review must target mechanisms, not personalities.

## Beginner intuition

Do not ask a friendly colleague “does this look okay?” Ask someone to prove the thesis wrong, find hidden assumptions and build the best competing case.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Red team | Independent challenge function tasked with attacking a conclusion. |
| Premortem | Exercise assuming the conclusion failed and reconstructing plausible reasons. |
| Contradiction register | Persistent list of unresolved incompatible claims or evidence. |
| Steelman | Strongest fair version of a rival view. |
| Kill criterion | Evidence or condition requiring withdrawal or material downgrade. |

## Mechanism map

1. Separate author and challenger roles.
2. Require a steelmanned rival model.
3. Run premortem across data, causality, policy and institutional response.
4. Search for contradictory primary evidence.
5. Define kill criteria before promotion.
6. Record dissent and later score which objections mattered.

## Formal structure and notation

### Adversarial posterior range

$$
p(H\mid D,M)\in[\underline p,\overline p]_{credible\ models}
$$

A range across credible rival models may be more honest than one-model confidence.

## Competing interpretations and adversarial synthesis

| View | What it explains | Where it can fail |
|---|---|---|
| Consensus review | Efficient and cohesive. | Groupthink and authority bias. |
| Formal red team | Creates independent challenge. | Can become ritual opposition. |
| Prediction tournament | Scores forecasts objectively. | May privilege easily scored questions over important mechanisms. |

## Historical and institutional cases

### Credit-boom optimism

Underwriting, collateral and refinancing assumptions require explicit hostile testing.

### Policy soft-landing thesis

Red team tests inflation persistence, lagged tightening and nonlinear financial stress.

## Multihorizon interpretation

- **Structural/secular:** identify persistent institutions, stocks or constraints governing **Adversarial Red Teams, Premortems and Contradiction Registers**; begin with: Separate author and challenger roles.
- **Cyclical:** determine how the mechanism changes across expansion, slowdown, recession, recovery, inflation and disinflation; interrogate: Require a steelmanned rival model.
- **Tactical/multi-week:** identify which expectation, valuation or policy assumption is vulnerable; test: Run premortem across data, causality, policy and institutional response.
- **Event/current:** define the new information, contemporaneous expectation and first observable mechanism; verify: Search for contradictory primary evidence.

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Red team lacks independence.
- Only stylistic objections are raised.
- Dissent is recorded but never changes conclusions.
- Kill criteria written after failure.
- Contradictions disappear from later drafts.
