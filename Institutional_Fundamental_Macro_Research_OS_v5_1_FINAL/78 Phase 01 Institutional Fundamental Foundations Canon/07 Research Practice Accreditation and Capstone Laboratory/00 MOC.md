## Required mastery outcomes
- Design bounded, falsifiable and decision-relevant research questions.
- Build claim-level evidence and citation records.
- Maintain assumptions, unknowns and model limitations.
- Reconstruct historical cases without hindsight.
- Conduct adversarial review and structured committee deliberation.
- Write layered institutional research.
- Use the glossary and formula atlas precisely.
- Pass the comprehensive examination and capstone transfer cases.

## Accreditation gate
Completion requires an examination score of at least 85 percent, no critical failure condition, and two accepted capstone cases reviewed by an independent reader.
