# Phase 01 Comprehensive Accreditation Examination

> [!abstract] Standard
> This is an open-source, closed-hindsight examination. Candidates may use the Vault and primary sources, but every answer must define the object, state assumptions, distinguish evidence from inference, and disclose uncertainty. Memorized vocabulary without transfer does not pass.

## Ontology and scope

1. Define fundamental analysis for a sovereign bond, bank equity, currency, gold and crude oil. Explain what is common and what changes.

2. Distinguish stock, flow, rate, level and change using one balance-sheet example.

3. Explain why a market price can be fundamental evidence without price patterns becoming a fundamental method.

4. Distinguish state, regime, structure, shock and transition.

## Epistemology and evidence

5. Build a fact–estimate–model–judgment–decision ladder for an inflation release.

6. Explain publication time, reference time and vintage with a historical example.

7. Design a claim-level citation record for a central-bank statement.

8. Identify selection and survivorship bias in a crisis sample.

9. Write a falsifiable claim and its severe test.

## Causality

10. Define a causal estimand for monetary tightening and distinguish it from an event response.

11. Draw a DAG for credit growth and GDP including policy reaction and expectations.

12. Explain simultaneity in exchange rates and interest rates.

13. Compare natural experiments, instruments, narrative identification and structural models.

14. Describe a reflexive loop and evidence that would distinguish it from common shocks.

## Mathematics and probability

15. Explain why an accounting identity is not a behavioral equation.

16. Calculate and interpret a simple Bayesian odds update.

17. Construct a reference class and explain its exclusions.

18. Compare point, interval and density forecasts.

19. Explain calibration, discrimination and sharpness.

20. Derive half-life for a stable first-order process.

21. Interpret a Lagrange multiplier in a bank-capital problem.

22. Explain why correlation can rise in stress without direct contagion.

## Competing models

23. Steelman Classical, Keynesian, Monetarist, Austrian, Post-Keynesian, fiscal-theory, institutional and complexity explanations for one episode.

24. Explain Lucas critique and Goodhart law using policy examples.

25. Design a disciplined model-pluralism matrix with discriminating evidence.

26. Explain how a forecast can become self-fulfilling or self-defeating.

## Multihorizon synthesis

27. Construct structural, cyclical, tactical and event states that coexist without contradiction.

28. Diagnose double counting in a multi-indicator dashboard.

29. Separate state, expectations, valuation, carry and risk premium for one asset.

30. Build a cross-asset transmission matrix for an inflation shock.

31. Define thesis half-life, invalidation and expiry.

32. Write a standardized “insufficient evidence” conclusion.

## Research practice

33. Convert a broad question into a complete research tuple.

34. Build a claim–evidence–contradiction matrix.

35. Create an assumption and unknown register.

36. Design a historical replay that prevents hindsight contamination.

37. Write a red-team premortem and kill criteria.

38. Design a committee process preserving independent priors and dissent.

39. Write a one-page institutional memo outline with layered appendices.

## Capstone requirement

Complete two cases from [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/07 Research Practice Accreditation and Capstone Laboratory/Phase 01 Capstone Historical and Institutional Cases]], one macro-policy case and one balance-sheet or physical-market case.

## Scoring

| Dimension | Weight | Failure condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Definition and scope | 15% | Object or horizon is undefined. |
| Evidence and information set | 20% | Hindsight, unsupported claims or missing provenance. |
| Causal and accounting logic | 20% | Correlation substitutes for mechanism or identities are misused. |
| Competing models and uncertainty | 15% | Rival views are omitted or caricatured. |
| Formal reasoning | 10% | Equations lack interpretation, units or assumptions. |
| Multihorizon synthesis | 10% | Horizons are collapsed or double counted. |
| Communication and governance | 10% | No invalidation, expiry, contradiction or unknown register. |

A total score of 85% is required, with no failure condition in any dimension.
