# Institutional Research Memo Architecture and Editorial Standards

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> A research memo is an auditable compression of a larger evidence process. It must preserve definitions, mechanism, uncertainty, rival views and conclusion conditions while making the most decision-relevant information retrievable.

## Beginner intuition

A good memo is not the longest document. It lets a reader see the claim, evidence, mechanism, risks and what would change the conclusion without searching through pages of narrative.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Executive thesis | Concise statement of the research conclusion, horizon and confidence. |
| Evidence packet | Structured collection of primary data, documents and calculations. |
| Mechanism chain | Ordered causal or accounting steps connecting evidence to conclusion. |
| Decision appendix | Mandate-specific implications separated from scientific claims. |
| Revision history | Record of material changes, reasons and evidence. |

## Mechanism map

1. Lead with proposition, horizon and uncertainty.
2. Define terms and priced baseline.
3. Present mechanism and discriminating evidence.
4. Steelman rival interpretation.
5. List scenarios, invalidation and expiry.
6. Attach claim matrix, sources and revision log.

## Formal structure and notation

### Compression loss concept

$$
Loss_{memo}=CriticalInformation_{source}-CriticalInformation_{memo}
$$

Editorial quality minimizes loss of material assumptions, uncertainty and contradiction while reducing irrelevant detail.

## Competing interpretations and adversarial synthesis

| View | What it explains | Where it can fail |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative memo | Readable and persuasive. | Can hide assumptions and evidence gaps. |
| Template memo | Comparable and auditable. | Can create boilerplate and false uniformity. |
| Layered document | Executive summary linked to deep appendices. | Requires disciplined cross-linking and maintenance. |

## Historical and institutional cases

### Central-bank preview

Must separate state forecast, priced distribution, policy reaction and scenario response.

### Corporate initiation

Needs accounting, industry, valuation, governance and risks without duplicating generic material.

## Multihorizon interpretation

- **Structural/secular:** identify persistent institutions, stocks or constraints governing **Institutional Research Memo Architecture and Editorial Standards**; begin with: Lead with proposition, horizon and uncertainty.
- **Cyclical:** determine how the mechanism changes across expansion, slowdown, recession, recovery, inflation and disinflation; interrogate: Define terms and priced baseline.
- **Tactical/multi-week:** identify which expectation, valuation or policy assumption is vulnerable; test: Present mechanism and discriminating evidence.
- **Event/current:** define the new information, contemporaneous expectation and first observable mechanism; verify: Steelman rival interpretation.
- Use the full inheritance and conflict standard in [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/06 Multihorizon Synthesis and Institutional Conclusions/Horizon Inheritance, Conflict Resolution and Double-Counting Control]].

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Conclusion before question definition.
- Generic sections repeated across topics.
- No claim-level sources.
- Rival view reduced to a straw man.
- No revision history or stale-data warning.
