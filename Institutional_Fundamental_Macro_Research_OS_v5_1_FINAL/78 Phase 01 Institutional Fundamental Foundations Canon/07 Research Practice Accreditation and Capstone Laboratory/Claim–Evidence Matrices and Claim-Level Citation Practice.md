# Claim–Evidence Matrices and Claim-Level Citation Practice

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Every material conclusion must be decomposed into claims with explicit evidence, locator, time status, source quality, inference type and contradiction status. A bibliography is not an evidence chain.

## Beginner intuition

Listing ten prestigious sources does not show which source supports which sentence. A claim–evidence matrix makes the connection visible and exposes unsupported leaps.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Claim | A proposition that can be evaluated as true, false, uncertain or conditional. |
| Evidence item | Observation, document, estimate, identification result or historical record bearing on a claim. |
| Locator | Exact table, page, paragraph, series, release or timestamp. |
| Inference type | Deductive, accounting, descriptive, causal, predictive, analogical or judgmental. |
| Contradiction status | Whether credible evidence supports, weakens or conflicts with the claim. |

## Mechanism map

1. Atomize the thesis into material claims.
2. Assign claim type and burden of proof.
3. Link primary evidence with exact locators and vintage.
4. Separate source statement from analyst inference.
5. Record contrary evidence and source dependence.
6. Update confidence when evidence or definitions change.

## Formal structure and notation

### Evidence-weighted claim support

$$
Support(H)=\sum_iw_iDirection_i-DependencePenalty-ContradictionPenalty

This is a governance representation; weights must reflect source quality and dependence, not authority alone.

## Competing interpretations and adversarial synthesis

| View | What it explains | Where it can fail |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative citation | Citations appear in prose. | Can hide unsupported transitions. |
| Structured claim ledger | Auditable and machine-readable. | Can become bureaucratic if every trivial sentence is logged. |
| Evidence hierarchy | Ranks primary and methodological sources. | A lower-ranked source may still contain unique direct evidence. |

## Historical and institutional cases

### Policy communication

Statement text, transcript, projection table and analyst interpretation must be separated.

### Company earnings quality

Reported figures, footnotes, management explanations and forensic inference have different evidentiary status.

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Citing a homepage rather than a locator.
- Using one source to support a stronger claim than it makes.
- Ignoring revision and publication time.
- Counting repeated reports of the same underlying source as independent.
- Omitting credible contradiction.
