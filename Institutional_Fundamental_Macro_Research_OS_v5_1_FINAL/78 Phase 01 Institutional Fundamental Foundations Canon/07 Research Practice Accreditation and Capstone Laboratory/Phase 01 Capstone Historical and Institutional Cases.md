# Phase 01 Capstone Historical and Institutional Cases

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Require transfer of the entire Foundations Canon to complex episodes. The cases are research assignments rather than finished narratives; completion requires point-in-time evidence, rival models and auditable synthesis.

## Great Inflation and Volcker disinflation

Separate supply, demand, expectations, fiscal, monetary and institutional mechanisms; reconstruct what was knowable before the regime was named; compare monetarist, New Keynesian, political-economy and credibility interpretations.

### Required reconstruction

1. Exact research object and cutoffs.
2. Contemporaneous facts, estimates and expectations.
3. Accounting and balance-sheet map.
4. At least four rival causal models.
5. Scenario tree and transition indicators.
6. Structural, cyclical, tactical and event interpretation.
7. Cross-asset and cross-country transmission.
8. Ex-ante conclusion with confidence, invalidation and expiry.
9. Separate ex-post attribution.
10. Source and contradiction ledger.

## Accreditation standard

A case passes only if another analyst can reproduce the information set, distinguish facts from judgments, challenge the causal map, and understand why the conclusion differed across horizons and mandates.
