## Required mastery outcomes
- Audit units, transformations, accounting identities and compounding.
- Use vectors, matrices, dynamic systems and constrained optimization with economic interpretation.
- Define and condition probabilities coherently.
- Update rival hypotheses with explicit likelihoods and priors.
- Construct and evaluate scenario and density forecasts.
- Separate beliefs from mandate-dependent decisions and use robust conclusions under ambiguity.

## Accreditation gate
A reader passes only when they can reconstruct the equations, explain every variable and assumption, identify misuse, and apply the mathematics to an unfamiliar economic system without replacing interpretation with notation.
