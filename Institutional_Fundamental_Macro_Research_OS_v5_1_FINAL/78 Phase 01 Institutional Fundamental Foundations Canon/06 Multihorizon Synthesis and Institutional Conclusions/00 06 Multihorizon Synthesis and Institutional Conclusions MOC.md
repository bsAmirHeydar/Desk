## Required mastery outcomes
- Define every conclusion by horizon, information set and expiry.
- Resolve cross-horizon conflict without narrative cherry-picking.
- Separate state, expectations, valuation, carry and risk premium.
- Map causal meaning across assets and mandates.
- Produce conditional, falsifiable and calibrated fundamental conclusions.

## Accreditation gate
A reader passes this subphase only when they can explain the concepts in plain language, reconstruct the formal logic, compare rival views, diagnose measurement error, and apply the framework to an unfamiliar historical case without hindsight.
