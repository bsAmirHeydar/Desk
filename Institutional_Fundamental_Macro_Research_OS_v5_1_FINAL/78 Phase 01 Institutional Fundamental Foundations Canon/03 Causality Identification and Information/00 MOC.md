# Subphase 03 — Causality, Identification and Information

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Build a complete causal discipline for macro-financial research, including counterfactuals, graphs, endogeneity, quasi-experiments, mechanisms, information shocks, feedback and transportability.

## Required mastery outcomes
- Translate narratives into explicit causal estimands and graphs.
- Diagnose endogeneity, simultaneity, anticipation and information effects.
- Evaluate quasi-experimental assumptions and external validity.
- Map transmission channels and reflexive feedback without circular reasoning.

## Accreditation gate
A reader passes this subphase only when they can explain the concepts in plain language, reconstruct the formal logic, compare rival views, diagnose measurement error, and apply the framework to an unfamiliar historical case without hindsight.
