# Stock-Flow-Consistent and Balance-Sheet Traditions

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Every financial asset has a liability counterpart, flows reconcile with stocks, and sector balance sheets constrain feasible paths.

## Beginner intuition

Focus on accounting closure, sectoral balances, portfolio allocation and dynamic consistency.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Core ontology | What entities, constraints and adjustment mechanisms the tradition treats as primary. |
| Closure rule | What balances the model or determines the path: prices, quantities, policy, expectations, finance or institutions. |
| Empirical signature | Observations that would be more likely if this framework is materially relevant. |
| Policy implication | Conditional conclusion generated by the model, not a universal prescription. |

Use [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/05 Competing Models Reflexivity and Pluralism/Economic School Evaluation Protocol]]. For **Stock-Flow-Consistent and Balance-Sheet Traditions**, the distinctive analytical claim is:

## Formal structure and notation

### Canonical formal skeleton

$$
\Delta Balance\ Sheet=Transactions+Valuation+Other\ Changes
$$

The equation is a compact entry point. Mastery requires understanding assumptions, units, institutional meaning and where the relation fails.

| View | What it explains | Where it can fail |
|---|---|---|

## Historical and institutional cases

### Sectoral balances

Private, government and external balances must reconcile.

### Financial fragility

Gross balance-sheet composition and currency maturity can matter even when net positions look balanced.

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Accounting coherence does not determine behavioral causality or unique forecasts.
- Using the school as identity rather than a conditional model.
- Citing famous conclusions without reconstructing assumptions.
- Ignoring evidence that falls outside the preferred ontology.
- Combining incompatible closure rules without explaining the synthesis.
