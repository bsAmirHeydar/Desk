# Post-Keynesian, Endogenous-Money and Minskyan Traditions

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Credit money is created within banking systems, demand is historically contingent, and financial stability can generate leverage and fragility.

## Analytical intuition

Focus on endogenous credit, mark-up pricing, income distribution, uncertainty, balance sheets and financial cycles.

## Historical and institutional cases

### Financial instability hypothesis

Hedge finance can migrate toward speculative and Ponzi structures during stable periods.

### Endogenous money

Bank lending creates deposits subject to capital, funding, risk and demand constraints rather than a fixed reserve multiplier.

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Frameworks can be rich institutionally but weakly disciplined if every outcome is described after the fact.
- Using the school as identity rather than a conditional model.
- Citing famous conclusions without reconstructing assumptions.
- Ignoring evidence that falls outside the preferred ontology.
- Combining incompatible closure rules without explaining the synthesis.
