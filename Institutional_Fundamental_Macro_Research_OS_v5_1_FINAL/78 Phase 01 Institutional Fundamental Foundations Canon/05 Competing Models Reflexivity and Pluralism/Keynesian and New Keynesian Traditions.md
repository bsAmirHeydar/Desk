# Keynesian and New Keynesian Traditions

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Demand, expectations, nominal rigidities and policy coordination can keep output away from potential and create state-dependent multipliers.

## Analytical intuition

Focus on effective demand, uncertainty, wage-price adjustment, monetary non-neutrality and stabilization policy.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Core ontology | What entities, constraints and adjustment mechanisms the tradition treats as primary. |
| Closure rule | What balances the model or determines the path: prices, quantities, policy, expectations, finance or institutions. |
| Empirical signature | Observations that would be more likely if this framework is materially relevant. |
| Policy implication | Conditional conclusion generated by the model, not a universal prescription. |

## Historical and institutional cases

### Liquidity trap

When policy rates and safe yields are constrained, fiscal and expectation channels can become more powerful.

### Phillips curve

Inflation depends on expected inflation, slack and shocks, but measurement and regime stability are contested.

- **Cyclical:** derive the tradition’s explanation of expansion, contraction, inflation, employment and financial fragility; compare its turning-point logic with serious rivals.
- **Tactical/multi-week:** determine which policy, expectation, funding or distributional change would make the framework’s mechanism newly relevant.
- **Event/current:** ask whether the event is genuine evidence for the mechanism or only a short observation compatible with many models.

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Simple gap models can understate financial structure, supply constraints and political implementation.
- Using the school as identity rather than a conditional model.
- Citing famous conclusions without reconstructing assumptions.
- Ignoring evidence that falls outside the preferred ontology.
- Combining incompatible closure rules without explaining the synthesis.
