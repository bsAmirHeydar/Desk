# Fiscal Theory, Functional Finance and MMT Traditions

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Fiscal commitments, monetary-fiscal coordination and nominal resource constraints can determine the price level and policy space.

## Beginner intuition

Focus on consolidated public balance sheets, currency sovereignty, taxation, debt valuation and inflation constraints.

## Historical and institutional cases

### Fiscal theory of price level

The real value of nominal government liabilities adjusts to expected primary surpluses under specified policy regimes.

### Modern Monetary Theory

Currency issuers face real-resource and inflation constraints rather than mechanical household financing constraints, with crucial institutional qualifications.

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Consolidation can hide institutional separation, market confidence, distribution and external constraints; slogans can replace conditional analysis.
- Using the school as identity rather than a conditional model.
- Citing famous conclusions without reconstructing assumptions.
- Ignoring evidence that falls outside the preferred ontology.
- Combining incompatible closure rules without explaining the synthesis.
