## Required mastery outcomes
- Explain the ontology, closure rule and empirical signature of every major tradition.
- Steelman rival schools before criticism.
- Use model pluralism without incoherent cherry-picking.
- Apply the Lucas critique, Goodhart law and reflexive feedback to model governance.

## Accreditation gate
A reader passes this subphase only when they can explain the concepts in plain language, reconstruct the formal logic, compare rival views, diagnose measurement error, and apply the framework to an unfamiliar historical case without hindsight.
