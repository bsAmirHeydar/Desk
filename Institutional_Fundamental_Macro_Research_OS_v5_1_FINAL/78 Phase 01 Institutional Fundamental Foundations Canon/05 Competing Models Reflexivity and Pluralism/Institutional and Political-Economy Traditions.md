# Institutional and Political-Economy Traditions

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> Rules, property rights, power, distribution, state capacity and political coalitions shape economic outcomes and the credibility of policy.

## Analytical intuition

Focus on institutional persistence, enforcement, rent allocation, legitimacy, path dependence and collective action.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Core ontology | What entities, constraints and adjustment mechanisms the tradition treats as primary. |
| Closure rule | What balances the model or determines the path: prices, quantities, policy, expectations, finance or institutions. |
| Empirical signature | Observations that would be more likely if this framework is materially relevant. |
| Policy implication | Conditional conclusion generated by the model, not a universal prescription. |

## Historical and institutional cases

### Central-bank independence

Legal design, political support and fiscal context jointly determine operational credibility.

### Development divergence

Similar factor endowments can produce different outcomes under different institutions and state capacity.

## Multihorizon interpretation

- **Structural/secular:** identify the institutions, property relations, technology, capital structure or balance-sheet architecture that make **Institutional and Political-Economy Traditions** informative over long horizons.
- **Cyclical:** derive the tradition’s explanation of expansion, contraction, inflation, employment and financial fragility; compare its turning-point logic with serious rivals.
- **Tactical/multi-week:** determine which policy, expectation, funding or distributional change would make the framework’s mechanism newly relevant.
- **Event/current:** ask whether the event is genuine evidence for the mechanism or only a short observation compatible with many models.
- Resolve conflicts through [[78 Phase 01 Institutional Fundamental Foundations Canon/05 Competing Models Reflexivity and Pluralism/Model Pluralism Without Incoherence]].

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Institutional narratives can become broad and hard to falsify without measurable mechanisms and comparisons.
- Using the school as identity rather than a conditional model.
- Citing famous conclusions without reconstructing assumptions.
- Ignoring evidence that falls outside the preferred ontology.
- Combining incompatible closure rules without explaining the synthesis.

## Adversarial analytical checks and accreditation questions

1. Write the strongest version of this framework without caricature.
2. Name three observations that would weaken its application to a current episode.
3. Compare its explanation of inflation, recession and financial instability with two rivals.
4. Identify what this framework sees that a standard aggregate model may omit.
