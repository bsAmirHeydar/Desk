# Investment-Committee Reasoning, Dissent and Decision Records

> [!abstract] Canonical thesis
> An institutional committee should aggregate differentiated evidence and accountability, not average opinions or ratify hierarchy. The record must preserve claims, probabilities, dissent, conditions and later attribution.

## Analytical intuition

A meeting is useful only if it changes or clarifies the decision. Everyone saying a view is not evidence; roles, questions and records matter.

## Institutional definitions and distinctions

| Term or distinction | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| Decision record | Timestamped account of information, alternatives, reasoning, conclusion and dissent. |
| Dissent | Substantive disagreement on facts, mechanisms, probabilities or mandate implications. |
| Escalation | Rule for sending unresolved risk or evidence conflict to higher authority. |
| Recusal | Removal from decision due to conflict or insufficient independence. |
| Accountability horizon | Time and outcome window over which a conclusion is reviewed. |

## Mechanism map

1. Circulate pre-read and claim ledger.
2. Separate evidence presentation from advocacy.
3. Elicit independent priors before discussion.
4. Debate diagnostic disagreements.
5. Record conditional conclusion and dissent.
6. Schedule review at evidence and calendar triggers.

## Failure modes and red-team tests

- Meeting after the decision is socially fixed.
- No independent pre-meeting probabilities.
- Dissent without named mechanism.
- No expiry or review trigger.
- Outcome review confuses luck and process.
