# Institutional Analytical Retrieval and Synthesis Standard

## Purpose

Define how a research process retrieves the minimum sufficient canon and converts it into an institutional conclusion without reading the Vault linearly or importing irrelevant material.

## Retrieval sequence

1. Define the object, jurisdiction, instrument, cutoff and horizons.
2. Open the governing object MOC and domain coverage map.
3. Retrieve accounting, institutional, state, expectations, valuation and transmission notes.
4. Retrieve at least one serious rival-model note.
5. Retrieve source contracts and point-in-time limitations.
6. Retrieve relevant historical regimes and cases.
7. Retrieve cross-asset and portfolio interactions.
8. Build a contradiction and unknown ledger before conclusion.

## Synthesis record

Every synthesis records:

- observed facts;
- estimated states;
- model-dependent claims;
- market-implied expectations;
- valuation and payoff assumptions;
- causal transmission;
- horizon-specific conclusions;
- alternative scenarios;
- contradicting evidence;
- confidence, invalidation, expiry and unresolved unknowns.

## Failure conditions

- The answer is a domain summary rather than an object-specific analysis.
- Major evidence is cited only at the source-homepage level.
- Current and historical information sets are mixed.
- The same driver is counted repeatedly through correlated indicators.
- A market relationship is treated as invariant across regimes.
- Confidence is stated without contradiction and unknown registers.
