# Failure domains

P10 distinguishes:
- SOURCE_INTEGRITY_FAILURE
- UPSTREAM_BINDING_FAILURE
- POINT_IN_TIME_FAILURE
- DATA_ROOT_FAILURE
- P08_OPERATIONAL_WARNING
- P09_OPERATIONAL_WARNING
- EMPIRICAL_EVIDENCE_PENDING
- PROMOTION_NOT_APPROVED

A scheduler or mutable-data warning cannot silently mutate science.
