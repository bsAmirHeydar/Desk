# Missing Driver Escalation

A large and persistent Pressure/Price disagreement is evidence that the current causal model may be incomplete, stale, wrongly timed or competing with an unobserved force.

P03 therefore escalates research rather than rewriting the thesis from price.

## Escalation levels

- `NONE`
- `WATCH`
- `RESEARCH_ESCALATION`
- `DECISION_CRITICAL`

Escalation depends on:

- transmission state;
- residual magnitude where available;
- Pressure confidence;
- pathway fragmentation;
- actual-response data quality;
- persistence across frozen snapshots.

## Candidate missing drivers

Candidates may be supplied by a research process, for example:

- unidentified liquidation;
- funding/collateral stress;
- options/dealer mechanics;
- sovereign/official flow;
- cross-asset deleveraging;
- stale or incorrect macro input;
- new event/news driver.

But every candidate remains `UNCONFIRMED` unless independent evidence supports it.

## Anti-storytelling hard law

From target price alone P03 may **not** emit:

- `ABSORPTION_CONFIRMED`;
- `LIQUIDITY_GRAB_CONFIRMED`;
- `INSTITUTIONAL_ACCUMULATION_CONFIRMED`;
- `FORCED_LIQUIDATION_CONFIRMED`.

A compression or negative-transmission state may motivate those research hypotheses, but it cannot prove them.
