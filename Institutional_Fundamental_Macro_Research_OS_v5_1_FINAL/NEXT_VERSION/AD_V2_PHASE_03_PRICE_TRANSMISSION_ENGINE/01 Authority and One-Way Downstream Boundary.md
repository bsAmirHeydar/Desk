# Price Transmission Authority

P03 owns only the **diagnostic relationship between frozen causal Pressure and subsequent market response**.

## Constitutional non-identity

`Directional Pressure != Price Transmission`

A market may legitimately be:

- `BUY_HIGH Pressure + NEGATIVE_TRANSMISSION`;
- `BUY_HIGH Pressure + COMPRESSION`;
- `SELL_HIGH Pressure + ALIGNED Transmission`;
- `BUY_MEDIUM Pressure + OVER_TRANSMISSION`.

These states are not contradictions.

## Price cannot alter Pressure

P03 may read a P02 Pressure state only if P02 integrity is `PASS`. It records the upstream Pressure fingerprint and headline but never writes into, rescales, consumes, reverses or reclassifies that Pressure.

If the target moves sharply against Pressure, the valid outputs are diagnostics such as:

- transmission disagreement;
- counterfactual residual;
- unmodeled-driver risk;
- research escalation;
- timing/decision constraint recommendation.

The invalid output is:

> Target price fell, therefore Buy Pressure is weaker.

Pressure can change only in a later P02 recomputation using changed causal drivers.

## Price is allowed here

Unlike P02, P03 is specifically authorized to observe target-price response because target price is the object being diagnosed. That authorization is downstream only.
