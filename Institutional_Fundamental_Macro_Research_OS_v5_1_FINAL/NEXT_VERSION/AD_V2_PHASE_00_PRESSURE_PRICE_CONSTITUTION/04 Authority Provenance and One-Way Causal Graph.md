# Authority, Provenance and One-Way Causal Graph

## Governing graph

The V2 market-reasoning path is one-way at the decision layer:

```text
D1 facts / expectations / causal market states / verified flow
    ↓
independent scientific states
    ↓
Module 89 Directional Pressure
    ↓
expected signatures / counterfactual response
    ↓
target Price Transmission
    ↓
model disagreement / missing-driver risk / latent-state hypotheses
    ↓
D3 edge and permission modulation
    ↓
execution timing / technical trigger
    ↓
D4 forward outcome learning
```

## Controlled audit callback

There is one permitted feedback path:

```text
Price disagreement
    ↓
RESEARCH_ESCALATION
    ↓
new/corrected causal evidence
    ↓
Pressure re-estimation
```

The callback is legal only when it produces **new evidence or a reason-coded correction**. `PRICE_MOVED_AGAINST_US` is not itself a valid pressure-revision reason code.

## Separate provenance ledgers

Future V2 implementation must keep at least two logically distinct ledgers:

### Pressure provenance

Every load-bearing pressure component must identify:

- evidence/root references;
- horizon;
- sign;
- materiality;
- freshness;
- independence cluster;
- confidence/source grade;
- whether it is root, causal channel, proxy or constraint.

### Transmission provenance

Every target-price claim must identify:

- target instrument;
- price source;
- timestamp/cutoff;
- response window;
- expected-signature reference;
- observed response;
- latency/path classification;
- whether the observation is diagnostic, timing, or D4 outcome evidence.

The same price observation cannot silently appear in both ledgers.

## Authority matrix

- Module 89: Pressure / Direction authority.
- Module 103: cognitive synthesis, transmission diagnostics, model disagreement and research escalation; no Direction flip.
- Module 101: edge/permission modulation; no Direction flip.
- Module 102: empirical calibration/promotion; no retrospective rewrite of frozen state.
- Technical/execution layer: timing/entry only; no Fundamental Pressure authority.
- Target price: no Pressure authority.

## Frozen-record law

After a point-in-time record is sealed, later price outcomes may score calibration or reveal a model defect. They must never rewrite the original pressure state as if the future were known at the cutoff.
