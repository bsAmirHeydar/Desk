# Directional Pressure Authority Constitution

## Definition

**Directional Pressure** is the signed, horizon-specific causal force implied by the best available evidence about economic state, expectations, policy reaction, discount rates, currency conditions, cash-flow/earnings changes, physical balance, funding/collateral conditions, institutional constraints, actual transaction pressure, structural demand/supply and other causally justified drivers.

It is not the same object as:

- target price direction;
- chart trend;
- momentum;
- breakout state;
- support/resistance state;
- realized return;
- MFE/MAE;
- trade outcome;
- price distance since a catalyst.

## Sole pressure authority

For the Fundamental strategy, Module 89 remains the owner of Direction and becomes the intended V2 owner of the explicit Directional Pressure object. Phase 00 does not activate a new pressure schema; it fixes the authority law later phases must implement.

## Target-price exclusion law

The target instrument's own price may not be used as any of the following for Directional Pressure:

- source evidence of causal force;
- component in a pressure aggregation;
- implicit or explicit weight;
- fallback when a driver is unavailable;
- tie-breaker when causal evidence is mixed;
- reason to strengthen or weaken pressure;
- reason to declare catalyst consumption;
- reason to infer actual buyer/seller identity;
- reason to infer structural demand/supply;
- post-hoc reason to preserve or rescue a thesis.

A target-price move against the current pressure estimate is **not sufficient evidence** to change the pressure estimate.

## What may change Pressure

Pressure may change only because the causal evidence changed, for example:

- a new or revised economic fact;
- a recoverable expectation or policy-path revision;
- a change in a causal leader such as real yields or broad USD conditions when justified by the asset model;
- verified flow/positioning/funding/mechanics evidence that constitutes a causal driver rather than a price proxy;
- a regime remap supported by evidence;
- a newly identified missing driver with traceable evidence;
- correction of a source, timestamp, horizon or root-dependence error.

## Cross-asset price nuance

The prohibition is against **target-price circularity**, not against all market prices.

Cross-asset market variables such as yields, FX indices, credit spreads or commodity inputs may enter Pressure when they are justified as causal state variables or reaction-function proxies. They remain subject to:

- root/dependence controls;
- timestamp and horizon alignment;
- mechanism evidence;
- no double counting;
- distinction between root driver and downstream signature.

For Gold, DXY and real/nominal yields are not automatically roots. Their role must be declared by the active causal model.

## Pressure confidence versus pressure magnitude

Target-price disagreement may lower **model-completeness confidence** or trigger an audit. It must not mechanically lower pressure magnitude. This separation permits a valid state such as:

- `Directional Pressure = BUY_HIGH`
- `Pressure Confidence = MEDIUM`
- `Price Transmission = NEGATIVE`
- `Unmodeled Driver Risk = HIGH`

without contradiction.

## No momentum bleed-through

Technical momentum can affect timing and trade permission only under its later contract. It cannot become a hidden proxy for Fundamental Pressure.
