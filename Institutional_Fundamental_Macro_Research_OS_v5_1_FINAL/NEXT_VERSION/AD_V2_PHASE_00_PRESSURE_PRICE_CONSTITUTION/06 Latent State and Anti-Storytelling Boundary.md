# Latent State and Anti-Storytelling Boundary

## Purpose

Later V2 phases will research latent states such as Unreleased Pressure, opposing-move maturity, absorption hypotheses and release readiness. Phase 00 defines what these concepts are **not allowed to become**.

## Unreleased Pressure

`Unreleased Pressure` is reserved as a future derived state describing causal force that plausibly remains unexpressed in the target price.

Phase 00 rules:

- it is not `Pressure - PriceMove`;
- it is not `100 - price_consumption`;
- it is not inferred from drawdown alone;
- it is not a probability;
- it cannot receive production authority in Phase 00;
- any future state must carry confidence, evidence and model assumptions.

Until validated, use qualitative/ordinal language only.

## Opposing-move maturity

A large counter-pressure price move is not mature merely because it is large. Future maturity analysis must consider independent evidence such as:

- opposing-driver freshness;
- identified flow continuation/exhaustion;
- funding/collateral stress;
- positioning adjustment;
- replacement sellers/buyers;
- impact efficiency;
- event/catalyst expiry;
- market mechanics and timing.

## Hidden-state epistemic classes

Any latent explanation must be labelled as one of:

- `OBSERVED` — directly evidenced under the declared data source;
- `SUPPORTED_INFERENCE` — multiple independent observations support the mechanism;
- `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` — mechanism is viable but not sufficiently evidenced;
- `UNRESOLVED` — evidence does not distinguish material candidates.

Price shape alone cannot elevate `LIQUIDITY_SWEEP`, `ABSORPTION`, `INVENTORY_TRANSFER`, `FORCED_LIQUIDATION`, or `INSTITUTIONAL_ACCUMULATION` above `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` unless the Phase-specific evidence policy explicitly permits it.

## Anti-storytelling rule

A latent-state explanation must compete against at least one rival when it is decision-material. The system must be able to say `UNKNOWN` rather than inventing a satisfying story.
