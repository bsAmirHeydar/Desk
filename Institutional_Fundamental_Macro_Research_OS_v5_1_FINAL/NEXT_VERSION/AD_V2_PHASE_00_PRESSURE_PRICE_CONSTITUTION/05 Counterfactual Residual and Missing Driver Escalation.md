# Counterfactual Residual and Missing Driver Escalation

## Purpose

Price/Pressure divergence is valuable because it may reveal incomplete causal understanding. Phase 00 converts that observation into a disciplined research trigger rather than a momentum-based pressure override.

## Pre-registration law

Before target-price behavior is used diagnostically, the active model should state expected signatures when material, including:

- causal leader(s);
- expected direction/order of cross-asset response;
- expected target-price response band or qualitative sign when justified;
- expected latency;
- failure or ambiguity conditions.

This reduces post-hoc storytelling.

## Counterfactual residual

Later V2 phases may define a `counterfactual_residual` as the gap between the model-implied response and observed response. Until D4 calibration exists, this remains ordinal/qualitative unless the underlying estimand is explicitly defined.

Valid qualitative states may include:

- `SMALL`
- `MODERATE`
- `LARGE`
- `EXTREME`
- `UNDETERMINED`

## Escalation logic

A material residual can trigger:

1. source/timestamp audit;
2. horizon audit;
3. dependence/double-count audit;
4. causal-leader recheck;
5. positioning/flow/funding/mechanics search;
6. event/news search;
7. rival-model tournament refresh;
8. `UNMODELED_DRIVER_RISK` escalation;
9. trade constraint or `NO_TRADE`.

Only if this process discovers new evidence may Pressure be re-estimated.

## No thesis rescue

The system is forbidden from asserting:

- `THE_MARKET_IS_WRONG`;
- `LIQUIDITY_GRAB`;
- `SMART_MONEY_ACCUMULATION`;
- `INSTITUTIONAL_BUYING`;
- `ABSORPTION`;

as a causal explanation solely because Price moved opposite the current Pressure thesis.

If the driver remains unknown, the honest state is `UNEXPLAINED_DIVERGENCE` with explicit uncertainty.
