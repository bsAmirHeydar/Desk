# Gold Price Transmission and Cross-Asset Pathways

P03 owns Transmission. P05 provides Gold-specific pathway templates.

Typical pathways include:

### Policy / disinflation
`policy repricing -> 2Y / real yields -> USD -> Gold target response`

### Fiscal / credibility risk
`fiscal-risk premium -> curve / inflation-risk / USD confidence -> Gold`

### Liquidity stress
`funding/collateral stress -> USD / cross-asset liquidation -> Gold path`

### Geopolitical safe haven
`risk shock -> safe-haven demand + oil/inflation/rates counter-channel -> Gold`

### Structural investment demand
`ETF / official / physical allocation -> persistent balance pressure -> slower Gold transmission`

GC price may be used as a mechanically linked target proxy. Silver, miners, rates and USD may be cross-asset diagnostics when their independence and mechanism are stated. No number of correlated price series creates a causal Pressure root by vote count.
