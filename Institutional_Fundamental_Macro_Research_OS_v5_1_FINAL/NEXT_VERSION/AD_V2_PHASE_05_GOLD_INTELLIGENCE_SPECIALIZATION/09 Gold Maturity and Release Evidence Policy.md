# Gold Maturity and Release Evidence Policy

P05 specializes which Gold observations can legally support P04 maturity/release.

## Stronger live evidence when available

- signed CME transaction/order-flow metrics with disclosed construction;
- directly observed depth/resiliency change;
- defensible options/dealer-mechanics state with explicit model lineage;
- funding/margin/liquidity state;
- event/mechanical state after the event occurs.

## Context rather than live trigger

- CFTC COT;
- WGC Gold Demand Trends;
- monthly LBMA clearing/vault data;
- COMEX stocks/deliveries unless the active thesis specifically concerns physical/location stress;
- broad ETF/official holdings.

## Never sufficient alone

- Gold price reversal;
- GC volume spike;
- OI change without directional/participant mapping;
- support/resistance break;
- Silver move;
- DXY move;
- options OI without dealer mapping.

A P04 `RELEASE` remains subject to P04 independent non-price evidence rules. P05 only defines admissible Gold evidence.
