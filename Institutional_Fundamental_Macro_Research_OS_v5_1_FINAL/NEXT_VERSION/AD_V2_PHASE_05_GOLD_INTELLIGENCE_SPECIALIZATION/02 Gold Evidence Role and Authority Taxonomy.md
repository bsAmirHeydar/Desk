# Gold Evidence Role and Authority Taxonomy

Every admitted Gold observation must have exactly declared roles.

## Legal role families

1. `P02_ROOT_ELIGIBLE` — may become a causal root only with an explicit mechanism, horizon, polarity/magnitude interpretation and provenance.
2. `P02_ROOT_SUPPORT_ONLY` — may support a root but cannot be independently weighted.
3. `P03_TARGET_RESPONSE` — target Gold Price response only.
4. `P03_PATHWAY_CHANNEL` — causal transmission/leader diagnostic.
5. `P04_EVIDENCE` — maps to a legal non-price P04 evidence role.
6. `EVENT_RESET` — can cap release/readiness around material scheduled or unscheduled events.
7. `STRUCTURAL_CONTEXT` — slow state; cannot masquerade as live intraday flow.
8. `ACTIVITY_ONLY` — informative activity but no directional-flow interpretation.
9. `COVERAGE_GAP` — explicitly unavailable/licensed/private state.

## Hard rules

- Target XAUUSD return can only be `P03_TARGET_RESPONSE`.
- GC price is a mechanically linked price proxy, not an independent P04 confirmation.
- Volume alone cannot become `OPPOSING_FLOW_ACTIVE`, `RELEASE_SUPPORT` or any signed-flow role.
- Open interest alone cannot infer buyer/seller direction.
- CFTC COT is positioning context with weekly/lagged semantics, not live order flow.
- ETF AUM movement is not investor flow without shares/holdings/basket decomposition.
- Options OI is not dealer inventory or gamma sign without a defensible model/data mapping.
- LBMA clearing is activity, not directional client flow.
- London vault stock is inventory state, not daily flow.
