# Non-Promotion, Backward Compatibility and P06 Handoff

P05 is additive and `SHADOW_ONLY`.

It does not modify:

- V1 production files;
- P02 Pressure authority;
- P03 Transmission authority;
- P04 Release authority;
- existing broker/execution authority.

P06 may integrate the P02-P05 state objects into RUN2, Run Capsules, persistence/history and Alpha Desk UX while retaining exact provenance and shadow-vs-production authority boundaries.
