# Gold Pressure Root Registry and Dependency Graph

P05 defines **root families**, not a new aggregate formula. P02 still owns aggregation.

Canonical Gold root families include:

- `POLICY_EXPECTATIONS` — expected policy path / reaction-function repricing;
- `REAL_RATE_DISCOUNT` — real opportunity-cost regime not already double-counted inside policy expectations;
- `USD_AUTONOMOUS` — autonomous USD shock only; DXY is otherwise a dependent pathway signal;
- `STRUCTURAL_OFFICIAL_DEMAND` — reserve diversification / official-sector demand;
- `ETF_INVESTMENT_DEMAND` — physically backed ETF investment allocation;
- `PHYSICAL_REGIONAL_BALANCE` — regional physical scarcity/premium/import/recycling balance;
- `GEOPOLITICAL_CREDIBILITY_RISK` — safe-haven / monetary-fiscal credibility risk;
- `FUNDING_COLLATERAL_STRESS` — dollar funding, collateral/margin or gold financing stress when materially causal.

## Dependency law

A policy shock may transmit through 2Y, real yields and DXY. Those three observations do **not** automatically become three independent roots. The root owner and dependent signals must be disclosed.

A source can change roles by regime only through an explicit model declaration. Runtime price movement cannot promote a dependent signal into a causal root.
