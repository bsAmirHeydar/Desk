# Gold Independence, Venue Boundaries and Anti-Double-Counting

P05 declares evidence independence groups.

Examples:

- XAUUSD and GC price belong to a shared target-price complex for confirmation counting.
- two GC feeds from different vendors are not two independent market mechanisms.
- DXY and EURUSD can share a USD/FX information dependency.
- 2Y, OIS and Fed-funds futures can share policy-path information.
- ETF flow from WGC and the same underlying sponsor holdings cannot be counted twice without decomposition.
- CFTC futures positioning and CME OI are related but not identical; their roles must remain distinct.

Independence is about underlying information/mechanism, not publisher count.

P04 release-support thresholds count unique independence groups only.
