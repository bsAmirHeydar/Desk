# AD-V2-P07 — D4 Calibration, True-Forward Validation and Scientific Promotion

P07 is the empirical-governance phase of Alpha Desk V2.

P00–P06 built the constitution, semantic ownership, Directional Pressure, Price Transmission, latent/release science, Gold specialization, and run/capsule/history integration. P07 does **not** invent another market-state model. It asks whether the V2 states are genuinely useful out of sample.

## Mission
1. freeze V2 state before outcome;
2. collect true-forward outcomes without rewriting commitments;
3. separate DEVELOPMENT_CASE from TRUE_FORWARD evidence;
4. calibrate MFE, MAE, realized R, timing and state transitions;
5. test incremental value beyond simpler baselines;
6. use dependence-aware sampling and multiple-testing disclosure;
7. enforce promotion, drift, demotion and revalidation gates;
8. produce a commissioning receipt without automatically changing V1 authority.

## Non-negotiable
Historical examples, including visually compelling large-R trades, may develop hypotheses but cannot count as true-forward promotion evidence.

P07 remains SHADOW_ONLY until empirical gates mature. V1 permission remains authoritative and broker authority remains NONE.
