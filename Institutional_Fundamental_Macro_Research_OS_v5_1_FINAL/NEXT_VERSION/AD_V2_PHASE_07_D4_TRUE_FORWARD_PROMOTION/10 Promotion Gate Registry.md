# Promotion gates

P07 uses staged promotion classes.

## RESEARCH_VALIDATED
The V2 state taxonomy has enough mature true-forward evidence to be considered empirically informative.

## MAINLINE_PRESENTATION_CANDIDATE
V2 may become the default research presentation layer if incremental value, calibration stability, coverage and true-forward floors pass independent review.

## DECISION_SUPPORT_CANDIDATE
V2 states may be considered for risk/permission modulation only after materially stronger evidence and explicit D4 governance approval.

## PERMISSION_MODULATION_CANDIDATE
Highest bar. P07 never auto-creates positive permission and never flips Fundamental Direction.

Passing a numeric gate yields `ELIGIBLE_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`, not automatic promotion.
