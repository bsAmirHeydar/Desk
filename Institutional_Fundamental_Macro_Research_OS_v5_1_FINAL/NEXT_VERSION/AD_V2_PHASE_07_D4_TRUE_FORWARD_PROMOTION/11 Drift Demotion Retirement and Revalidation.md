# Drift and demotion

Any promoted V2 science remains revocable.

Monitor:
- coverage degradation;
- state-frequency drift;
- regime concentration;
- worsening MFE/MAE asymmetry;
- release false-positive rate;
- tail harm;
- source/ontology changes.

Material deterioration creates a Drift Alert and can force demotion to SHADOW_ONLY pending revalidation.
