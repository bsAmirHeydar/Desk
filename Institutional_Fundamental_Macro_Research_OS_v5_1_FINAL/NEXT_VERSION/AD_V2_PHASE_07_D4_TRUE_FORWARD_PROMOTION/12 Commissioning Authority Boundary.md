# Commissioning boundary

P07 can produce a **Commissioning Eligibility Receipt**. It does not silently edit V1 production runtime.

Until a future operator-approved commissioning action consumes a fully validated receipt:
- V1 production remains authoritative;
- P02–P06 remain V2 scientific state owners;
- V1 trade permission is inherited;
- no broker write is allowed.

Even a successful P07 validation may promote presentation/research support before any permission authority is considered.
