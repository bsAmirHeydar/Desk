# Baselines and ablation

P07 does not validate a complex stack merely because its absolute results look good.

Required comparisons include:
1. V1 inherited permission context;
2. P02 Pressure-only state;
3. P02 + P03 transmission;
4. full P02–P05 latent/release state.

The key question is **incremental value**. P04/P05 states should improve decision-relevant outcome characteristics beyond Pressure alone, otherwise their promotion case is weak.

No baseline is allowed to mutate Pressure or become new authority merely because it wins a historical comparison.
