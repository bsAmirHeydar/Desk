# Gold-specific validation questions

Gold validation must preserve P05 venue boundaries.

Examples:
- Does BUY_HIGH + NEGATIVE_TRANSMISSION + high latent reserve precede favorable Gold excursion more often than Pressure-only states?
- Does GC listed-flow decay add incremental information without treating GC as the whole Gold market?
- Does Event Reset correctly cap pre-event release readiness?
- Does lagged CFTC improve multi-day cohorts but fail to improve intraday timing as expected?
- Are OTC observability gaps associated with higher false-release rates?

No single venue may be generalized into global Gold flow without explicit evidence.
