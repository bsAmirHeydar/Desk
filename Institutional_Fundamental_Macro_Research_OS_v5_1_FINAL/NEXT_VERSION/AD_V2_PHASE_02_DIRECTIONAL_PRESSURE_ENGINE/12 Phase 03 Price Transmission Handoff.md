# Phase 03 Price Transmission Handoff

P03 may build the Price Transmission science only after P02 Pressure passes.

P03 must consume P02 Pressure as immutable upstream causal state.

P03 may add:

- expected price/cross-asset response signatures;
- actual-vs-expected response residuals;
- aligned/delayed/negative/compressed/over-transmission states;
- transmission efficiency;
- missing-driver escalation triggered by pressure/price disagreement.

P03 must not mutate P02 Pressure merely because target price moved against it.

Unreleased Pressure, Opposing-Move Maturity and Release Readiness remain reserved for the later latent/release phase after P03 establishes the transmission object.
