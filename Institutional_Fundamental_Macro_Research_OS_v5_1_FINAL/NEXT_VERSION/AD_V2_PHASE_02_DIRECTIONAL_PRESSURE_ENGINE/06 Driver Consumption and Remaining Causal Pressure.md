# Driver Consumption and Remaining Causal Pressure

P02 carries two Module 89 objects forward without conflating them with price behavior.

## Driver Consumption

Fundamental Consumption belongs to Module 89's causal lifecycle. It can change through information incorporation, expectations repricing, verified flow propagation, expiry, reinforcement and other evidence-based mechanisms.

Target-price distance alone cannot change it.

## Remaining Causal Pressure

Remaining Causal Pressure is Module 89 Remaining Fundamental Pressure expressed in the P02 pressure namespace.

It is **not**:

- `100 - consumption`;
- Remaining Asymmetry;
- Unreleased Pressure;
- expected future return.

P02 does not create Unreleased Pressure. That concept is deliberately reserved for a later latent-state phase after Price Transmission exists.
