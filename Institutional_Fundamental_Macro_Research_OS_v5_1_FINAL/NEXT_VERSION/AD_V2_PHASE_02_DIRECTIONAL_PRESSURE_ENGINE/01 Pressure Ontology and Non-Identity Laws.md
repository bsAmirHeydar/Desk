# Directional Pressure Ontology

Directional Pressure is a horizon-specific causal state describing the signed force acting on an asset from independently justified economic, expectations, policy, discount-rate, currency, physical-balance, funding, institutional, flow and structural drivers.

## Pressure is not

- target price direction;
- chart trend or momentum;
- breakout/support/resistance state;
- realized return;
- trade outcome;
- MFE/MAE;
- Remaining Asymmetry;
- Price Consumption;
- Price Transmission;
- Unreleased Pressure.

## Core Pressure tuple

P02 emits:

`{sign, magnitude_range, magnitude_class, trend, acceleration, persistence, freshness, driver_consumption, remaining_causal_pressure, contradiction_load, confidence, provenance}`

Each member is separately governed. A change in one does not silently imply a change in another.

## Valid examples

- `BUY_HIGH` + `Pressure Trend=RISING` + `Remaining Causal Pressure=LOW`.
- `BUY_HIGH` + `Driver Consumption=MATURE_REPRICING`.
- `BUY_HIGH` + low confidence because an important root is unavailable.

These are not contradictions.
