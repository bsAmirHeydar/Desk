# Non-Promotion, Backward Compatibility and Rollback

P02 is additive under `NEXT_VERSION` only.

- V1 remains closed.
- P00/P01 remain unchanged.
- no production R2/R3/R4 decision authority changes;
- no trade Permission changes;
- no broker authority changes;
- no live report field is silently replaced.

Legacy Module 89 science can be wrapped into the P02 vocabulary, while explicit-root mode remains a shadow research candidate.

Rollback removes P02 only when installed files still match patch hashes. It never removes P00/P01 or mutable research history.
