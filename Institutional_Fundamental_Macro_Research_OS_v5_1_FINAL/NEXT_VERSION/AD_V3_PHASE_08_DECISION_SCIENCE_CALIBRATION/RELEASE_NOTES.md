# AD-V3-P08 Release Notes

P08 replaces coarse count-dependent decision interpretation with a downstream, decomposable calibration layer. P03 remains the causal authority. P08 distinguishes magnitude, horizon authority, freshness, quality, persistence, contradiction, breadth and fragility; it then governs edge and permission. Historical information value is episode-based, overlap-controlled, shrunk toward neutral for small samples, and explicitly not prospective validation. No production promotion or broker execution capability is introduced.
