# R01 Release Notes

- Fixes the V3 certification flaw where P09 sample maturity could satisfy the sole forward production gate without proving decision quality.
- Adds ex-ante frozen quality, coverage and critical-subgroup policies.
- Preserves all existing P09 predictions/outcomes/episodes as immutable observational evidence.

- Does not modify P08 decision thresholds and does not promote V3.
