# AD-V3.1-R01 — Forward Quality & Promotion Science

R01 is a versioned qualification amendment to the certified V3 release. It does not change Gold causal science or P08 decisions. It separates sample maturity from demonstrated prospective quality and production eligibility.

Core rule: enough episodes are necessary, never sufficient. Production additionally requires forward quality, coverage, absence of critical subgroup failure, and explicit operator approval.
