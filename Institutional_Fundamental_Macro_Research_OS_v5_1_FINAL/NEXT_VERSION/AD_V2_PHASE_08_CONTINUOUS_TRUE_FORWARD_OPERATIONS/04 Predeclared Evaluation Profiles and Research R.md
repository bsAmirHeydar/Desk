# Predeclared evaluation profiles

P07 uses MFE/MAE in R units. P08 refuses to invent a risk unit after seeing the outcome.

An evaluation profile must be sealed **before** the P07 commitment it scores. It declares:
- subject and horizon;
- maturity window;
- one-R price distance;
- reference-price capture window;
- maximum observation gap;
- whether the profile is counterfactual research or realized execution.

No active profile means the true-forward commitment is still valid, but automatic R-scoring is disabled. P08 may preserve raw maturity evidence, while P07 promotion remains pending. A profile created after a commitment cannot retroactively score it.
