# REV 3.3.3 — Horizon Eligibility and Flow Semantics

## Live-discovered defects
1. A monthly SGE withdrawal level entered the SESSION_1_6H transaction plane even though the fact contract did not activate it at session horizon.
2. The generic flow rule treated any positive scalar as bullish, conflating gross activity/levels with signed net directional flow.

## Rules
- Every reasoning row records `horizon_active` and its `active_horizons`.
- CAUSAL_FUNDAMENTAL, REALIZED_TRANSACTION and MECHANICAL_FORCED cannot contribute to the current horizon when inactive.
- STRUCTURAL_CARRY remains a separate slow background plane and never gains session-direction authority.
- Signed flow requires an explicit signed field, or a declared change in an observed level.
- Gross positive activity is context only unless directional semantics are explicitly available.
- SGE withdrawals, China imports, India imports and holdings levels use change-versus-prior-reference-period semantics, not absolute-positive semantics.
