# REV 3.3.4 — Semantic Evidence Packet

P03 semantic adjudication must be grounded in the exact observations consumed by the causal brain.

The packet exports, for every requested semantic fact:
- exact fact_id and observation_id;
- current and previous observation;
- source, epistemic state, directness, reference period, timestamps, provenance, warnings;
- reasoning role, pressure plane, causal root family;
- current horizon eligibility and authority scope;
- whether the fact may add to current causal Direction.

The packet is evidence only. It never grants Direction or trade permission. Structural carry remains structural background and cannot be promoted into current-horizon causal Direction merely through semantic interpretation.
