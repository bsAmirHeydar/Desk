# Authority Graph

The legal graph is one-way:

`P02 CAUSAL PRESSURE -> P03 PRICE TRANSMISSION -> P04 LATENT / MATURITY / RELEASE DIAGNOSTICS`

Independent P04 evidence may challenge completeness of the causal model. If it identifies a new causal driver, P04 must request a **new P02 computation**. It may not silently modify the existing P02 Pressure state.

## Hard non-identities

- `Unreleased Pressure != Pressure - Price Move`
- `Unreleased Pressure != Remaining Causal Pressure`
- `Opposing-Move Maturity != Price Distance`
- `Opposing-Move Maturity != Time Elapsed`
- `Release Readiness != Momentum`
- `Release != Price Reversal`
- `Latent Causal Reserve != Expected Return`
- `High Release Readiness != Trade Permission`
- `Absorption Hypothesis != Confirmed Absorption`

Price distance and candle direction are never direct P04 independent-evidence inputs. Their valid summary arrives through the P03 Transmission object.
