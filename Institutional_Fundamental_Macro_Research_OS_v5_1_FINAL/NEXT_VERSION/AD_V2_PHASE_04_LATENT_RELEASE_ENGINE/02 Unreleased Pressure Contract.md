# Unreleased Pressure

Unreleased Pressure estimates the **remaining causal capacity that has not yet been expressed through expected target-price transmission**.

It is ordinal, not probabilistic and not a forecast of dollars or R.

## Required ingredients

A high Unreleased state requires a conjunction of evidence, including:

1. directional P02 Pressure of material magnitude;
2. meaningful Remaining Causal Pressure;
3. non-trivial Persistence;
4. driver consumption that is not already exhausted;
5. P03 evidence that expected transmission is delayed, compressed, under-transmitted or negative;
6. no unresolved new causal driver that should first be incorporated into P02.

Independent evidence that original pressure roots remain persistent can strengthen confidence. It does not overwrite P02.

## Maximum pre-calibration class

P04 intentionally caps the scientific vocabulary at `VERY_HIGH`. `EXTREME` is reserved until D4/true-forward calibration demonstrates that such a category has stable empirical meaning.
