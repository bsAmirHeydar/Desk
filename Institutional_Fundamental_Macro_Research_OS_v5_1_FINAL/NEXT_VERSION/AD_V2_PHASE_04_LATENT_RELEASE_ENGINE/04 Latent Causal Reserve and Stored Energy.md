# Latent Causal Reserve

P04 uses the phrase **Latent Causal Reserve** as the formal object behind the trader-language concept of "stored energy".

It is not physical energy, price momentum, volatility or expected return.

A high reserve means:

- the upstream Pressure remains material and persistent;
- substantial causal pressure remains;
- transmission has not fully expressed the expected effect;
- the opposing move has evidence of maturity rather than a newly strengthening independent cause.

Vocabulary:

`LOW`, `BUILDING`, `ELEVATED`, `HIGH`, `VERY_HIGH`, `UNDETERMINED`.

The object must always disclose the evidence and caps that produced it.
