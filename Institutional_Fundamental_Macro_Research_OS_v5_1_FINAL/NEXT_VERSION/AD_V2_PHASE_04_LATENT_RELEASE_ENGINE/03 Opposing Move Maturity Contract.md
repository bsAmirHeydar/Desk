# Opposing-Move Maturity

The purpose of maturity is not to ask whether Price has moved "far enough". It asks whether the **mechanisms sponsoring the move against P02 Pressure** are forming, active, decaying, or exhausted.

## Evidence domains

P04 accepts independent evidence from:

- flow;
- positioning;
- funding/liquidity;
- mechanics;
- options/dealer state;
- physical balance;
- event state;
- causal cross-asset state;
- structural state.

## Typical roles

Active opposition:
- fresh opposing causal driver;
- opposing flow active;
- positioning unwind active;
- funding headwind active;
- mechanical headwind active;
- replacement opposing driver.

Maturity/exhaustion:
- opposing flow decaying;
- positioning unwind exhausting;
- funding headwind easing;
- mechanical headwind easing.

Price alone cannot promote maturity. Time alone cannot promote maturity.

## States

`UNRESOLVED`, `FORMING`, `ACTIVE`, `MATURE`, `EXHAUSTING`, `EXHAUSTED`.

`EXHAUSTED` requires multiple independent non-price evidence groups and no fresh replacement driver.
