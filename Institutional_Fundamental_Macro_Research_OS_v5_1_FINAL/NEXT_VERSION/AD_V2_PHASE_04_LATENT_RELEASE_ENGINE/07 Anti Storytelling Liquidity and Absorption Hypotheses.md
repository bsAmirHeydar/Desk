# Anti-Storytelling

P04 may maintain hypotheses such as:

- absorption;
- liquidity sweep;
- positioning liquidation;
- dealer inventory transfer;
- mechanical suppression;
- delayed transmission.

But target price action alone can never promote any of them.

## Hypothesis status

P04 deliberately caps liquidity/absorption language at:

`UNSUPPORTED`, `UNCONFIRMED`, `SUPPORTED`, `STRONGLY_SUPPORTED`.

There is no `CONFIRMED` state in P04.

`SUPPORTED` requires at least two independent non-price groups carrying specific hypothesis evidence. This is support for further research, not participant-level proof.
