# Shadow challengers

P09 can register immutable challenger specifications. A challenger is a **proposal**, not an installed science rule.

Allowed challenger families include pressure-root research, expected-signature review, latent threshold review, maturity-rule review, release-rule review, evidence-role review, missing-driver search, event-reset guard and data-quality guard.

Forbidden challenger content includes target-price-to-pressure influence, weakening anti-storytelling, permission creation, broker writes, future-data fitting and removal of independence controls.
