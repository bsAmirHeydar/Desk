# Failure taxonomy

P09 recognizes hypothesis families including:
- `PRESSURE_MODEL_MISS`
- `TRANSMISSION_EXPECTATION_MISS`
- `LATENT_PRESSURE_OVERSTATEMENT`
- `OPPOSING_MATURITY_PREMATURE`
- `RELEASE_FALSE_POSITIVE`
- `MISSING_DRIVER_UNRESOLVED`
- `EVENT_RESET_GUARD_MISS`
- `DATA_QUALITY_OR_COVERAGE`
- `EVALUATION_PROFILE_MISMATCH`
- `NO_CLEAR_MODEL_FAILURE`

These are diagnostic hypotheses, not facts. Each family has a scientific owner and an allowed evolution path.
