# AD-V2-P09 — Scientific Learning, Failure Attribution & Model Evolution Governance

P09 closes the scientific learning loop without creating online-learning authority.

## Mission
1. convert mature P07/P08 outcomes into immutable learning cases;
2. distinguish outcome disappointment from proven model error;
3. classify recurring failure hypotheses by scientific owner (P02/P03/P04/P05/P06/P08/data);
4. aggregate only dependence-aware independent episodes;
5. prevent a single dramatic winner or loser from rewriting the model;
6. generate research hypotheses and shadow challenger proposals only after recurrence gates;
7. freeze candidate/experiment lineage before any new validation data;
8. prohibit automatic mutation of P02–P08 science, V1 permission, or broker authority;
9. create an auditable research queue and model-evolution history.

P09 is a learning/governance layer. It does not change live science by itself.
