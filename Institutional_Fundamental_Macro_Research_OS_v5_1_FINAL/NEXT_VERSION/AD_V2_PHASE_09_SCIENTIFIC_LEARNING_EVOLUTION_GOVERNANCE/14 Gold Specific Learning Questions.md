# Gold-specific learning questions

P09 explicitly tracks whether Gold errors cluster around:
- policy/real-yield/USD root ownership;
- negative transmission with unresolved listed/OTC flow;
- overstatement of Unreleased Pressure;
- premature opposing-move maturity;
- false release/readiness around macro events, auctions or COMEX mechanics;
- weak physical-demand observability;
- options/dealer model gaps;
- evaluation profiles inconsistent with the user's actual technical risk unit.

These are research questions, not predeclared answers.
