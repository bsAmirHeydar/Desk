# Promotion boundary

P09 does not promote challengers. It creates the evidence lineage required for a future challenger-validation/promotion phase **only if** evidence justifies one.

Expected state after P09:
- V2 incumbent science unchanged;
- continuous true-forward collection active through P08;
- learning cases and research queue active;
- recurring failure patterns auditable;
- challenger proposals immutable and non-authoritative;
- no automatic model mutation, permission change or broker write.
