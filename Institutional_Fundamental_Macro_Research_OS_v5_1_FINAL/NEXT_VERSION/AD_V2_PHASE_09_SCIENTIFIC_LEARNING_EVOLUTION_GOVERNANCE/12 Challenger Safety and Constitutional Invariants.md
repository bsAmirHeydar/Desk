# Challenger safety

All challenger specifications inherit P00 constitutional invariants:
- Pressure remains independent of target-price action;
- Price is downstream transmission evidence;
- no target candle/breakout/momentum may become Pressure authority;
- liquidity/absorption remains unconfirmed without independent evidence;
- missing data remains missing;
- no positive permission or broker write is allowed.

A challenger attempting to weaken these invariants is rejected before experiment creation.
