# 01 — Scientific Constitution and V2 Freeze

## Why V2 is frozen
V3 must be testable against a stable baseline. P01 therefore adds a new shadow science surface instead of editing V2 Direction, Pressure, Transmission, latent-release, launcher, Control Room or production manifests.

`baseline/v2_frozen_surface_manifest.json` binds the current V2 surfaces by SHA-256. P01 acceptance fails if those files change.

## Four pressure planes
V3 separates:

1. **Causal / Fundamental Pressure** — independent incentives and expectations.
2. **Realized Transaction Pressure** — observed transfer/flow evidence.
3. **Mechanical / Forced Pressure** — liquidation, hedging, roll, expiry, rebalance, margin.
4. **Structural Carry** — slow official/supply/credibility state.

Positioning, funding, vulnerability and price transmission are distinct state objects rather than forced into one directional score.

## Price boundary
Price is not ignored. It is used to test transmission and model completeness. A price move contrary to causal expectations increases `MISSING_DRIVER_RISK` or reduces `MODEL_COMPLETENESS`; it does not directly weaken or reverse causal Pressure.
