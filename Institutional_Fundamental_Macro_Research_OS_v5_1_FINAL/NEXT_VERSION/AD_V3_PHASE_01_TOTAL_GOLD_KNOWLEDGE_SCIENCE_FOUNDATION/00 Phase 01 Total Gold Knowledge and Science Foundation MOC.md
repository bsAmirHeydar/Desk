## Mission
P01 does not try to predict Gold and does not fetch live data. It defines, exhaustively and mechanically, **what the V3 Gold brain is allowed/required to know** before P02 builds live acquisition.

P01 turns dispersed Vault knowledge into one canonical machine-readable Gold fact universe, while strengthening the scientific constitution:

1. `Pressure ≠ Price` remains constitutional.
2. One scalar pressure score is replaced conceptually by four distinct pressure planes.
3. Target price is a downstream response and model diagnostic, never a causal root.
4. Root independence becomes **shock ownership + conditional dependence**.
5. Missing data are not renormalized away.
6. Freshness and economic persistence are different objects.
7. Consumption becomes a vector/state machine, not a subjective percent.
8. `Stored Energy` is deprecated in favor of the stricter `Unexpressed Causal Imbalance`, only after a missing-driver search.
9. Uncalibrated 0–100 user-facing precision is forbidden.
10. Private/paid gaps are registered explicitly instead of being hidden.
