# 03 — Gold Master Fact Registry

`gold_master_fact_registry.json` is the P01 machine-readable inventory of the known Gold fact universe.

Current registered facts: **192**.

The registry intentionally includes both observable and non-observable facts. A known private gap is more scientific than silently pretending the fact does not exist.

Major families include:

- US macro, Fed, policy pricing and event surprise;
- nominal yields, real yields, breakevens, term premium and Treasury supply;
- broad USD complex and CNH;
- fiscal/sovereign/monetary credibility;
- global monetary/liquidity context;
- official-sector demand and reserve diversification;
- ETF holdings/flows/creations-redemptions;
- all major CFTC participant classes;
- futures activity, curve, roll and delivery mechanics;
- options IV, skew, OI topology, expiry and dealer-inference boundary;
- COMEX registered/eligible stocks and deliveries;
- London vault, clearing, OTC activity, lease/forward/EFP gaps;
- China physical subsystem;
- India physical subsystem;
- jewellery, bar/coin and technology demand;
- mine production, recycling, producer hedging and disruptions;
- USD funding, repo, dealers and cross-currency funding;
- geopolitics, sanctions, oil/inflation and forced liquidation;
- CTA/vol-control/rebalancing/margin mechanics;
- narrative, attention and cross-asset diagnostics;
- timing/session/fixing/expiry/event clocks;
- explicit private/paid facts and model-completeness state.

P02 must bind every applicable fact to actual source/acquisition contracts. P01 source hints are **not** evidence of live coverage.

The registry is role-governed through `config/fact_role_registry.json`; each fact role has explicit pressure-plane compatibility and decision rights. Duplicate horizons/terms are invalid. Structural-root observations may condition a matching causal root only through the explicit structural-to-causal bridge and never by being relabeled as intraday flow.
