# 02 — Causal Ownership, Dependency and Anti-Double-Counting

The V2 idea of independent roots is retained but hardened. V3 does not treat markets as a flat weighted ballot.

Canonical graph:

`Shock → Causal Root → Transmission → Participant Response → Realized Flow → Mechanics → Price`

A CPI surprise can change policy pricing, 2Y yields, real yields and USD. Those downstream states may corroborate transmission, but they are not four new independent versions of the same shock.

The same rule applies in reverse: a fiscal/term-premium shock can raise nominal yields while supporting Gold through credibility risk. V3 must identify the **shock owner** before interpreting a yield move.

Conditional dependence is regime-specific. Two roots may be independently definable but become dependent during a particular crisis; P03 will have to preserve that dependence rather than counting static labels.
