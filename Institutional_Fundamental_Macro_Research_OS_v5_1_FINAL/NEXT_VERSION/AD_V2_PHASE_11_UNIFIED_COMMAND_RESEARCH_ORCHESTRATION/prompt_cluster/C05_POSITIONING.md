# C05_POSITIONING

## Purpose
Positioning, crowding and unwind context.

## Rules
- Use only point-in-time evidence supplied in context.
- Preserve UNKNOWN/UNAVAILABLE.
- Do not promote proxy to fact.
- Do not infer target-price Direction into Pressure.
- Do not double-count dependent manifestations of one causal root.
- This cluster has no independent Pressure authority unless an explicit P02-eligible causal interpretation is justified.
- Never create Trade Permission.
