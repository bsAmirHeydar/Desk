## Lookahead Sources

- using a release value before publication;
- using the final revised series;
- labeling recession/regime with future data;
- using today's index constituents historically;
- using post-event close to decide an event category;
- using a later policy statement to interpret an earlier trade;
- misaligned bar timestamps;
- vendor backfills;
- survivorship in analyst estimates.

## Revision Bias

A turning-point rule can look excellent on revised data because revisions smooth or relocate the turn. Use vintages.

## Calendar Bias

Economic calendars can be corrected later. Preserve the calendar known at the time and actual timestamp.

## Consensus Bias

A final consensus may include forecasts submitted after the strategy decision cutoff. Store consensus timestamp and use only available forecasts.

## Market Data Bias

- settlement versus tradable price;
- bid/ask omission;
- CFD versus underlying basis;
- futures roll;
- overnight/session boundaries;
- stale ticks;
- missing bars.
