## Core doctrine

```text
Observation
→ Measurement and vintage control
→ State distribution
→ Expectations and priced distribution
→ Causal and rival models
→ Scenario/payoff distribution
→ Portfolio-aware permission
→ Technical execution handoff
→ Attribution, calibration and retirement
```

The Vault separates economic truth from market expectations, expectations from asset payoff, and asset payoff from executable trade. Fundamentals restrict the allowed trade set; technical structure retains authority over entry, structural stop and target.

## Operating boundary

The Vault is a research architecture, curriculum, model library and operating standard. It does not contain live licensed market data, a current consensus feed, dealer inventory, or automatically proven alpha. Institutional capability emerges only after the point-in-time laboratory, live data, model implementation, execution stack and promotion gates are deployed.
