# Residual Frontiers and Research Agenda

V12 completes the analytical architecture but leaves empirical frontiers:

- point-in-time institutional text and attention datasets;
- direct portfolio, prime-broker and real-money adoption evidence;
- calibrated narrative-attention shares;
- validated transition-hazard models;
- regime-stable cross-asset narrative graphs;
- multilingual text timing and entity resolution;
- human-labelled frozen narrative packets with reliable agreement;
- empirical separation of attention, flow and causal control.

These limitations require confidence caps, not invented precision. The next research phase should collect contemporaneous packets, run double-blind annotation and test ordinal states with purged walk-forward evaluation.
