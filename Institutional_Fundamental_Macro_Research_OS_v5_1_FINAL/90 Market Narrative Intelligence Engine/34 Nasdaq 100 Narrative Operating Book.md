# Nasdaq 100 Narrative Operating Book

## Narrative families

Maintain candidates for Fed path/real yields, AI monetization, AI capex return, semiconductor cycle, mega-cap revisions, concentration/passive flow, soft landing, growth scare, dollar liquidity, fiscal term premium, regulation, dealer/systematic flow, valuation compression and productivity regime.

## Leader map

Rates narratives should identify front-end rates, real yields and duration valuation. AI narratives should identify earnings revisions, guidance, capex efficiency, semiconductor demand and concentration breadth. Flow narratives must be separated from fact-led earnings or policy narratives.

## Typical conflicts

AI monetization may dominate multi-day while real yields dominate the session. A mega-cap beat can reinforce the headline but weaken validity if forward cash-flow revisions or capex returns disappoint. High attention to AI with saturated positioning creates a different state from persistent earnings improvement with low attention.

## Daily outputs

Report the dominant narrative by horizon, strongest challenger, ignored persistent fact, rate/earnings causal leader, concentration dependence, narrative saturation, replacement trigger and first market expected to confirm.
