# Unscheduled Shock and Narrative Hijack Protocol

## Timestamp priority

For social posts, geopolitical headlines, sanctions, tariffs, emergency policy actions or corporate leaks, locate the earliest verified public timestamp and state timestamp confidence.

## Hijack versus replacement

An unscheduled shock can seize tactical attention without replacing the structural narrative. Use `FLOW_OR_HEADLINE_HIJACK` when immediate price control changes but fact validity, persistence or broader adoption remain uncertain.

## Verification sequence

Record source reliability, official confirmation, affected channels, first causal leader, cross-asset coherence, contradiction, expected expiry and conditions for escalation into a true narrative takeover.
