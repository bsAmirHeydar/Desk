# Narrative Analyst Worksheet and Ledger

## Worksheet sequence

1. Freeze timestamp, cutoff and prior state.
2. List facts and V11 state fields.
3. Mark attention evidence by asset/horizon.
4. Generate candidates and rivals.
5. Complete validity and dominance independently.
6. Identify leaders and cross-asset sequence.
7. Assess fact and narrative persistence separately.
8. Assess narrative consumption, remaining power, fragility and replacement risk.
9. Surface ignored facts and discovery catalysts.
10. Record confidence caps and next update triggers.

## Required ledgers

- fact ledger;
- attention evidence ledger;
- narrative candidate ledger;
- leader-change ledger;
- transition ledger;
- ignored-fact ledger;
- provenance and unavailable-input ledger.

Analyst notes must distinguish observation, deterministic derivation, market implication, model inference, proxy and structured judgment.
