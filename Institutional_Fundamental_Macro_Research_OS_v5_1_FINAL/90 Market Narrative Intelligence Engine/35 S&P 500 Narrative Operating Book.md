# S&P 500 Narrative Operating Book

## Narrative families

Track broad earnings growth, margin resilience, soft landing, recession risk, sector breadth, fiscal impulse, consumer resilience, labor cooling, credit stability, buybacks, concentration versus equal weight, term premium, inflation/policy path, systematic flow and valuation/risk premium.

## Breadth discipline

An index rally led by a few mega-caps does not establish a broad soft-landing narrative. Compare equal weight, cyclicals/defensives, credit, banks, small caps, earnings revisions and margin guidance.

## Control conflicts

Systematic flows may control the session while broad earnings revisions control multi-day expectations. Credit deterioration can be an ignored fact even when index attention remains on rate cuts.

## Daily outputs

Name whether the dominant interpretation is cash-flow, policy, valuation or flow-led; identify breadth, strongest opposing sector evidence, persistence, saturation and the event capable of transferring attention.
