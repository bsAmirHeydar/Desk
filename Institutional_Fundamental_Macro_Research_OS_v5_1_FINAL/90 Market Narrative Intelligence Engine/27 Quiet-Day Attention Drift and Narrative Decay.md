# Quiet-Day Attention Drift and Narrative Decay

## No-headline state change

Attention can drift on quiet days because prior information is absorbed, positions rebalance, liquidity changes, the next catalyst approaches or rival narratives gain explanatory power.

## Mandatory reassessment

At scheduled checkpoints assess whether awareness, sensitivity, institutional adoption, narrative control, saturation, remaining power and transition risk changed even without a new headline.

Do not apply fixed time decay. A persistent narrative may remain stable; a fragile narrative may lose control rapidly. Record `NO_MATERIAL_NARRATIVE_CHANGE` when evidence is insufficient rather than inventing movement.
