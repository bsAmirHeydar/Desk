# Narrative Consumption Remaining Power and Exhaustion

## Separate consumption object

Narrative consumption measures adoption and expression, not the economic consumption of the underlying fact. Evaluate awareness saturation, belief adoption, positioning completion, valuation embedding, policy embedding, media saturation, sensitivity decay, cross-asset transmission and flow exhaustion.

## Remaining narrative power

Do not compute `100 - consumption`. Decompose remaining power into undiscovered audience, unresolved fact support, unfinished institutional adoption, unfinished positioning, cross-asset lag, reinforcement potential, policy-response potential, reflexive continuation and remaining explanatory control.

## Canonical states

Support dominant-but-consumed, dominant-and-unconsumed, weak-but-emerging, valid-but-ignored, invalid-but-reflexively-dominant and saturated-but-structurally-persistent states.
