# Gold Narrative Operating Book

## Distinct narrative complexes

Separate real-yield, dollar, central-bank buying, reserve diversification, fiscal/monetary credibility, geopolitical safe haven, sanctions/reserve risk, liquidity liquidation, ETF/futures flow, physical demand, inflation hedge and policy-easing narratives.

## Horizon separation

Official-sector and reserve-diversification facts can be persistent but low-salience intraday. Real yields or dollar liquidity can control the session. A geopolitical headline can dominate attention briefly while physical or official demand anchors the structural state.

## Failure states

Safe-haven narratives fail tactically when dollar funding stress forces liquidation. Central-bank narratives should not be inferred from price alone. ETF and futures flow do not substitute for physical-market evidence.

## Daily outputs

Report macro, physical, official-sector and headline narrative ranks separately; identify the price controller, fact persistence, liquidity risk, narrative saturation and what would cause safe-haven-to-liquidation transition.
