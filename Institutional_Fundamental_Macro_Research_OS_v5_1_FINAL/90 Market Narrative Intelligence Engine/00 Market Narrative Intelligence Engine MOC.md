# Market Attention Narrative Selection Reflexivity and Daily Intelligence Engine

## Mission

Module 90 is the canonical V12 control layer for identifying what the market is paying attention to, how selected facts become narratives, which narratives compete for control, whether a dominant narrative is valid, how reflexive loops amplify or destroy it, and how the result becomes a point-in-time daily intelligence state.

It extends—never replaces—[[89 Fundamental Force Consumption Persistence and Asymmetry Calibration Engine/00 Fundamental Force Consumption Persistence and Asymmetry Calibration Engine MOC]]. Module 89 remains authoritative for fact force, fact persistence, fact consumption, remaining fundamental pressure, reversal hazard and fundamental asymmetry. Module 90 is authoritative for attention, narrative objects, narrative validity, narrative dominance, narrative persistence, ignored facts, reflexivity and narrative transitions.

## Operating doctrine

```text
Fact universe
→ V11 fact-state vector
→ market-attention selection
→ narrative-candidate generation
→ competing-narrative tournament
→ dominance/validity separation
→ price-flow-reflexivity mapping
→ persistence/consumption/transition
→ daily asset-and-horizon state
```

Non-negotiable separations:

- fact strength is not narrative strength;
- fact persistence is not narrative persistence;
- attention is not causality;
- dominance is not validity;
- media leadership is not institutional adoption;
- price control is not proof of truth;
- narrative consumption is not fact consumption;
- one asset or horizon does not inherit another's narrative automatically.
