# EURUSD Narrative Operating Book

## Narrative families

Track Fed–ECB divergence, relative growth, relative inflation, front-end differential, dollar funding, safe-haven dollar, euro-area fiscal/political risk, energy terms of trade, balance of payments, repatriation, month/quarter-end flow, fixing flow, global risk and policy credibility.

## Mechanical controls

DXY is mechanically related and is not independent confirmation. Use US and euro rates, basis/funding, risk assets, energy and verified flow evidence to distinguish policy narratives from flow hijacks.

## Horizon conflicts

Month-end flow may dominate one session while relative policy remains the multi-day controller. Political headlines may capture media attention without changing rate expectations or capital flows.

## Daily outputs

Name the active differential, attention leader, price controller, flow controller, ignored external-balance fact, persistence, replacement trigger and first rate market expected to confirm.
