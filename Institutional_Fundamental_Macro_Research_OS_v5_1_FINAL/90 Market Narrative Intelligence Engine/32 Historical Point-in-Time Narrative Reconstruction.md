# Historical Point-in-Time Narrative Reconstruction

## Reconstruction contract

At each historical cutoff, freeze official data vintages, consensus, policy pricing, contemporaneous text, market state, options/flow proxies and the prior narrative state. Later outcomes and revised explanations are prohibited from candidate selection.

## Narrative ledger

Store first detection, source timestamp, candidate set, leader expectations, state transitions and analyst confidence. A later successful narrative cannot be backfilled as dominant if it was not observable at the cutoff.

## Missing history

When contemporaneous text or positioning is unavailable, label the state `PARTIAL_RECONSTRUCTION`, reduce confidence and avoid precise attention or adoption claims.
