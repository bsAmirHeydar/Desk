# Narrative Dominance and Price Control

## Dominance dimensions

Narrative dominance is multidimensional. Record separately whether a narrative is attention-dominant, price-dominant, flow-dominant, media-dominant, structurally dominant or tactically dominant.

## Price-control evidence

A narrative gains price-control status when confirming and contradicting information repeatedly produce asymmetric responses, the expected causal leader moves first, related sectors or assets follow coherently, options reprice in the predicted direction and rival explanations lose coverage.

One event is not enough. Control should be demonstrated across a sequence or a clearly identified high-information event.

## Fragile dominance

A narrative may control price while validity is low. Label this explicitly as reflexive or flow-supported dominance. Such a state can persist, but replacement risk rises when reinforcement depends on price alone, positioning becomes one-sided or a missing fact is likely to become salient.
