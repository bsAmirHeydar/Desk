# Narrative Fragility Contradiction and Failure

## Fragility drivers

Fragility rises when dominance depends on one evidence family, positioning is crowded, validity is weak, omitted facts are strong, causal leadership breaks, the next event is binary, liquidity is poor or the narrative requires continued price confirmation.

## Failure taxonomy

- fact contradiction;
- causal-chain break;
- expectation reset;
- policy reaction;
- flow exhaustion;
- positioning reversal;
- cross-asset confirmation break;
- rival-model takeover;
- regime transition;
- timestamp or data-quality failure.

## Output

Record contradiction load, fragility class, failure triggers, first observable warning and replacement candidate. A narrative can lose control without becoming invalid, and can become invalid while remaining temporarily dominant.
