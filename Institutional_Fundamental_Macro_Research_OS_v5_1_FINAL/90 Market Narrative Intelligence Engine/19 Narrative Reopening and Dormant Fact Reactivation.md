# Narrative Reopening and Dormant Fact Reactivation

## Reopening

A narrative is reopened when new independent evidence restores attention or expands the unresolved expectation gap. Repetition without new information is not sufficient.

Reopening sources include a second data release, policy reaction, earnings confirmation, cross-asset leadership, physical-market evidence or a failed attempt by a rival narrative to displace the thesis.

## Dormant fact reactivation

A durable fact may remain outside attention until a discovery catalyst makes it decision-relevant. Record the dormant fact, reason for neglect, trigger, expected first mover and whether the new attention reflects genuine relevance or opportunistic story formation.

## State effect

Reopening can lower narrative consumption and raise remaining narrative power, but the analyst must distinguish actual new capacity from renewed media volume.
