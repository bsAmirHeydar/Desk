# Daily Full-Market Narrative Intelligence Workflow

## Step 1 — Freeze

Record UTC and New York time, source cutoff, market cutoff, data availability, first release versus revision and prior immutable state.

## Step 2 — Build the fact universe

Collect overnight, 24-hour, inherited structural/cyclical, active multi-day, scheduled, unscheduled, earnings, policy, fiscal/geopolitical, liquidity/funding and verified flow facts.

## Step 3 — Run V11

For each material fact produce direction, force, fact persistence, consumption, remaining pressure, reversal risk and asymmetry with provenance.

## Step 4 — Run V12

Generate narrative candidates, map attention evidence, run the tournament, separate validity/dominance, identify leaders, ignored facts, reflexive loops and transition risks.

## Step 5 — Produce asset/horizon states

For Nasdaq 100, S&P 500, Gold and EURUSD report structural, cyclical, multi-day, daily, session and event states without averaging conflicts.

## Step 6 — Publish and persist

Create the executive map, fact ledger, attention map, narrative matrix, deep asset analyses, transition watch, watchlist, unknowns/confidence caps and machine-readable state. Record all Vault notes retrieved.
