# Narrative Validity and Fact Support

## Independent validity test

Validity asks whether the narrative's selected facts, expectation revision and causal mechanism are supported. It is assessed independently of dominance, popularity and profitability.

Evaluate factual accuracy, completeness, timing coherence, expectation consistency, market-implied coherence, cross-asset logic, rival-model resilience and sensitivity to omitted facts. Assign one state: `WELL_SUPPORTED`, `SUPPORTED_WITH_GAPS`, `PLAUSIBLE`, `CONTESTED`, `WEAKLY_SUPPORTED`, `REFLEXIVE_BUT_FACTUALLY_THIN`, `MISLEADING`, `INVALIDATED` or `UNDETERMINED`.

## Omission audit

Every narrative must list ignored facts and explain whether the omission is harmless, horizon-specific or fatal. A narrative that requires suppressing a strong contradictory fact should carry a high contradiction load even when it controls price.

## Update rule

Validity changes only when evidence, expectations or causal interpretation changes. A price move may motivate reassessment, but cannot by itself upgrade validity.
