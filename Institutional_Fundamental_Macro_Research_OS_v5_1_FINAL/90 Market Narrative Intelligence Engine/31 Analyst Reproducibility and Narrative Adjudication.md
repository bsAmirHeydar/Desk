# Analyst Reproducibility and Narrative Adjudication

## Double-blind protocol

Two analysts independently freeze the fact universe, generate candidates, rank attention, identify dominant narratives, score validity, surface ignored facts and predeclare transition triggers.

Compare fact-selection agreement, candidate overlap, dominant-narrative agreement, validity state, attention rank, ignored-fact overlap, transition-trigger agreement and source divergence.

## Adjudication

Separate evidence disputes from interpretation, horizon, asset and missing-data disputes. A third adjudicator may select a state only after both ledgers are preserved. Residual disagreement remains part of confidence.

Use weighted kappa or rank correlation only when assumptions fit the score type; do not create metric theatre.
