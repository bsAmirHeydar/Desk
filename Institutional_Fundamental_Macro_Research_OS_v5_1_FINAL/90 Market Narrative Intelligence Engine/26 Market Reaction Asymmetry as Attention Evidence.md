# Market Reaction Asymmetry as Attention Evidence

## Reaction test

Repeatedly compare responses to confirming and contradicting information. A market that reacts strongly to confirmation and weakly to contradiction may be attention-concentrated, position-supported or reflexively committed. The reverse can signal fatigue or narrative replacement.

## Controls

Normalize for surprise, liquidity, event timing, options state and competing catalysts. Reaction asymmetry is evidence of sensitivity, not proof of truth. One outlier event is insufficient.

## Output

Record event pair, expected reaction, realized qualitative response, causal leader, rival explanation and whether asymmetry changed attention, dominance, fragility or consumption.
