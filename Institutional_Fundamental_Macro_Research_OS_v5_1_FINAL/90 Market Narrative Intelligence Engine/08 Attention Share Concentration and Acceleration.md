# Attention Share Concentration and Acceleration

## Measurement without fake precision

V12 does not require attention estimates to sum to 100. In ordinal mode, report rank, band, concentration and acceleration. Precise shares are allowed only in an empirically calibrated mode with a declared measurement universe and validation.

## Core dimensions

- `attention_rank`: relative position among active objects;
- `attention_band`: low, medium, high or dominant;
- `attention_concentration`: fragmented, mixed, concentrated or monopolized;
- `attention_acceleration`: rising, stable, decaying or abruptly displaced;
- `attention_breadth`: number of independent institutions, markets or sectors responding;
- `competing_attention_load`: pressure from rival topics.

## Interpretation

Concentrated attention can increase reaction sensitivity but also fragility. Rapid acceleration may indicate discovery, event reset or reflexive feedback. Deceleration may mean consumption, contradiction, event waiting or simple displacement; the analyst must discriminate rather than infer decay mechanically.
