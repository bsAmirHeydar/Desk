# Media Policy Analyst and Social Transmission Channels

## Transmission channels

Narratives spread through official communication, policy Q&A, earnings calls, sell-side revisions, buy-side discussion, public media, specialist media and social channels. Each channel has different incentives, audience and timestamp quality.

## Interpretation

Media volume measures exposure, not adoption. Policy repetition can change expectations directly. Analyst revisions can reveal institutional embedding. Social acceleration may reveal salience but also noise or manipulation.

Store channel, source class, first observed time, independence, audience and whether the observation supports awareness, interpretation, institutional adoption or only publicity. Do not merge those evidentiary roles.
