# Attention Evidence and Observability Tiers

## Evidence hierarchy

Attention is latent and must be inferred. Evidence quality depends on whether the analyst observes actual decision behavior or only public traces.

- Tier 0: official communication, first-release data and verified market-implied variables.
- Tier 1: public market reactions, options and cross-asset behavior.
- Tier 2: contemporaneous public institutional text, earnings-call emphasis and analyst revisions.
- Tier 3: licensed text, flow or positioning datasets.
- Tier 4: proprietary internal order, portfolio or prime-broker evidence.
- Tier 5: unavailable or unobservable.

## Triangulation

High-confidence attention requires multiple independent evidence families. Media frequency alone can indicate awareness but not institutional adoption. Price alone can indicate sensitivity but not the selected interpretation. Options alone can indicate distributional repricing but not its cause.

## Confidence caps

When only public text and price proxies exist, precise attention share and institutional-adoption claims are prohibited. Use an ordinal band and cap confidence. Store every supporting observation with timestamp, source locator, provenance and horizon.
