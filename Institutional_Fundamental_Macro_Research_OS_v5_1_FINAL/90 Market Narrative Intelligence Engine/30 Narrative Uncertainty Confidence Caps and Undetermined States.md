# Narrative Uncertainty Confidence Caps and Undetermined States

## Uncertainty is a state

Use `UNDETERMINED` when the evidence cannot distinguish attention, flow or causal explanations. Do not force a dominant narrative every day.

## Confidence caps

- Public media plus price only: cap institutional-adoption confidence.
- Price-only flow inference: cap flow-control confidence.
- Missing contemporaneous text: cap historical narrative confidence.
- Missing expectation baseline: cap validity and surprise interpretation.
- Mechanically related cross-asset evidence: cap confirmation confidence.

Store unavailable inputs, proxy limitations, rival models and the observation that would resolve uncertainty.
