# Scheduled Event Narrative Reset Protocol

## Event windows

Create frozen states at T-60, T-15, T0, T+5, T+15, T+30 and T+60 when relevant. Before the release, record expected outcomes, incumbent narrative, challenger and predeclared leader sequence.

## Reset logic

The event may reinforce, weaken, fragment, displace or reopen a narrative. Separate headline surprise, composition, revisions, policy reaction and positioning surprise. A reset requires evidence that the market's decision variable or interpretation changed—not merely volatility.

## Post-event output

Update V11 fact force/consumption first, then V12 attention, validity, dominance, price control and transition state. Preserve every prior snapshot.
