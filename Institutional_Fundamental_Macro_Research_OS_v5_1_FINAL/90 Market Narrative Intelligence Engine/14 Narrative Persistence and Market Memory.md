# Narrative Persistence and Market Memory

## Persistence object

Narrative persistence measures how long the market is likely to keep using an interpretation as an organizing frame. It depends on explanatory usefulness, policy repetition, institutional embedding, portfolio relevance, position inertia, event calendar, reinforcement cadence and rival pressure.

## Market memory

Memory persists through reference levels, prior forecasts, positioning, risk limits, policy language and recurring comparison points. A narrative can survive a quiet period because institutions continue to organize decisions around it even when media attention falls.

## Outputs

Record persistence class, survival window, memory anchors, decay basis, reinforcement conditions, displacement conditions and confidence. Do not force one half-life across fact, attention, flow and narrative.
