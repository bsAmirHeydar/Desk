# Causal Leader Attention Leader and Flow Leader Separation

## Leader taxonomy

- `fundamental_state_leader`: the state variable with the strongest underlying force;
- `attention_leader`: the topic receiving the greatest decision focus;
- `causal_leader`: the variable that should move first if the proposed mechanism is active;
- `price_controller`: the narrative best explaining target sensitivity;
- `flow_controller`: the mechanical or institutional flow dominating immediate movement;
- `media_leader`: the most amplified public topic;
- `policy_leader`: the variable emphasized by the reaction function.

## Adjudication

Leaders may coincide, but the system must not assume it. A month-end flow can control EURUSD while Fed–ECB divergence remains the fundamental leader. AI headlines can lead media attention while real yields control Nasdaq valuation.

For each leader record evidence, horizon, start time, confidence and a change trigger. A leader change is a state event and must preserve the outgoing leader rather than overwrite history.
