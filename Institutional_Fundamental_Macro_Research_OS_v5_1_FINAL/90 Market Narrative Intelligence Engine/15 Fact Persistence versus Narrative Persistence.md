# Fact Persistence versus Narrative Persistence

## Two clocks

Fact persistence belongs to V11 and measures the survival of an economic, policy, earnings, physical or liquidity process. Narrative persistence measures the survival of the market's interpretation and attention regime.

## Four canonical combinations

1. Durable fact / durable narrative — structural process remains active and continuously organizes pricing.
2. Durable fact / short narrative — reality persists but attention is displaced; this can create latent asymmetry.
3. Short fact / durable narrative — reflexive adoption, positioning or policy response outlives the catalyst.
4. Short fact / short narrative — transient shock with rapid consumption.

## Analytical use

Store both clocks and explain divergence. Never copy V11 `fact_persistence` into V12 `narrative_persistence`. A persistent narrative with weakening fact support should receive higher fragility and replacement risk.
