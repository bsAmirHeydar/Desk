# Ignored Facts Blind Spots and Latent Asymmetry

## Primary question

Which strong and persistent facts are under-attended relative to their plausible asset consequence?

## Classification

Use `IGNORED_BUT_POWERFUL`, `UNDER_ATTENDED`, `KNOWN_BUT_DISMISSED`, `DORMANT_STRUCTURAL`, `WAITING_FOR_CATALYST`, `RIGHTLY_IGNORED` or `UNDETERMINED`.

## Asymmetry test

Neglect becomes actionable fundamental asymmetry only when the fact is valid, persistent, linked to an unresolved expectation gap, has a plausible discovery catalyst and affects an asset whose current narrative leaves limited defense against the repricing. Unpopular facts are not automatically opportunities.

For each candidate record reason for neglect, discovery trigger, target asset/horizon, expected first mover, contradiction load and whether current price/valuation already embeds the risk through another channel.
