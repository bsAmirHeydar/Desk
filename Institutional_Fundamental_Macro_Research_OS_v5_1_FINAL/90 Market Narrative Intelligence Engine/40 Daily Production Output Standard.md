# Daily Production Output Standard

## Required sections

1. Executive Market Map for Nasdaq 100, S&P 500, Gold and EURUSD.
2. Global Fact Ledger with timestamps, surprise, materiality, fact persistence, consumption, remaining pressure and provenance.
3. Market Attention Map with rank/band, concentration, acceleration, evidence and ignored facts.
4. Competing Narrative Matrix with thesis, fact basis, validity, dominance, price control, persistence, saturation, fragility and transition trigger.
5. Asset-specific deep analysis across structural, cyclical, tactical and event horizons.
6. Narrative Transition Watch.
7. Daily Watchlist with expected reinforcement, weakening and replacement outcomes.
8. Unknowns, proxy fields and confidence caps.
9. Machine-readable V12 state record.

## Communication discipline

Use plain language in the executive layer and retain technical provenance in appendices. Do not report ordinal numbers as probabilities. When no narrative is credibly dominant, say so explicitly.
