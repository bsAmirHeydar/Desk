Reconstruct the completed day's immutable state path. Attribute every material change in fact force, consumption, attention, narrative validity, dominance, price control and transition risk to evidence available at that timestamp. Separate ex-ante quality from outcome, identify ignored facts that gained or lost salience, and create the next-day inherited state without using post-cutoff information.
