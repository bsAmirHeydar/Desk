# Multi-Horizon and Multi-Asset Narrative States

## Required horizons

`MICRO_0_15M`, `SHORT_15_60M`, `SESSION_1_6H`, `DAILY_OPEN_TO_CLOSE`, `MULTI_DAY_2_10D`, `SWING_2_8W`, `CYCLICAL` and `STRUCTURAL`.

## State inheritance

Long-horizon narratives provide priors, not automatic tactical signals. A flow narrative can dominate EURUSD for one session while policy divergence remains the cyclical thesis. An intraday real-yield shock can displace AI monetization in Nasdaq without invalidating the multi-week earnings narrative.

For every asset/horizon report dominant narrative, challenger, attention state, leaders, validity, persistence, fact persistence, consumption, remaining power, saturation, fragility and transition trigger.

Conflicts remain explicit; never average incompatible horizons.
