# Scope Definitions and Honest Ten-of-Ten Standard

## Scope

The V12 object is the market's **selection and interpretation of reality**. It begins after the fact universe and the V11 fundamental-state vector have been constructed. It asks which facts are salient, how they are interpreted, which interpretation coordinates behavior, and whether that interpretation controls price, flow or policy expectations.

A ten-of-ten architecture does not promise perfect narrative detection. It requires explicit objects, field-level provenance, falsifiable transitions, asset/horizon separation, rival narratives, no-lookahead reconstruction and honest `UNDETERMINED` states.

## Completion tests

A V12 analysis is complete only when it can answer all of the following without collapsing concepts:

1. What facts existed at the cutoff?
2. Which facts were strong and persistent under V11?
3. Which facts received attention, and what proves that attention?
4. What interpretation joined those facts into a narrative?
5. Which competing interpretations remained viable?
6. Which narrative was attention-dominant, price-dominant, flow-dominant and causally supported?
7. Was dominance supported by facts or sustained mainly through reflexivity?
8. What powerful facts were ignored?
9. What would reinforce, displace or invalidate the narrative?
10. How did the answer differ by asset and horizon?

## Internal-certification boundary

Internal certification covers architecture, schema, prompt behavior and deterministic validators. Empirical claims require a frozen historical dataset and purged out-of-sample tests. Media counts, price moves and public proxies may support an ordinal state but cannot create a fabricated probability or precise attention share.
