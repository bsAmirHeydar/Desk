# Benchmark Scenarios and Acceptance Tests

Thirty frozen-information benchmark contracts are stored in the `benchmarks` directory. They test ignored strong facts, weak but dominant narratives, validity/dominance divergence, fact/narrative persistence divergence, flow hijacks, event resets, fragmentation, co-dominance, saturation without fact consumption and no-hindsight reconstruction.

Every benchmark includes allowed evidence, prohibited future information, expected candidate set, acceptable state ranges, required rivals, confidence caps and pass/fail criteria. Benchmarks test reasoning architecture, not external causal truth.
