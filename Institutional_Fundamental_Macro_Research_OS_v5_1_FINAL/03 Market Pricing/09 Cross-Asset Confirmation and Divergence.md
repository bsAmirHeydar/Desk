## Purpose

Cross-asset analysis is not a collection of correlations. It is a test of causal consistency.

A proposed macro shock should affect the markets closest to the transmission channel.

## Core Monitor

For US index, gold, and macro CFD trading:

## Coherent Clusters

### Hawkish inflation repricing

- front-end yields up;
- real yields up;
- cuts priced out;
- dollar up;
- gold down;
- Nasdaq underperforms;
- credit may remain stable initially.

### Benign disinflation

- front-end yields down;
- real yields down;
- dollar softer;
- credit stable;
- equity breadth improves;
- volatility falls;
- gold supported.

### Growth scare

- yields down;
- credit wider;
- oil/cyclicals weaker;
- defensives stronger;
- volatility higher;
- dollar may strengthen if funding demand dominates.

### Fiscal/term-premium shock

- long-end yields up more than front end;
- curve steepens bearishly;
- duration equities weaken;
- dollar response mixed;
- gold response depends on real yields versus credibility demand.

### Commodity supply shock

- oil/gas up;
- breakevens up;
- real growth expectations down;
- importers’ currencies weaker;
- energy equities stronger;
- broad equities mixed/weak.

## Divergence Types

### Timing divergence

One market leads; another catches up.

### Channel divergence

Markets reflect different parts of the shock.

Example: yields rise on growth, but Nasdaq falls on discount rates.

### Horizon divergence

Intraday flow conflicts with swing fundamentals.

### False divergence

Prices are compared without duration, currency, session, or contract adjustment.

### Regime transition

Historical correlation breaks because the dominant constraint changed.

## Confirmation Rule

Do not demand every asset move in the textbook direction. Demand that the **most causal markets** confirm.

Examples:

- policy thesis → front end first;
- real-rate gold thesis → real yields and dollar;
- growth scare → credit and cyclicals;
- physical oil thesis → curve and products;
- FX relative-policy thesis → rate differentials.

## Divergence Trade

A divergence is tradeable only if you specify:

1. which market is leading;
2. why the lagging market should converge;
3. the catalyst or flow;
4. the time horizon;
5. what would prove the divergence structural rather than temporary.

## Live Use

During a setup, ask:

- Is cross-asset confirmation improving?
- Is the execution asset lagging or rejecting the macro impulse?
- Has the causal driver reversed?
- Is the technical trigger aligned with the current impulse?
- Is the move now mostly flow rather than information?

Cross-asset evidence should change confidence, not move the stop arbitrarily.
