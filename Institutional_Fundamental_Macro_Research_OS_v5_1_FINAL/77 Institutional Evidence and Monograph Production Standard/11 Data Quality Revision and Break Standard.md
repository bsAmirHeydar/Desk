# 11 Data Quality Revision and Break Standard

Quality is assessed across completeness, validity, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, accuracy, stability and lineage.

Revision analysis reports first-to-latest change, bias, variance, benchmark effects and rank stability. Structural breaks include methodology, coverage, classification, regulation, market design and data-vendor changes. Breaks are modeled or segmented; they are never silently ignored.

Critical series have SLAs, quarantine rules, fallback sources and consumer notifications.
