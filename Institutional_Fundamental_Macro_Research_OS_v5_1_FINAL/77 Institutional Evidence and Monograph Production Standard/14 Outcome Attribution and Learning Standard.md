# 14 Outcome Attribution and Learning Standard

Outcome review separates:

- information-set quality;
- expectation and pricing measurement;
- causal-model quality;
- scenario probabilities;
- expression and hedge selection;
- portfolio interaction;
- liquidity, impact and financing;
- timing and operational execution;
- residual noise.

The review preserves the original record and prohibits hindsight editing. Rejected opportunities and no-deployment decisions are included to measure opportunity cost and avoided loss.
