# 13 Uncertainty Calibration and Decision Threshold Standard

Point estimates are insufficient. Research reports distributions, intervals, sensitivity to assumptions and structural uncertainty.

Forecast probabilities are tested with reliability diagrams, Brier scores, log scores and subgroup calibration. Decision thresholds are chosen under asymmetric utility and constraints. Confidence is capped when data, model identification, liquidity or regime stability are weak.
