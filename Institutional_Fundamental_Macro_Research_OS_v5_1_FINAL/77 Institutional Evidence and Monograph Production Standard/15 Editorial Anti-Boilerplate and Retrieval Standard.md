# 15 Editorial Anti-Boilerplate and Retrieval Standard

Editorial quality is measured by information density, internal consistency and retrieval usefulness.

Shared doctrine appears once in canonical standards. Domain notes contain unique mechanics, data, equations, examples and failure modes. MOCs provide navigation rather than restating chapters. Titles and aliases are controlled. Search tests verify that a query returns the relevant domain note rather than dozens of near-duplicates.
