# 10 Citation Locator and Archival Standard

Citations must locate the evidence at the most precise durable level available: series ID, table, page, paragraph, filing item, transcript line, API field or legal clause.

Web pages that change require archived snapshots or content hashes. PDFs require page and table references. Machine-readable data require query parameters and vintage. Transcripts require speaker and time or line range. When licensing prevents storage, the desk stores metadata, hash and regeneration instructions.
