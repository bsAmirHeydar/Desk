# 12 Model Comparison and Benchmark Standard

Every advanced model competes with transparent baselines appropriate to the task: historical mean, random walk, simple autoregression, survey consensus, static accounting rule, or market-implied benchmark.

Comparisons use identical point-in-time information, forecast origins, horizons and loss functions. Statistical significance is secondary to economic value, calibration, stability, cost and interpretability. Challenger models remain active after promotion to detect decay.
