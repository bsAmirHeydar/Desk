# 06 Country Book Production Standard

A country book is a living institutional model of the state, policy system, balance sheets and investable markets.

## Mandatory chapters

Institutions and law; national accounts; inflation; labor; fiscal accounts; public debt; central bank; banking and credit; households; corporations; housing; external accounts; currency regime; reserves and capital controls; trade and commodities; political economy; demographics; climate and energy; local market structure; source quality; historical regimes; scenario and vulnerability matrix.

## Locality rule

Local definitions, accounting calendars, tax rules, settlement conventions and policy institutions take priority over imported U.S. templates. Translation tables may compare countries but may not erase local mechanics.
