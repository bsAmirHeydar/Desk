# 08 Source Contract Production Standard

A source contract describes what the institution receives, when, under which rights, in which format, with which methodology and how failures are handled.

## Fields

Provider, product, legal entity, license, redistribution rights, fields, definitions, units, frequencies, publication schedule, revisions, historical depth, timestamp semantics, delivery protocol, authentication, expected latency, quality tests, outage behavior, retention, cost center, owner and replacement source.

Source contracts are versioned. Methodology changes create a new contract version and trigger downstream impact analysis.
