# 07 Asset Driver Book Production Standard

An asset driver book maps the economic and institutional variables that determine expected cash flows, discount rates, scarcity, carry, optionality and risk premia.

## Required content

- instrument and market specification;
- fundamental valuation or balance framework;
- structural, cyclical, tactical, multi-day and intraday driver hierarchy;
- market-implied quantities and positioning;
- cross-asset leaders and confirmations;
- scenario payoff by instrument;
- liquidity, financing, basis and capacity;
- point-in-time historical sensitivity estimates;
- model disagreement and failure regimes;
- monitoring dashboard specification.

Driver books must not be generic lists. They must quantify or explicitly schedule the quantification of sensitivities and thresholds.
