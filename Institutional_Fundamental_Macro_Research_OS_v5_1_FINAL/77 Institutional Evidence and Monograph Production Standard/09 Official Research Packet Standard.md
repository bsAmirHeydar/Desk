# 09 Official Research Packet Standard

A research packet is more than a link list. It is an archived, extracted and compared evidence package for a release, report, filing or policy event.

It contains the source artifact, timestamp, executive extraction, tables and figures used, changes from the prior version, methodology changes, important revisions, claim IDs, market-implied baseline before publication, desk interpretation, dissent, unknowns and follow-up tasks.

Packets for recurring releases support structured comparison across vintages and automated detection of changed language, tables and assumptions.
