# 05 Historical Case Reconstruction Standard

A case study must reconstruct the historical information set, not tell a story using later knowledge.

## Required layers

- event and information timeline in original timezones;
- first-release macro and corporate data;
- contemporaneous forecasts and consensus distribution;
- curve, options, credit, FX and relevant physical-market snapshots;
- policy and legal documents available at the cutoff;
- positioning and liquidity evidence with uncertainty labels;
- ex-ante causal and rival models;
- decision state and risk constraints before outcome;
- outcome path at predefined horizons;
- ex-post audit separated from the original decision;
- lessons that distinguish process failure from randomness.

A case with no archived source packet or timestamp audit is illustrative, not research-grade.
