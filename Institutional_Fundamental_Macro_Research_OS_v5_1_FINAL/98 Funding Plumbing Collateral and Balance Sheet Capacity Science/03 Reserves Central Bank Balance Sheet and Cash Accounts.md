# Reserves, Central-Bank Balance Sheet and Cash Accounts

Stocks and flows are distinct. Reserve balances, government cash accounts, central-bank assets/liabilities and standing facilities may affect funding conditions, but each series has accounting identities and counterparties.

Required analysis:
1. observed balance-sheet fact;
2. change decomposition where possible;
3. settlement/effective timing;
4. relevant intermediary channel;
5. horizon-specific expected transmission;
6. competing offsets.

Never convert aggregate balance-sheet expansion into guaranteed asset-price direction.
