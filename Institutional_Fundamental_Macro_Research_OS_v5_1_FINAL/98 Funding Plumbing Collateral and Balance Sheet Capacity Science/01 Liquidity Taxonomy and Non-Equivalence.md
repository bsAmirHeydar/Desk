# Liquidity Taxonomy

Keep separate:
- **monetary liquidity**: central-bank/reserve/money conditions;
- **funding liquidity**: ability/cost to finance positions;
- **market liquidity**: ability to transact with limited price impact;
- **collateral liquidity**: availability/quality/financing terms of collateral;
- **dealer balance-sheet capacity**: intermediary ability/willingness to warehouse risk;
- **solvency/credit quality**: balance-sheet asset/liability adequacy.

A fall in one does not mechanically imply a fall in all others.
