# Six-Market Funding Mapping

## U.S. equity indices
Dollar funding, secured financing, reserves, Treasury settlement, dealer balance sheet and margin can alter transmission/capacity, especially around stress/calendars. They do not create earnings/rate-based Fundamental Direction.

## EURUSD / USDJPY
Bilateral central-bank/funding blocks, cross-currency basis, money-market conditions and official operations matter. Analyze both currency blocks; never infer spot direction from a single funding variable.

## XAUUSD
USD funding, futures margin/collateral, lease/basis or physical financing where sourced may affect carrying/arbitrage channels. Public coverage of global OTC/physical financing is partial.
