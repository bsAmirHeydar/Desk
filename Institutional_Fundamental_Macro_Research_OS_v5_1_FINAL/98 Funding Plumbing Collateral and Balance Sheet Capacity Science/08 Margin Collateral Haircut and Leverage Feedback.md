# Margin, Collateral, Haircut and Leverage Feedback

Margin/haircut changes can create nonlinear financing and forced-deleveraging channels.

Store:
- authority/venue/clearinghouse;
- instrument;
- effective time;
- initial/maintenance margin or haircut;
- change magnitude;
- participant scope;
- estimated affected exposure only as derived/model inference.

Expected deleveraging is not realized flow until transaction evidence exists.
