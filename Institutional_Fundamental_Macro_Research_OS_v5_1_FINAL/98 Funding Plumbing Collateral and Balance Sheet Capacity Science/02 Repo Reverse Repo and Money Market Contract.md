# Repo, Reverse Repo and Money-Market Contract

For each observation preserve facility/market identity, rate, quantity, tenor, counterparty eligibility, collateral terms, operation date, settlement/maturity and publication time.

Do not read a central-bank facility balance as a direct equity buy/sell signal. The causal chain must state how a funding condition could transmit into risk-taking, collateral demand, dealer balance sheet or cash availability.
