# Primary Dealer Positions, Financing and Settlement Fails

## Why it matters
Treasury and dealer balance-sheet conditions can alter rate transmission, market-making capacity and settlement stress across equities, FX and gold.

## Direct public layer
New York Fed Primary Dealer Statistics provide aggregate dealer-community positions, transactions, financing and fails with a weekly reporting clock.

## Interpretation
- position stock != transaction flow;
- financing volume != available risk capacity;
- settlement fails can indicate scarcity/friction but require issue/context decomposition;
- primary dealers are a critical intermediary subset, not the whole market.

## Horizon
Use as multi-day/structural state and event context. Never relabel weekly aggregate data as current intraday dealer inventory.
