# Cross-Currency Basis and Dollar Funding

For FX-sensitive instruments, cross-currency funding conditions may reveal relative demand for balance sheet/currency funding.

Preserve:
- currency pair;
- tenor;
- benchmark curves;
- collateral/CSA assumptions when material;
- timestamp;
- source quality;
- construction method.

Basis is not identical to spot FX direction. It is a funding-price state that can transmit differently across horizons and regimes.
