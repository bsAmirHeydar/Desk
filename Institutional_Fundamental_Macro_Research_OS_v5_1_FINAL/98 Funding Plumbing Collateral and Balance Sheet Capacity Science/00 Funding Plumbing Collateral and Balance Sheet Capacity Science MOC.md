# 98 — Funding, Plumbing, Collateral and Balance-Sheet Capacity Science

**Release:** V18.0 / D2  
**Authority:** `CANONICAL_SHADOW`  
**Permission authority:** none in V18.

## Mission
Model whether the financial transmission system can **fund, collateralize, intermediate, settle and carry risk** across the relevant horizon.

## Core law
`MONETARY_LIQUIDITY != FUNDING_LIQUIDITY != MARKET_LIQUIDITY != DEALER_CAPACITY != SOLVENCY`

The module refuses a single undifferentiated “liquidity” score unless every component and transformation is declared.

## Domains
- secured/unsecured funding;
- central-bank reserves and facilities;
- repo/reverse-repo and money-market conditions;
- collateral scarcity/abundance and specialness;
- cross-currency funding/basis;
- Treasury/cash settlement pressure;
- dealer/bank balance-sheet capacity;
- margin and financing constraints;
- calendar balance-sheet effects.
