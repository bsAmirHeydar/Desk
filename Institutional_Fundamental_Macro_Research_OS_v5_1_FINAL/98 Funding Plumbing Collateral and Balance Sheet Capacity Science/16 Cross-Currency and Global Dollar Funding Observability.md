# Cross-Currency and Global Dollar Funding Observability

## Components
SOFR/secured funding, dealer financing, bank balance-sheet data, cross-currency basis, central-bank facilities, BIS global liquidity, Treasury settlement and relevant ECB/BoJ statistics.

## Multi-horizon rule
High-frequency prices such as basis/funding rates can be current-state evidence; H.8/Z.1/BIS aggregates are slower structural context. Never let slow data masquerade as a live funding shock.

## FX rule
Funding/basis affects transmission and carry economics but cannot independently originate Fundamental Direction. EURUSD/USDJPY remain bilateral macro/policy systems.
