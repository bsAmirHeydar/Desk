# Secured Funding Stress

Track secured funding conditions using direct official/exchange/administrator facts when available:
- benchmark secured rates;
- general collateral conditions;
- dispersion and tails;
- specific collateral specialness where sourced;
- operation take-up;
- fails/settlement indicators where available.

A spread or specialness measure is interpreted only relative to its instrument, benchmark and historical/regime reference. Stress inference is derived, not an observed fact.
