# Treasury Settlement, Supply and Cash-Drain Channels

Separate:
- auction announcement;
- auction execution;
- settlement date;
- coupon/bill maturity and redemptions;
- government cash-account changes;
- dealer/intermediary inventory effects;
- investor allocation response.

Gross issuance is not identical to net liquidity drain. The relevant effect depends on buyers, settlement, financing, reserves, money-fund allocation and other simultaneous balance-sheet flows.
