# Dealer and Bank Balance-Sheet Capacity

Direct measures of dealer inventory, leverage or balance-sheet constraints may be unavailable or licensed. Public proxies must remain proxies.

Potential evidence classes:
- regulatory/public balance-sheet facts;
- primary-dealer statistics where applicable;
- repo/funding spreads;
- intermediation volumes;
- quarter/year-end calendar constraints;
- model-implied capacity.

Never infer exact dealer risk capacity from one spread or price move.
