# Contradiction, Unknowns and Missing-Data Gate

## Mandatory ledgers

### Contradiction ledger

Record evidence that does not fit the primary model, its source, reliability, horizon and potential explanation. Contradictions cannot be hidden in footnotes.

### Unknowns ledger

Classify unknowns as:

- unavailable but measurable;
- structurally unobservable;
- dependent on licensed/proprietary data;
- subject to future policy or behavioral choice;
- model uncertainty;
- definitional uncertainty.

### Proxy ledger

For each proxy state the target construct, mapping rationale, historical stability, current failure risk and confidence cap.

## Missing-data behavior

If data are unavailable:

- narrow the conclusion;
- lower the confidence cap;
- switch from FULL to CONDITIONAL certification;
- specify what observation would resolve the uncertainty.

Do not fill gaps with generic statements such as “institutional flows likely supported the move” unless a source or explicitly labelled hypothesis exists.

## Contradiction resolution

Possible outcomes:

- measurement mismatch;
- different horizon;
- common-factor contamination;
- regime transition;
- primary model weakening;
- data error;
- genuine unresolved conflict.

Unresolved conflict is an acceptable conclusion. False synthesis is not.
