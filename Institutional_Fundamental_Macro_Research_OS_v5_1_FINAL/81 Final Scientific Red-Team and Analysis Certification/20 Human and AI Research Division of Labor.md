# Human and AI Research Division of Labor

## AI strengths

- broad Vault retrieval;
- structured comparison of models;
- source discovery and synthesis;
- consistency checks across horizons and assets;
- generation of explicit scenario and contradiction ledgers;
- application of standard output contracts.

## AI failure risks

- confident fabrication of unavailable data;
- citation mismatch;
- treating the newest search result as the best source;
- reading only a small part of a large Vault;
- collapsing uncertainty into a clean narrative;
- confusing instrument conventions;
- inability to access licensed positioning, dealer or consensus data.

## Human responsibilities

- define the research object and decision context;
- supply licensed or proprietary information where relevant;
- challenge causal assumptions and institutional feasibility;
- validate critical calculations and document locators;
- approve conditional use of proxies;
- own the final decision and risk.

## Mandatory AI behavior

The assistant must disclose what it opened, what it could not access, which evidence is current, which claims are inferred and why the report did or did not pass certification. It must not imply that use of a large Vault guarantees completeness.
