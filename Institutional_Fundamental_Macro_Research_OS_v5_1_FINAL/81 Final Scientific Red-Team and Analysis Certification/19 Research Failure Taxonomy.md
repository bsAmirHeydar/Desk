# Research Failure Taxonomy

## Scope failures

- wrong instrument, index version, contract month, currency or entity;
- question answered at the wrong horizon;
- country analysis reduced to generic global macro.

## Evidence failures

- citation laundering through secondary articles;
- using one source under multiple labels as triangulation;
- unsupported precision;
- source date after historical cutoff;
- source does not support the cited claim.

## Measurement failures

- stock–flow confusion;
- nominal–real confusion;
- denominator and base effects;
- annualization errors;
- revision and seasonal-adjustment errors;
- incomparable accounting or national definitions.

## Model failures

- monocausal narrative;
- omitted reaction function;
- correlation treated as causality;
- failure to model risk premium;
- equilibrium relationship used for event timing;
- historical analogue treated as deterministic.

## Market-translation failures

- ignoring what is already priced;
- no carry or financing analysis;
- position stock confused with current flow;
- liquidity and balance-sheet constraints ignored;
- target asset response used as sole confirmation.

## Communication failures

- contradictions hidden;
- unknowns converted into estimates;
- methods repeated instead of applied;
- executive summary stronger than evidence;
- certification verdict omitted.
