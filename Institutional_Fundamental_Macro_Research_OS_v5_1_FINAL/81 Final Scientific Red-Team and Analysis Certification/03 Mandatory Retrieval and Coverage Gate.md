# Mandatory Retrieval and Coverage Gate

## Objective

The central failure of broad knowledge systems is retrieval omission: the answer appears sophisticated but only uses the easiest macro narrative. This gate requires a traceable mapping from the analysis object to every material Vault domain.

## Retrieval layers

1. **Core inference standards** — evidence, causality, horizons, uncertainty and source lineage.
2. **Object-specific science** — the asset, issuer, country, sector, physical market or institution.
3. **Macro state** — growth, inflation, labor, fiscal, monetary, credit and liquidity where relevant.
4. **Market pricing** — curves, valuation, implied distributions, carry, basis and risk premium.
5. **Institutional transmission** — banks, dealers, asset managers, hedgers, official institutions and constraints.
6. **Cross-asset confirmation** — rates, FX, credit, equities, commodities and volatility.
7. **Historical regime and analogues** — used as reference classes, not deterministic templates.
8. **Current or historical source routes** — official data and primary documents.
9. **Output contract** — the report format and certification gates.

## Coverage ledger

The report must list:

| Domain | Notes used | Why material | Evidence obtained | Omission risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

A domain may be marked immaterial only with a reason. Silence is not a reason.

## Minimum object-specific retrieval

- **Equity index**: rates, earnings, sector composition, index methodology, breadth/concentration, credit, FX, volatility and systematic flows.
- **Single company**: filings, segment economics, accounting quality, capital structure, valuation, industry, macro sensitivities and governance.
- **FX pair**: both countries, policy paths, external accounts, valuation, funding basis, intervention, carry and global risk regime.
- **Sovereign bond**: policy path, inflation, fiscal, issuance, investor base, liquidity, term premium, currency and sovereign risk.
- **Commodity**: physical balance, quality/location/time spreads, inventories, logistics, hedging, policy and substitution.
- **Country**: national accounts, fiscal, monetary, banks, external balance, institutional capacity, political economy and market structure.
- **Portfolio**: every underlying exposure plus hidden common factors, nonlinearities, liquidity and scenario concentration.

## Veto conditions

The retrieval gate fails if:

- the Vault Reading Ledger is absent;
- a major driver is omitted without justification;
- the analysis relies on one asset-class narrative only;
- country or contract identity is unresolved;
- the report uses generic market commentary where an object-specific monograph exists.
