# Benchmark Scoring Rubric and Regression Standard

## Scoring

Each suite is scored using the 60-point certification scorecard. Additional benchmark-specific deductions apply:

- minus 10 for missing mandatory asset or institutional domain;
- minus 10 for citation mismatch or unsupported current fact;
- automatic rejection for historical lookahead;
- minus 5 for each major horizon collapsed into another;
- minus 5 for presenting a proxy as observation;
- minus 5 for omitting a serious rival model;
- minus 5 for failing to answer the special question.

## Regression standard

A prompt or Vault change is accepted only if it does not reduce any benchmark below CONDITIONAL and the median score across all suites is at least 55. No integrity veto may occur.

## Review record

Store the prompt version, Vault version, analysis date, evidence availability, score by category, failed gates, reviewer comments and corrective action.
