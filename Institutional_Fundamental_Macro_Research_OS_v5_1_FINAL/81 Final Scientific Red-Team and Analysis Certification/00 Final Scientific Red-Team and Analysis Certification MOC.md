# Final Scientific Red-Team and Analysis Certification

> [!abstract] Mission
> Convert the Vault from a broad institutional knowledge canon into a disciplined analysis-production system that can reject incomplete work, expose missing evidence, distinguish current from historical knowledge, and certify a report only when the research object has been covered at institutional depth.

## Certification doctrine

A report is not certified because it is long, confident or comprehensive-looking. It is certified only when it passes all material gates:

1. the analysis object and instrument are defined;
2. the relevant Vault domains were retrieved;
3. time-sensitive facts were verified from current primary sources;
4. historical work is locked to a valid information cutoff and vintage;
5. state, expectations, valuation, carry, risk premium and positioning are separated;
6. causal and rival models are explicit;
7. cross-asset and balance-sheet transmission is tested;
8. contradictions and unknowns are preserved;
9. scenarios, signposts, invalidation and expiry are stated;
10. every load-bearing claim has an evidence locator;
11. no unavailable licensed or proprietary information is invented;
12. the final report satisfies the relevant domain scorecard and benchmark suite.
