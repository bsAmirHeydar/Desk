# Output Completeness and Decision Usefulness Gate

## Institutional report order

1. certification verdict;
2. object, mode, cutoff and source freshness;
3. executive conclusion by horizon;
4. Vault Reading Ledger;
5. claim–evidence ledger;
6. state vector;
7. expectations and pricing baseline;
8. causal and rival-model map;
9. object-specific fundamentals;
10. cross-asset and institutional transmission;
11. valuation, carry and payoff;
12. scenarios and tails;
13. contradictions and unknowns;
14. confidence, invalidation, expiry and update triggers;
15. machine-readable context object;
16. certification scorecard.

## Decision usefulness

The report must answer:

- what is known;
- what is estimated;
- what the market appears to expect;
- what assumption is most vulnerable;
- which mechanism would cause repricing;
- which observations would confirm or reject it;
- how conclusions differ by horizon;
- what cannot be concluded.

## Compression rule

Do not repeat generic methodology in every section. Apply it. Long background is justified only when it changes the current conclusion or explains a disputed mechanism.

## Veto conditions

- the executive conclusion contradicts later sections;
- material contradictions are omitted from the summary;
- the report does not answer the special question;
- the output is an encyclopedia entry rather than an analysis of the specified object;
- machine-readable fields contain stronger claims than the prose.
