# Country Books and Historical Regime Atlas — Cross-Domain Transmission Matrix

## Translation rule

No domain conclusion is complete until the effect on adjacent systems is traced. Translate through expected cash flows, discount rates, currency value, credit availability, collateral, physical scarcity, volatility, liquidity and risk premium.

## Matrix

| Origin driver | First receiver | Secondary receiver | Confirmation | Common reversal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| growth and inflation structure | policy or primary market | financing and valuation | independent source and related market | prior pricing or policy offset |
| fiscal and monetary institutions | balance sheet or income | demand and cash flow | account reconciliation | substitution or lag |
| bank and household balance sheets | expectations | rates, FX or risk premium | contemporaneous pricing | positioning or liquidity |
| external position and currency | institutional constraint | supply, funding or market depth | operational evidence | backstop or adaptation |

## Double-count control

When two observations arise from the same underlying shock, treat them as one evidence family. Confirmation requires a different data-generating process or institutional channel.
