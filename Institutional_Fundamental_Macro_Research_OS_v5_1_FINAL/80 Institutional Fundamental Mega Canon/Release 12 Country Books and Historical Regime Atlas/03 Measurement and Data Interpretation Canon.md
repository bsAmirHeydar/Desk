# Country Books and Historical Regime Atlas — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **United States Country System:** growth and inflation, Fed path, Treasury supply, credit conditions, dollar and external flows.
- **Euro Area Monetary Union System:** core-periphery spreads, credit conditions, wage and inflation divergence, fiscal rules, external balance.
- **United Kingdom Country System:** wage and services inflation, gilt term premium, sterling funding, mortgage rates, current account.
- **Japan Country System:** wages and inflation, JGB curve, FX hedging cost, fiscal impulse, capital outflows.
- **China Country System:** credit impulse, property sales, local financing stress, exports and production, reserves and fixing.
- **Canada Country System:** mortgage resets, household debt service, terms of trade, labor supply, CAD rate differential.
- **Australia and New Zealand Systems:** terms of trade, mortgage rates, population growth, bank funding, China exposure.
- **Switzerland Country System:** FX intervention, inflation, current account, sight deposits, real exchange rate.
- **India Country System:** inflation composition, credit growth, fiscal impulse, oil sensitivity, capital flows.
- **Brazil and Mexico Systems:** real rates, fiscal expectations, terms of trade, foreign participation, currency carry.
- **Emerging Asia Technology Export Systems:** export orders, chip cycle, trade balance, FX reserves, capital flows.
- **Turkey and South Africa Vulnerability Systems:** real rates, reserve adequacy, current account, FX-protected liabilities, sovereign spread.
- **Commodity Exporter Sovereign Systems:** fiscal breakeven, production volume, reserve assets, nonresource balance, currency sensitivity.
- **Historical Regime Atlas and Analogue Method:** state-vector similarity, policy capacity, leverage, external balance, market structure.
- **Historical Crisis Reconstruction Protocol:** timeline, information set, scenario distribution, market response, model error.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
