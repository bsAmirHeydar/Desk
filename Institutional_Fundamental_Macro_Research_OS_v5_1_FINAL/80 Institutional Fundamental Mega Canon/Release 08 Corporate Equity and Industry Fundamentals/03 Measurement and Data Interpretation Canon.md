# Corporate, Equity and Industry Fundamentals — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Three-Statement Integration:** cash conversion, balance-sheet growth, free cash flow, net debt, return on invested capital.
- **Revenue Recognition Price Volume and Mix:** organic growth, price-volume-mix bridge, bookings and backlog, deferred revenue, receivables.
- **Margins Operating Leverage and Cost Structure:** incremental margin, unit economics, cost inflation, utilization, segment margin.
- **Working Capital and Cash Conversion:** cash conversion cycle, days sales outstanding, inventory days, payables days, working-capital cash flow.
- **Free Cash Flow Reinvestment and Maintenance Capital:** FCF margin, maintenance capex estimate, cash taxes, lease-adjusted cash flow, reinvestment rate.
- **ROIC Economic Profit and Incremental Returns:** ROIC, incremental ROIC, economic profit, reinvestment rate, fade period.
- **Capital Allocation Buybacks Dividends and M&A:** deal returns, buyback yield, share-count change, leverage, post-deal integration.
- **Debt Refinancing Covenants and Liquidity:** net leverage, interest coverage, maturity wall, covenant headroom, refinancing spread.
- **Accounting Quality and Forensic Signals:** accrual ratio, cash-to-earnings, receivable growth, inventory divergence, serial adjustments.
- **Valuation DCF Reverse DCF and Residual Income:** implied growth, implied margin, FCF yield, economic-profit spread, scenario value.
- **Earnings Expectations Revisions and Surprise Quality:** EPS and revenue revisions, guidance delta, revision breadth, post-earnings drift, estimate dispersion.
- **Industry Structure Competition and Moats:** market share, pricing power, customer retention, returns on capital, capacity additions.
- **Equity Risk Premium and Discount-Rate Translation:** implied ERP, earnings yield gap, credit-equity relation, valuation dispersion, duration sensitivity.
- **Index Mechanics Concentration and Passive Flows:** top-weight concentration, index earnings contribution, rebalance flow, breadth, equal-weight divergence.
- **Technology Semiconductors and Capital Cycle:** book-to-bill, inventory days, utilization, capex, lead times.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
