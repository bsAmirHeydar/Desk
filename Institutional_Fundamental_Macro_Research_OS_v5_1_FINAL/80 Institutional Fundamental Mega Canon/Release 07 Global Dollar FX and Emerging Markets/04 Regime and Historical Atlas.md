## Historical case method

Each case must freeze the information set, reconstruct expectations, identify balance sheets and policy constraints, trace the transmission sequence and separate ex-ante conclusions from ex-post outcomes. Analogues are accepted only when mechanisms and constraints match.

## Required case families

- benign expansion and normalization;
- inflationary overheating and policy restraint;
- recession and demand destruction;
- banking or nonbank funding stress;
- fiscal or sovereign credibility shock;
- commodity or geopolitical supply shock;
- productivity or structural-capacity shift;
- policy-regime transition.
