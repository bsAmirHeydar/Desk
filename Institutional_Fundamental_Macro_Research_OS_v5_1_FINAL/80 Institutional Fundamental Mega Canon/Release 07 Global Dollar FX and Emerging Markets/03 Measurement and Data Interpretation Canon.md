# Global Dollar, FX and Emerging Markets — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Global Dollar System and Offshore Credit:** BIS dollar credit, cross-border claims, dollar bond issuance, FX swap volume, funding spreads.
- **Cross-Currency Basis and CIP Deviations:** basis curve, hedging-cost estimates, quarter-end moves, currency-specific funding stress, cross-market spreads.
- **Balance of Payments and External Adjustment:** trade balance, primary income, portfolio and direct flows, reserve accumulation, financing composition.
- **International Investment Position and Currency Mismatch:** net IIP, gross external debt, short-term external debt, FX mismatch, income balance.
- **Capital Flows and Flow-Price Feedback:** fund flows, custody data, TIC flows, foreign ownership, flow-price elasticity.
- **FX Valuation Purchasing Power and Equilibrium:** real effective exchange rate, PPP gap, FEER/BEER ranges, unit labor cost, external balance gap.
- **Carry Value Momentum and Regime Interaction:** forward points, real-rate differential, REER gap, trend strength, risk reversal.
- **Central-Bank Intervention and Reserve Management:** reserve changes, forward book, intervention estimates, money-market effects, exchange-rate response.
- **Emerging-Market Local Rates and Currency Risk:** real carry, foreign ownership, reserve coverage, local curve, CDS and FX basis.
- **Currency Crisis and Sudden-Stop Mechanics:** reserve adequacy, short-term debt, current-account financing, forward liabilities, deposit dollarization.
- **Commodity Currencies and Terms of Trade:** commodity price basket, trade balance, capital expenditure, fiscal sensitivity, real-rate differential.
- **Dollar Regimes and Global Financial Cycle:** DXY and broad dollar, cross-currency basis, global credit spreads, EM flows, U.S. real yields.
- **Stablecoins and Digital Dollar Channels:** market capitalization, redemption flows, reserve composition, on-chain transfer, peg deviations.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
