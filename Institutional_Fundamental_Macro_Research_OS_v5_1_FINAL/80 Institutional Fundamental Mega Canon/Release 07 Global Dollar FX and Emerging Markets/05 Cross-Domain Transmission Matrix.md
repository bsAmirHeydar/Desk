# Global Dollar, FX and Emerging Markets — Cross-Domain Transmission Matrix

## Translation rule

No domain conclusion is complete until the effect on adjacent systems is traced. Translate through expected cash flows, discount rates, currency value, credit availability, collateral, physical scarcity, volatility, liquidity and risk premium.

## Matrix

| Origin driver | First receiver | Secondary receiver | Confirmation | Common reversal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| relative policy and growth | policy or primary market | financing and valuation | independent source and related market | prior pricing or policy offset |
| external balance and funding | balance sheet or income | demand and cash flow | account reconciliation | substitution or lag |
| hedging demand and basis | expectations | rates, FX or risk premium | contemporaneous pricing | positioning or liquidity |
| reserve and intervention capacity | institutional constraint | supply, funding or market depth | operational evidence | backstop or adaptation |

## Double-count control

When two observations arise from the same underlying shock, treat them as one evidence family. Confirmation requires a different data-generating process or institutional channel.
