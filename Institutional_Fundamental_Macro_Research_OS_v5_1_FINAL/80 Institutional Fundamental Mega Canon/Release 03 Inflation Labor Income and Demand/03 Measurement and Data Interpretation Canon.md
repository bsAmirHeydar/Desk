# Inflation, Labor, Income and Demand — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Inflation Measurement CPI PCE and Deflators:** headline and core rates, trimmed and median measures, annualized short-run momentum, diffusion and breadth, weight contribution.
- **Inflation Decomposition and Price-Setting Channels:** component contribution, relative-price dispersion, producer-consumer pass-through, margin proxies, import-price sensitivity.
- **Shelter Housing and Rent Inflation:** market-rent growth, official shelter momentum, multifamily completions, vacancy rates, rent-income burden.
- **Wages Productivity and Unit Labor Costs:** compensation per hour, unit labor cost, wage distribution, job-switcher premium, real wage growth.
- **Labor Demand Vacancies Hiring and Separations:** vacancy-unemployment ratio, hiring rate, quits rate, initial and continuing claims, aggregate weekly hours.
- **Labor Supply Participation Demographics and Migration:** prime-age participation, employment-population ratio, labor-force growth, participation gap, hours per worker.
- **Unemployment Slack and Matching Efficiency:** U-3 and broader measures, flow hazard rates, long-term unemployment, vacancy-unemployment relation, underemployment.
- **Household Income Distribution and Consumption Capacity:** real disposable income, income growth by percentile, consumer-credit stress, essential-spending share, cash-flow sensitivity.
- **Inflation Expectations and Credibility:** short and long expectations, dispersion, anchoring measures, inflation risk premium, forecast revisions.
- **Inflation Persistence Regimes and Disinflation:** rolling persistence, component reversal rates, services ex-shelter momentum, wage-productivity gap, inflation breadth.
- **Demand Formation and Distributional Multipliers:** spending by income group, card and retail proxies, debt-service burden, transfer pass-through, saving drawdown.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
