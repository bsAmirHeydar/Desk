# Inflation, Labor, Income and Demand — Cross-Domain Transmission Matrix

## Translation rule

No domain conclusion is complete until the effect on adjacent systems is traced. Translate through expected cash flows, discount rates, currency value, credit availability, collateral, physical scarcity, volatility, liquidity and risk premium.

## Matrix

| Origin driver | First receiver | Secondary receiver | Confirmation | Common reversal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| inflation source and breadth | policy or primary market | financing and valuation | independent source and related market | prior pricing or policy offset |
| persistence and mean reversion | balance sheet or income | demand and cash flow | account reconciliation | substitution or lag |
| wages, productivity and unit labor cost | expectations | rates, FX or risk premium | contemporaneous pricing | positioning or liquidity |
| labor demand and supply | institutional constraint | supply, funding or market depth | operational evidence | backstop or adaptation |

## Double-count control

When two observations arise from the same underlying shock, treat them as one evidence family. Confirmation requires a different data-generating process or institutional channel.
