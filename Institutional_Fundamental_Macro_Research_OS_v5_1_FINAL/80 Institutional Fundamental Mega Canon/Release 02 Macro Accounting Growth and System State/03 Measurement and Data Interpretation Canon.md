# Macro Accounting, Growth and System State — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **National Accounts and GDP Architecture:** real final sales, gross domestic income, nominal GDP, implicit deflator, contribution tables.
- **Sectoral Balances and Financial Positions:** sectoral net lending, debt-service ratios, interest-income transfers, financial-account flows, external financing gap.
- **Flow of Funds and Balance-Sheet Transmission:** credit growth by holder, equity issuance and buybacks, deposit and money-market flows, duration transfer, net worth decomposition.
- **Household Income Consumption and Saving:** real disposable income, consumption by category, saving rate, debt service, delinquency and revolving credit.
- **Business Investment and Capital Formation:** capital expenditure, orders and shipments, capacity utilization, investment intentions, depreciation and net capital formation.
- **Inventories Trade and Final Demand:** inventory contribution, real final sales to domestic purchasers, trade volumes, import penetration, inventory-sales ratios.
- **Productivity Potential Output and Supply Capacity:** output per hour, unit labor cost, capital services, participation-adjusted labor input, potential-output ranges.
- **Business-Cycle Regime and Turning Points:** composite leading indicators, payroll and income breadth, industrial production, real sales, recession probability distributions.
- **Housing Residential Investment and Shelter System:** starts, permits and completions, sales and months supply, mortgage applications, price-to-income, rent and vacancy measures.
- **Supply Chains Inventories and Production Networks:** delivery times, freight indicators, backlogs, input inventories, production-to-orders ratios.
- **Growth Nowcast Interpretation and Revision Risk:** nowcast level and change, forecast interval, release contribution, revision profile, model dispersion.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
