# Release 02 — Macro Accounting, Growth and System State

> [!abstract] Mission
> Construct a complete, internally consistent view of economic activity from accounting identities, sector balance sheets, growth composition, productivity, investment, housing and cyclical dynamics. The release must support both current-state diagnosis and historical reconstruction without confusing nominal growth, real growth, price effects, revisions or one-off contributions.
