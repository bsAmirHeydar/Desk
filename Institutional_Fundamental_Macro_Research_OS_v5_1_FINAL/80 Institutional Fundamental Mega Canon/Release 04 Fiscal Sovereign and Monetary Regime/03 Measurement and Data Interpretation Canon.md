# Fiscal, Sovereign and Monetary Regime — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Fiscal Impulse Structural Balance and Demand Impact:** fiscal impulse, primary spending growth, tax elasticity, transfer flows, government contribution to demand.
- **Government Spending Taxes and Fiscal Multipliers:** multiplier ranges, execution rate, domestic content, recipient propensity to spend, crowding-out indicators.
- **Sovereign Debt Arithmetic and Sustainability:** interest-growth differential, gross financing need, average maturity, interest-revenue ratio, debt stabilizing primary balance.
- **Fiscal Dominance and Monetary-Fiscal Interaction:** fiscal sensitivity to rates, duration held by private sector, central-bank holdings, interest expenditure, inflation compensation.
- **Central-Bank Reaction Functions:** policy-rate path, meeting probabilities, forecast revisions, speech and statement changes, reaction-function residual.
- **Policy Instruments Conventional and Unconventional:** balance-sheet composition, reserve levels, facility usage, term-premium effects, liquidity distribution.
- **QE QT Reserves and Balance-Sheet Regimes:** reserve distribution, repo rates, term premium, bank liquidity ratios, money-market spreads.
- **Financial Conditions and Monetary Transmission:** mortgage and corporate rates, credit spreads, exchange rate, equity valuation, lending standards.
- **Credibility Time Consistency and Policy Error:** expectation anchoring, policy surprise, forecast bias, term-premium response, credibility risk premium.
- **Fiscal-Monetary Regime Classification:** joint impulse matrix, real-rate stance, primary-balance direction, debt-service pressure, external constraint.
- **Sovereign Issuance and Private-Sector Absorption:** net duration supply, auction demand, dealer inventories, foreign holdings, money-market substitution.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
