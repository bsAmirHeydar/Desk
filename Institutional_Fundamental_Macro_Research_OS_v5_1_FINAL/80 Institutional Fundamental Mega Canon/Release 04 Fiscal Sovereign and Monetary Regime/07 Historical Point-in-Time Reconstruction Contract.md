# Fiscal, Sovereign and Monetary Regime — Historical Point-in-Time Reconstruction Contract

## Hard cutoff

The analysis may use only information released and reasonably accessible before the specified timestamp. Revised values, later constituent changes, later policy knowledge and observed outcomes are prohibited until a separate ex-post section.

## Reconstruction packet

- publication calendar and time-zone normalization;
- vintage data and contemporaneous methodology;
- market and expectations snapshot;
- institutional and balance-sheet state;
- rival models available at the time;
- ex-ante scenario distribution and unknowns;
- archive gaps and confidence penalty;
- separate ex-post attribution.
