# Global Claim–Evidence and Unknowns Contract

Every material statement is labeled as observation, published estimate, market-implied estimate, model inference, judgment, scenario assumption or unknown. Required fields are source, locator, release time, vintage, transformation, relevant horizon, supporting mechanism, opposing evidence and confidence ceiling.

An unknown is not a blank. It records why the information is missing, whether it is measurable, what proxy is available, what decisions it constrains and which future event may resolve it.
