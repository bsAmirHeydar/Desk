# Cross-Asset, Geopolitics and Structural Systems — Causal Map and Rival Models

## Causal discipline

The release must distinguish structural drivers, cyclical impulses, policy reactions, market expectations and balance-sheet feedback. The same observation may have different effects depending on who holds the exposure, how it is financed and what was priced in advance.

## Canonical causal sequence

```text
Observed or estimated state
→ expectations revision
→ policy, cash-flow, physical-balance or funding channel
→ intermediary and balance-sheet response
→ valuation, carry, liquidity and risk-premium change
→ cross-domain confirmation or contradiction
→ scenario payoff distribution
```

## Rival-model requirement

For every major conclusion, document at least three competing interpretations: a real-economy or cash-flow explanation; a balance-sheet, supply or institutional explanation; and a expectations, liquidity or risk-premium explanation. Add structural-break and measurement-error models when material.

## Driver axes

- discount-rate transmission
- cash-flow and growth transmission
- funding and collateral
- risk premium and volatility
- trade and supply-chain disruption
- resource and energy security
- demographic and productivity trend
- institutional credibility
