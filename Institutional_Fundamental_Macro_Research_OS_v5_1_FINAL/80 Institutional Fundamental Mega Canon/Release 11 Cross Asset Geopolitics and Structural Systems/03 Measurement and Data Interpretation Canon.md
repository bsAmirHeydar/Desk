# Cross-Asset, Geopolitics and Structural Systems — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Cross-Asset Transmission Matrix:** leader-lag map, beta by regime, correlation change, spread response, confirmation hierarchy.
- **Relative Value and Common-Driver Decomposition:** hedged residual, factor-neutral spread, z-score with regime control, carry-adjusted value, basis stability.
- **Global Financial Cycle and Risk Appetite:** broad dollar, global credit spreads, cross-border credit, volatility indexes, EM flow.
- **Geopolitical Shock Taxonomy:** physical supply loss, policy action, insurance and freight, market closure, escalation indicators.
- **Sanctions Economic Warfare and Financial Channels:** trade rerouting, payment flows, discounts and premiums, technology access, compliance costs.
- **Tariffs Trade Policy and Supply-Chain Reallocation:** import prices, trade volumes, supplier relocation, margin impact, effective tariff rate.
- **Energy Security and Strategic Reserves:** import dependence, days of cover, spare capacity, infrastructure outage, energy intensity.
- **Climate Physical Risk and Transition Policy:** hazard exposure, insured loss, carbon price, capital expenditure, stranded-asset risk.
- **Demographics Migration and Dependency:** working-age growth, dependency ratio, participation, household formation, pension burden.
- **Institutions Political Economy and Credibility:** policy volatility, rule changes, tax capacity, contract enforcement, risk premium.
- **Technology AI Productivity and Capital Cycle:** productivity measures, investment intensity, capacity utilization, adoption, returns on capital.
- **Supply-Chain Resilience and Strategic Materials:** concentration indexes, lead times, inventory cover, capacity pipeline, trade rerouting.
- **Tail Systems and Nonlinear Regime Change:** threshold indicators, scenario signposts, contagion paths, option tail price, policy capacity.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
