# Retrieval, Source Canon, Integration and Final Adversarial Audit — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Institutional Query Taxonomy:** object completeness, cutoff clarity, horizon definition, output contract, missing input.
- **Vault Retrieval and Domain Selection:** retrieval coverage, irrelevant retrieval, missing domain, link integrity, evidence diversity.
- **Claim Evidence Lineage and Citation Locators:** citation completeness, source diversity, vintage integrity, unsupported claim, conflicting source.
- **Source Hierarchy and Reliability:** authority, method transparency, timeliness, revision policy, conflict of interest.
- **Contradiction and Rival-Model Registry:** evidence weight, model-specific prediction, resolution trigger, residual uncertainty, confidence impact.
- **Unknowns and Uncertainty Ledger:** unknown severity, sensitivity, confidence ceiling, required update, fallback scenario.
- **Current Analysis Output Contract:** completeness, source freshness, horizon separation, decision relevance, updateability.
- **Historical Point-in-Time Output Contract:** lookahead violations, archive gaps, revision leakage, outcome contamination, reconstruction confidence.
- **Asset Market and Relative-Value Output Standard:** driver hierarchy, valuation gap, scenario payoff, cross-asset confirmation, risk asymmetry.
- **Country Sovereign and Regional Output Standard:** country state vector, funding need, currency risk, policy capacity, historical regime.
- **Corporate and Equity Output Standard:** earnings quality, cash conversion, ROIC, revision trend, implied expectations.
- **Commodity Physical-Market Output Standard:** balance residual, inventory signal, curve and basis, logistics risk, policy effect.
- **Portfolio Exposure and Scenario Output Standard:** factor concentration, scenario loss, hedge reliability, liquidity need, correlation break.
- **Prompt and Agent Instruction Standard:** retrieval proof, citation quality, unknown handling, hallucination control, completion check.
- **Coverage Matrix and Knowledge Graph Audit:** coverage gap, orphan note, broken link, duplicate content, unsupported output.
- **Anti-Boilerplate and Depth Audit:** duplicate ratio, topic-specific density, source specificity, equation uniqueness, case completeness.
- **Final Ten-of-Ten Adversarial Audit:** critical gap count, contradiction handling, retrieval success, unsupported claim rate, adversarial pass.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
