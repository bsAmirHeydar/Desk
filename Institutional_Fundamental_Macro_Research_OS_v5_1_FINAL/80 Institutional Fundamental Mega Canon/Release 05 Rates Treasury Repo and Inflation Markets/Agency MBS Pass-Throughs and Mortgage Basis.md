# Agency MBS Pass-Throughs and Mortgage Basis

## Economic object

Agency mortgage-backed securities transform pools of fixed-rate mortgages into pass-through cash flows with government-agency credit support but borrower prepayment optionality. The central risk is not ordinary default; it is uncertain timing and negative convexity.

## Cash-flow mechanics

Scheduled principal and interest are passed through net of servicing and guarantee fees. Borrowers may prepay through refinancing, home sale, curtailment or default-related liquidation. Prepayment changes average life, duration, convexity and reinvestment risk.

## State vector

- mortgage rate versus borrower coupon distribution;
- refinancing incentive and burnout;
- housing turnover, home prices and credit availability;
- seasoning, loan size, geography and servicer behavior;
- prepayment speeds and model dispersion;
- specified-pool attributes and TBA deliverability;
- Treasury and swap volatility;
- bank, REIT, insurer, foreign and central-bank demand;
- dealer balance sheet and dollar-roll specialness.

## Negative convexity

When rates fall, prepayments accelerate and duration shortens; when rates rise, prepayments slow and duration extends. Hedgers may buy duration into rallies and sell duration into selloffs, amplifying rate moves. The effect depends on outstanding coupon distribution and hedge behavior.

## TBA and specified pools

The to-be-announced market creates fungibility through standardized delivery, while specified pools trade for prepayment characteristics. Analyze cheapest-to-deliver economics, pay-ups, settlement month, dollar rolls, implied financing and fails.

## Mortgage basis

The option-adjusted spread must compensate for prepayment uncertainty, volatility, liquidity, financing and model risk. Compare MBS with Treasuries and swaps using consistent duration and option assumptions. A wider nominal spread may not be cheap if volatility or adverse convexity has risen.

## Institutional transmission

Banks evaluate capital, liquidity and deposit duration; mortgage REITs use leverage and repo; insurers value asset-liability matching; foreign buyers face currency hedging; the Federal Reserve can alter float and convexity distribution through holdings and runoff.

## Current-analysis contract

Separate rate-level, volatility, supply, bank demand, Fed runoff, prepayment and financing effects. Identify whether the mortgage basis move is macro, option, technical-supply or balance-sheet driven.

## Historical contract

Use the contemporaneous mortgage universe, Fed portfolio, volatility surface, refinancing incentive and prepayment estimates. Current borrower coupons cannot be projected backward.
