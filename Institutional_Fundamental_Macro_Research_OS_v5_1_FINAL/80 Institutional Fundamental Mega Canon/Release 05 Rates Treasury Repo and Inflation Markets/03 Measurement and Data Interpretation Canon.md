# Rates, Treasury, Repo and Inflation Markets — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **OIS Policy Path and Meeting-Dated Pricing:** implied meeting moves, terminal rate, cuts or hikes priced, distribution skew, policy-path revision.
- **Yield Curve Decomposition and Forward Rates:** 2s10s and 5s30s, level-slope-curvature, forward-rate shifts, butterflies, curve residuals.
- **Term Premium Models and Interpretation:** ACM-style estimates, survey residual, rate volatility, net duration supply, foreign demand.
- **Real Rates and Inflation Compensation:** real curve, breakeven curve, swap-breakeven basis, inflation risk premium, seasonality.
- **Treasury Refunding Issuance and Duration Supply:** net coupon supply, weighted duration, gross versus net issuance, dealer takedown, Treasury cash balance.
- **Treasury Auctions and Demand Quality:** tail or stop-through, bid-to-cover, dealer share, indirect share, secondary-market follow-through.
- **Repo Collateral and Funding Conditions:** SOFR and repo dispersion, specialness, fails, dealer financing, quarter-end pressure.
- **Swap Spreads and Balance-Sheet Intermediation:** swap-spread curve, issuance calendar, repo conditions, bank CDS, duration hedging.
- **Treasury Futures CTD and Net Basis:** CTD switch points, gross basis, net basis, implied repo rate, DV01 hedge ratio.
- **Duration Convexity and Key-Rate Risk:** parallel and nonparallel P&L, bucketed DV01, convexity contribution, carry and roll-down, hedge residual.
- **Mortgage Convexity and Hedging Flows:** current coupon, option-adjusted spread, duration profile, mortgage-Treasury spread, hedging flow proxies.
- **Sovereign Relative Value and Curve Expression:** z-spread and residual, carry-roll, repo-adjusted value, liquidity premium, hedge stability.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
