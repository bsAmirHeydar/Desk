# Mega Canon Completion and Residual Open Questions

## Completed

Releases 02–14 now have dedicated domain monographs, state vectors, causal maps, measurement canons, regime atlases, cross-domain matrices, current contracts, historical contracts, scenario systems and ten-of-ten audits.

## Residual open questions

Some questions cannot be made final by any static knowledge base: the true neutral rate, exact term premium, causal effect of policy under regime change, unobserved dealer inventories, future productivity from new technologies, climate-tail probabilities, geopolitical escalation and the correct probability of structural breaks. The Vault records competing models and evidence rather than pretending these are solved.

## Maintenance rule

A canon is evergreen only if source methodologies, institutional rules, market structure and historical evidence are updated. Revisions must preserve previous versions when they change a historical interpretation.
