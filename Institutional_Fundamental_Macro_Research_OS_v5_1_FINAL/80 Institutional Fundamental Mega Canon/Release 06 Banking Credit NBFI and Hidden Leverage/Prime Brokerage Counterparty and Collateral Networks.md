# Prime Brokerage, Counterparty and Collateral Networks

## System role

Prime brokers provide financing, custody, securities lending, derivatives, execution and operational services to leveraged clients. Risk is networked across client portfolios, legal entities, collateral pools, central counterparties and dealers.

## Exposure measurement

Gross notional is not economic exposure. Measure current replacement cost, potential future exposure, netting set, collateral, initial and variation margin, wrong-way risk, concentration and close-out liquidity.

## Client leverage

Analyze financing by product, haircut, rehypothecation, synthetic versus cash exposure, crowded positions and liquidity. Family-office, hedge-fund or relative-value leverage can be hidden across multiple primes.

## Margin spiral

Price decline → collateral call → forced deleveraging → wider basis and lower liquidity → further price decline. The mechanism is stronger when positions are common, collateral is correlated and dealers reduce balance sheet simultaneously.

## Legal and operational issues

Review master agreements, cross-default, termination rights, collateral eligibility, jurisdiction, segregation and settlement. Apparent diversification across counterparties may fail if they share clearing or funding channels.

## Current analysis

Use disclosed counterparty data, regulatory reports, basis, funding rates, securities lending, margin changes and dealer commentary where reliable. Never infer exact client positions without evidence.
