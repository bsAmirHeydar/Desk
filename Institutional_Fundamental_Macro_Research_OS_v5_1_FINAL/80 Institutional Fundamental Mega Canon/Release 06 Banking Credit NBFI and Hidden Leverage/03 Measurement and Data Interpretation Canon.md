# Banking, Credit, NBFI and Hidden Leverage — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Bank Balance-Sheet Mechanics:** loan growth, deposit beta and mix, net interest margin, capital ratios, liquidity coverage.
- **Deposits Money Creation and Funding Competition:** deposit flows, noninterest-bearing share, wholesale funding, cash and securities sales, deposit cost.
- **Bank Lending Standards and Credit Supply:** SLOOS standards, bank loan growth, survey demand, credit line usage, small-business access.
- **Credit Cycle and Financial Accelerator:** credit-to-income growth, debt-service ratios, lending standards, asset-price collateral, refinancing needs.
- **Corporate Credit Spreads and Relative Risk:** option-adjusted spreads, spread dispersion, rating migration, new-issue concession, equity-credit divergence.
- **Default Recovery and Distress Dynamics:** distress ratio, default rate, recovery rate, interest coverage, maturity concentration.
- **Private Credit Structure and Opacity:** reported yield, nonaccruals, payment-in-kind income, leverage, valuation marks.
- **NBFI System and Liquidity Transformation:** redemptions, liquidity buffers, derivative margin, asset sales, funding maturity.
- **Hidden Leverage and Derivative Exposures:** gross and net exposure, margin requirement, haircuts, counterparty concentration, basis positions.
- **Margin Liquidity Spirals and Fire Sales:** margin calls, funding spreads, market depth, asset-sale volume, cross-market correlation.
- **Contagion Networks and Counterparty Risk:** counterparty concentration, interbank spreads, clearing-member stress, common-ownership overlap, liquidity hoarding.
- **Commercial Real Estate and Bank Stress:** vacancy and rents, cap rates, transaction prices, maturity wall, nonperforming loans.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
