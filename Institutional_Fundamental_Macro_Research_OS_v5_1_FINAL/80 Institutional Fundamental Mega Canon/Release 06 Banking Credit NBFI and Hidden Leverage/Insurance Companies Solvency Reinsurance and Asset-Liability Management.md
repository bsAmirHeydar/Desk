# Insurance Companies, Solvency, Reinsurance and Asset-Liability Management

## Business model

Insurers exchange current premiums for contingent future claims. Fundamental analysis must integrate underwriting, reserving, asset returns, capital regulation, reinsurance, liquidity and the duration/options embedded in liabilities.

## Sector distinctions

- life and annuity insurers: long-duration guarantees, lapse behavior, spread income and asset-liability matching;
- property and casualty: pricing cycles, catastrophe exposure, loss inflation and reserve development;
- health: medical cost trend, utilization, regulation and risk adjustment;
- reinsurers: catastrophe aggregation, retrocession, pricing and collateral;
- financial guaranty and specialty lines: concentrated tail and correlation risks.

## Liability analysis

Assess claim frequency and severity, policy guarantees, surrender and lapse options, duration, inflation sensitivity, catastrophe exposure, reserve assumptions and adverse development. Reported reserves are estimates and can create delayed recognition.

## Asset analysis

Map credit quality, duration, liquidity, private assets, structured credit, commercial real estate, derivatives, securities lending and unrealized gains/losses. Book-value accounting can delay visible stress, while collateral and policyholder flows create real liquidity needs.

## Capital and solvency

Understand statutory accounting, risk-based capital, admissible assets, dividend restrictions, group support and Own Risk and Solvency Assessment. Regulatory capital is a constraint, not a complete economic-capital measure.

## Reinsurance

Evaluate counterparty credit, collateral, recoverables, contract wording, commutations, concentration, finite-risk features and whether reinsurance genuinely transfers risk. Reinsurance can reduce volatility but add counterparty and basis risk.

## Asset-liability management

Stress interest rates, credit spreads, equity, volatility, lapse, claims and liquidity jointly. Duration matching can fail when liability options change with rates or market stress. Analyze hedging cost and basis.

## Current-analysis contract

Separate underwriting margin, reserve development, investment spread, capital, liquidity and regulatory constraints. Identify whether observed pressure is economic, accounting, collateral or confidence-driven.
