# Real Assets, Digital Assets, Derivatives and Market-Implied Information — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Housing Real Estate and Cap-Rate System:** NOI growth, vacancy, transaction cap rate, debt-service coverage, loan maturity.
- **REIT Infrastructure and Regulated Assets:** funds from operations, same-store growth, occupancy, cost of capital, capital pipeline.
- **Private Assets Valuation and Liquidity:** PME and IRR limits, NAV change, cash-flow multiple, discount to NAV, unfunded commitments.
- **Bitcoin Monetary Network and Liquidity Regimes:** realized capitalization, ETF flows, funding rates, open interest, long-term holder supply.
- **Stablecoins Crypto Credit and Settlement:** supply growth, redemptions, peg stability, on-chain velocity, lending rates.
- **Futures Options and Contract Economics:** open interest, basis and roll, margin requirement, implied financing, deliverable supply.
- **Implied Volatility Surface and Term Structure:** surface level, risk reversal, butterfly, calendar spread, variance term structure.
- **Risk-Neutral Distribution and Market-Implied Scenarios:** implied skewness, kurtosis, tail probability, variance risk premium, distribution shifts.
- **Dealer Gamma Vanna Charm and Hedging:** strike concentration, estimated gamma, volatility move, time-to-expiry, hedging-flow scenarios.
- **Volatility Risk Premium and Convexity Supply:** implied-realized spread, variance swap level, skew premium, tail hedge cost, option-selling flows.
- **Systematic Flows CTA Risk Parity and Vol Control:** estimated exposure, volatility trigger, correlation shock, trend break, rebalance schedule.
- **Market Liquidity Depth and Price Impact:** spread, depth, price impact, turnover, recovery time.
- **ETF Creation Redemption and Index Flow:** premium-discount, shares outstanding, basket liquidity, rebalance flow, tracking error.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
