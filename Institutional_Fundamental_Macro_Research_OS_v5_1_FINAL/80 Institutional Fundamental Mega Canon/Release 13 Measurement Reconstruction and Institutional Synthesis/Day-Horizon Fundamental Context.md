# Day-Horizon Fundamental Context

> [!abstract] Analytical purpose
> Day-horizon analysis focuses on inherited state, overnight repricing, scheduled events, funding, flows and the expected half-life of information. It remains fundamental and market-structure based.

The objective is not to memorize terminology; it is to make the domain retrievable and usable in current, historical, cross-asset and scenario analysis. ## Competing models

- A demand-led interpretation in which overnight information is the dominant state variable.
- A supply, balance-sheet or institutional interpretation in which calendar and policy events constrains adjustment.
- A market-pricing interpretation in which the observed move primarily reflects prior expectations, positioning, liquidity or risk premium rather than a change in underlying state.
- A structural-break interpretation in which historical elasticities no longer transport because policy, technology, regulation or market structure has changed.

A final synthesis may assign different weights to the models, but it must not silently merge mutually inconsistent assumptions.

## Regime dependence

- **Day-Horizon Fundamental Context in expansion with benign funding and anchored expectations:** emphasize persistence, capacity and valuation rather than crisis channels.
- **Day-Horizon Fundamental Context in a late-cycle or inflation-constrained regime:** emphasize policy reaction, financing cost, margin pressure and nonlinear sensitivity.
- **Day-Horizon Fundamental Context in contraction or deleveraging:** emphasize liquidity, balance sheets, default, forced adjustment and policy backstops.
- **Day-Horizon Fundamental Context under a supply or geopolitical shock:** separate physical loss and substitution from nominal repricing and policy response.
- **Day-Horizon Fundamental Context during financial stress:** prioritize funding, collateral, market depth and institutional capacity over average historical relationships.

## Multihorizon interpretation

- **Structural:** assess whether day-horizon fundamental context changes productive capacity, institutional architecture, demographics, resource security or long-run risk premia.
- **Cyclical:** determine its relation to growth, inflation, credit and policy over quarters.
- **Tactical:** identify the expectations or valuation gap that could close over weeks.
- **Multi-day:** require a persistent information change, a catalyst sequence and a defined expiry.
- **Day horizon:** isolate newly available information, the leading transmission market and the expected half-life without importing unrelated short-horizon noise.

## Cross-domain and cross-asset translation

- **Base state for Day-Horizon Fundamental Context:** the modal continuation under current policy, balance-sheet and market assumptions.
- **Alternative state for Day-Horizon Fundamental Context:** a plausible change in one or more key drivers with explicit signposts.
- **Adverse tail for Day-Horizon Fundamental Context:** a nonlinear funding, policy, supply or confidence event that changes historical relationships.
- **Benign tail for Day-Horizon Fundamental Context:** an upside combination of capacity, productivity, credibility, funding or supply normalization.
- **Residual model risk for Day-Horizon Fundamental Context:** the risk that the model class is wrong even if all measured inputs are accurate.

## Failure modes and red-team checks

- For **Day-Horizon Fundamental Context**, confusing a level with its rate of change or an annualized short-run rate with a year-over-year rate.
- Treating an estimate, proxy or model output as a directly observed fact.
- Using revised data or later-known outcomes in a historical information set.
- Assuming a stable coefficient across regimes, countries, instruments or horizons.
- Double-counting the same underlying shock through correlated indicators.
- Ignoring financing, liquidity, policy response or institutional constraints when translating state to payoff.
- Replacing a missing measurement with narrative certainty instead of recording an unknown.
