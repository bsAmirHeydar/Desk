# Measurement, Reconstruction and Institutional Synthesis — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Nowcasting Architecture and Ragged Edge:** nowcast distribution, release contribution, forecast revision, interval width, model dispersion.
- **State-Space Models and Kalman Filtering:** filtered and smoothed state, innovation, likelihood, state uncertainty, diagnostics.
- **Dynamic Factor Models:** factor estimates, variance explained, news decomposition, loading stability, forecast performance.
- **MIDAS Bridge and Mixed-Frequency Regression:** forecast error, lag weights, indicator contribution, real-time performance, stability.
- **VAR BVAR and Structural Identification:** impulse response, historical decomposition, forecast distribution, posterior uncertainty, identification sensitivity.
- **Local Projections and State-Dependent Effects:** response path, cumulative effect, state difference, standard error, robustness.
- **Panel Models Instruments and Natural Experiments:** treatment effect, pretrend, first stage, heterogeneity, external validity.
- **Event Studies and Market Response Measurement:** abnormal return, rate and FX response, volume and spread, reversal, cross-asset confirmation.
- **Density Forecasts Calibration and Scoring:** Brier score, log score, coverage, PIT histogram, decision-weighted loss.
- **Point-in-Time Historical Reconstruction:** available information, missing archive, revision delta, cutoff integrity, ex-post separation.
- **Current Full-Spectrum Analysis Synthesis:** horizon conclusions, driver hierarchy, confidence, invalidation, update triggers.
- **Day-Horizon Fundamental Context:** session state, event risk, leader confirmation, expiry, no-deployment conditions.
- **Multi-Day Fundamental Context:** half-life, catalyst map, expected repricing, overnight risk, expiry.
- **Scenario Analysis Wargaming and Tail States:** probability range, scenario payoff, trigger, second-order effect, residual risk.
- **Portfolio Fundamental Exposure and Hidden Drivers:** factor exposure, scenario P&L, correlation under stress, liquidity concentration, hedge failure.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
