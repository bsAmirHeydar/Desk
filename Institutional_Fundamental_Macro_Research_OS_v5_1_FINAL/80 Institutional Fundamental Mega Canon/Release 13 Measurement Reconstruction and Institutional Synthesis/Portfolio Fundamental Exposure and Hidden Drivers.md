## Competing models

- A demand-led interpretation in which growth and inflation beta is the dominant state variable.
- A supply, balance-sheet or institutional interpretation in which rate and dollar exposure constrains adjustment.
- A market-pricing interpretation in which the observed move primarily reflects prior expectations, positioning, liquidity or risk premium rather than a change in underlying state.
- A structural-break interpretation in which historical elasticities no longer transport because policy, technology, regulation or market structure has changed.

A final synthesis may assign different weights to the models, but it must not silently merge mutually inconsistent assumptions.

## Regime dependence

- **Portfolio Fundamental Exposure and Hidden Drivers in expansion with benign funding and anchored expectations:** emphasize persistence, capacity and valuation rather than crisis channels.
- **Portfolio Fundamental Exposure and Hidden Drivers in a late-cycle or inflation-constrained regime:** emphasize policy reaction, financing cost, margin pressure and nonlinear sensitivity.
- **Portfolio Fundamental Exposure and Hidden Drivers in contraction or deleveraging:** emphasize liquidity, balance sheets, default, forced adjustment and policy backstops.

## Failure modes and red-team checks

- For **Portfolio Fundamental Exposure and Hidden Drivers**, confusing a level with its rate of change or an annualized short-run rate with a year-over-year rate.
- Treating an estimate, proxy or model output as a directly observed fact.
