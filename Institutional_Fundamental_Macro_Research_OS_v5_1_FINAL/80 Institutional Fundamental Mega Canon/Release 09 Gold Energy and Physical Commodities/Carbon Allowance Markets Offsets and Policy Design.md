# Carbon Allowance Markets, Offsets and Policy Design

## Instruments

Distinguish compliance allowances, voluntary offsets, renewable certificates and carbon taxes. They have different legal claims, supply rules, quality and demand.

## Cap-and-trade state vector

Cap trajectory, auction schedule, free allocation, banking, borrowing, reserve mechanisms, sector coverage, emissions, power and fuel switching, industrial output, weather, hedging and policy credibility.

## Allowance supply

Headline cap is not current float. Account for banking, market-stability reserves, auction changes, free allocation, cancellation, new-sector entry and government intervention.

## Demand

Compliance demand depends on verified emissions and abatement choices. Utilities may hedge forward; industrials may respond more slowly. Fuel spreads and renewable output affect power-sector emissions.

## Offsets

Assess additionality, permanence, leakage, measurement, verification, registry, vintage, legal eligibility and reversal risk. Cheap offsets are not interchangeable with high-integrity compliance instruments.

## Policy risk

Carbon markets are policy-created and exposed to rule changes, price caps/floors, political backlash, border adjustment and industrial support. Treat policy reaction as endogenous to price and distributional effects.

## Analysis contract

Name jurisdiction and instrument, legal compliance use, cap mechanics, banked supply, marginal abatement channel, hedging structure and policy calendar.
