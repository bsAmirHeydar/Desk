# Gold, Energy and Physical Commodities — Measurement and Data Interpretation Canon

## Minimum metadata

Every series or qualitative observation must include definition, unit, frequency, seasonal treatment, release time, reference period, revision schedule, history availability, transformation, known methodological breaks and appropriate horizon.

## Interpretation hierarchy

1. Confirm the object measured and its institutional boundary.
2. Separate level, change, acceleration, breadth and distribution.
3. Compare with contemporaneous expectation and prior release.
4. Reconcile related accounts and stock-flow constraints.
5. Assess revisions, base effects and one-offs.
6. Translate the information through causal channels only after the measurement is understood.

## Core topic measurements

- **Gold Monetary and Physical Demand System:** real-yield beta, ETF holdings, CFTC positioning, central-bank purchases, physical premiums.
- **Silver Dual Monetary and Industrial Demand:** gold-silver ratio, fabrication demand, ETF holdings, mine supply, inventory.
- **Central-Bank Reserve Demand and Gold Allocation:** official reserves, purchase disclosures, trade flows, vault and customs data, reserve composition.
- **Gold ETF Futures and Physical Market Linkage:** ETF flows, open interest, basis, lease and swap rates, regional premiums.
- **Global Oil Balance and Barrel Accounting:** supply-demand balance, inventory change, refinery runs, exports and imports, floating storage.
- **Refining Product Markets and Crack Spreads:** crack spreads, refinery utilization, product stocks, margins, regional differentials.
- **Oil Inventories Storage and Curve Structure:** days of supply, inventory versus seasonal norm, front spreads, storage economics, physical premiums.
- **OPEC Spare Capacity and Supply Policy:** actual production, exports, spare capacity estimates, compliance, fiscal sensitivity.
- **Natural Gas LNG and Regional Balances:** storage versus norm, LNG feedgas, pipeline nominations, degree days, regional basis.
- **Power Markets Capacity and Fuel Switching:** spark and dark spreads, reserve margin, outages, nodal prices, fuel switching.
- **Copper Industrial Metals and China Demand:** exchange inventories, treatment charges, physical premiums, imports, manufacturing indicators.
- **Agriculture Weather and Balance Sheets:** stocks-to-use, crop condition, export sales, weather anomalies, basis.
- **Shipping Freight and Trade Bottlenecks:** freight indexes, port congestion, vessel tracking, orderbook, route distance.
- **Carbon Critical Minerals and Transition Constraints:** allowance curves, project pipeline, supply concentration, inventory, policy targets.

## Quality failures

The release fails if it uses revised historical data as if contemporaneous, treats a survey balance as a level, mixes nominal and real measures, ignores currency or unit changes, or combines dependent proxies as independent evidence.
