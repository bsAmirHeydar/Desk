# Global Multihorizon and Cross-Asset Synthesis Standard

## Horizon stack

- Structural: institutions, demographics, technology, resources and long-run balance sheets.
- Cyclical: growth, inflation, credit and policy over quarters.
- Tactical: expectations, valuation and policy repricing over weeks.
- Multi-day: persistent information change, catalyst path, carry and positioning.
- Day horizon: new information, session funding, market-implied response and information half-life.
- Event: surprise vector, immediate policy inference and cross-market confirmation.

## Conflict rule

Horizon conflict is reported, not averaged away. Lower-horizon information can change timing, confidence and scenario weight without automatically overturning the structural state.

## Cross-asset rule

Rates, FX, credit, equity, commodity, derivatives and funding evidence are integrated by causal role. Correlated reactions generated by one shock are one evidence family, not multiple independent confirmations.
