# Institutional Analysis Retrieval, Synthesis and Output Architecture

## Mission

Ensure that the completed canon can produce an institutional analysis rather than remain a passive library.

## Required capabilities

The Vault must support the following query classes:

- live full-spectrum market state;
- historical point-in-time reconstruction;
- single-asset driver and valuation analysis;
- cross-asset relative-value and transmission analysis;
- country and sovereign analysis;
- company, industry and equity analysis;
- commodity physical-balance analysis;
- policy, data-release and geopolitical-event analysis;
- portfolio hidden-exposure and scenario analysis;
- structural regime and long-horizon analysis.

## Retrieval architecture

Every query must identify:

1. the analysis object;
2. the cutoff and information set;
3. the primary and secondary horizons;
4. the governing country, institution and market structure;
5. the relevant domain MOCs and source contracts;
6. the required competing models;
7. the relevant historical analogues;
8. the minimum cross-asset confirmation set;
9. the required contradiction and unknown ledgers;
10. the final output contract.

## Synthesis architecture

A complete synthesis separates:

- observed state;
- estimated latent state;
- expectations and distribution;
- market pricing and valuation;
- causal transmission;
- institutional constraints;
- positioning and liquidity;
- scenarios and tails;
- horizon-specific conclusions;
- confidence, invalidation, expiry and unknowns.

## Acceptance gate

Stage 52 is complete when each major domain can be retrieved through a deterministic map and combined into each analysis object without missing a required layer or importing irrelevant boilerplate.
