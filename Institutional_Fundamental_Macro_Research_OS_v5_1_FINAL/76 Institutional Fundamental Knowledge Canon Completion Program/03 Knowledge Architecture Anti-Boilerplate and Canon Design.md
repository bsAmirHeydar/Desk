# Stage 03 — Knowledge Architecture Anti-Boilerplate and Canon Design

## Mission

Restructure the Vault so common standards are centralized and every subject note contains unique knowledge.

## Why this stage is necessary

For **Stage 03 — Knowledge Architecture Anti-Boilerplate and Canon Design**, a billion-dollar institutional reference requires an analysis-ready canon rather than disconnected definitions or repeated templates. This stage must make the subject retrievable, causally explicit, historically grounded, cross-domain integrated and capable of populating current and historical institutional reports without generic filler.

## Knowledge domains to extract and expand

- **Moc Hierarchy** — define the concept, explain its mechanisms, document measurement, competing views, historical evidence, market implications and limitations.
- **Canonical Definitions** — define the concept, explain its mechanisms, document measurement, competing views, historical evidence, market implications and limitations.
- **Shared Standards Versus Subject Monographs** — define the concept, explain its mechanisms, document measurement, competing views, historical evidence, market implications and limitations.
- **Anti-Duplication Rules** — define the concept, explain its mechanisms, document measurement, competing views, historical evidence, market implications and limitations.
- **Cross-Linking And Retrieval** — define the concept, explain its mechanisms, document measurement, competing views, historical evidence, market implications and limitations.
- **Editorial Consistency** — define the concept, explain its mechanisms, document measurement, competing views, historical evidence, market implications and limitations.

## Required depth layers

Every subject created or rewritten in this stage must contain all of the following layers:

1. **Beginner intuition** — a clear explanation that assumes no prior specialist knowledge.
2. **Institutional definition** — precise language, terminology, units, conventions and scope.
3. **Formal structure** — identities, equations, models, diagrams and derivations where relevant.
4. **Competing explanations** — rival schools, alternative assumptions and unresolved debates.
5. **Measurement architecture** — how the concept is observed, estimated or proxied; revisions and data limitations.
6. **Causal mechanism** — step-by-step transmission rather than unsupported correlation.
7. **Historical evidence** — major regimes, turning points, successes, failures and structural breaks.
8. **Cross-market integration** — implications for rates, FX, equities, credit, commodities, volatility and liquidity where relevant.
9. **Cross-country variation** — how institutions, regimes and market structures alter the mechanism.
10. **Multihorizon interpretation** — structural, cyclical, tactical, multi-day, daily, event and microstructure consequences.
11. **Failure modes** — conditions under which the model breaks, reverses or becomes uninformative.
12. **Learning apparatus** — glossary, examples, questions, exercises, source ladder and cross-links.

## Required Vault artifacts

This stage is complete only after the Vault contains:

- an analysis-ready master canon for **Stage 03 — Knowledge Architecture Anti-Boilerplate and Canon Design**;
- an explicit state vector and driver hierarchy;
- a causal map with competing models and observable discriminators;
- formal identities, equations and measurement conventions where applicable;
- a point-in-time measurement and revision standard;
- a regime and historical evidence atlas;
- cross-asset, cross-country and institutional transmission matrices;
- current and historical report contracts;
- scenario, contradiction, unknown and failure registers;
- claim-level source routes and exact evidence requirements;
- links to all upstream dependencies and downstream analysis objects.

## Explicit exclusions

The boundary for **Stage 03 — Knowledge Architecture Anti-Boilerplate and Canon Design** is governed by [[76 Institutional Fundamental Knowledge Canon Completion Program/02 Fundamental-Only Boundary and Removal of Non-Fundamental Methods]]. Software deployment, pedagogy and non-fundamental methods are outside the core knowledge-completion score.

## Ten-of-ten exit audit

- No subject note may repeat generic research boilerplate.
- Common standards are written once and embedded or linked.
- Each monograph must add topic-specific mechanisms, models, evidence, controversies, history and applications.
- The architecture must support both linear learning and non-linear institutional retrieval.
- A beginner can learn the subject in sequence without external prerequisites being silently assumed.
- A specialist can locate formal definitions, models, controversies, historical cases and primary sources without encountering generic filler.
- The subject is connected to all relevant assets, countries and time horizons.
- The final note explicitly lists any unresolved research frontier; an unresolved frontier is acceptable, but an unacknowledged gap is not.
