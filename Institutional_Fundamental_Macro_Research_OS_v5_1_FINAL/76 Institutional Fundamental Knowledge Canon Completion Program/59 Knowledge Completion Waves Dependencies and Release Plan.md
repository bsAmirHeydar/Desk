# Knowledge Completion Waves, Dependencies and Release Plan

## Release philosophy

The remaining program is organized around analytical capability, not lesson sequencing. Each release must complete a coherent group of domains that jointly support a class of institutional questions.

## Release bundles

### Release 01 — Fundamental inference foundations — completed

Stages 01–07. Provides ontology, evidence, causality, uncertainty, competing models and multihorizon synthesis.

### Release 02 — Macro accounting, growth and system state — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 08–11. Completes national accounts, sector balances, flow of funds, growth, cycles, productivity, capital and innovation. Output target: complete macro-state decomposition.

### Release 03 — Inflation, labor, income and demand — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 12–13. Output target: inflation and demand regime analysis with component, persistence, wage, productivity and distributional channels.

### Release 04 — Fiscal, sovereign and monetary regime — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 14–17. Output target: integrated fiscal-monetary-policy analysis, sovereign credibility and financial-condition transmission.

### Release 05 — Rates, inflation markets and sovereign plumbing — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 18–20 and relevant Stage 30 material. Output target: policy path, yield-curve, term-premium, inflation-compensation, Treasury, repo, collateral and relative-value analysis.

### Release 06 — Banking, credit, NBFI and hidden leverage — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 21–23. Output target: balance-sheet, credit-cycle, default, funding, liquidity and contagion analysis.

### Release 07 — Global dollar, external accounts, FX and emerging markets — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 24–26. Output target: global funding, balance-of-payments, currency valuation, capital-flow and sovereign-vulnerability analysis.

### Release 08 — Corporate, equity and industry fundamentals — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 27–29. Output target: company, sector and equity-index analysis integrating accounting, business economics, valuation, financing and expectations.

### Release 09 — Monetary metals and physical commodities — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 31–33. May be divided into metals and energy/physical-systems releases. Output target: complete physical-balance, monetary-demand, logistics, policy and financialization analysis.

### Release 10 — Real assets, digital assets, derivatives and market-implied information — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 34–37. Output target: real-asset, digital-asset, volatility, derivatives, positioning, liquidity and systematic-flow analysis.

### Release 11 — Cross-asset, geopolitics and structural systems — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 38–41. Output target: cross-asset transmission, relative value, sanctions, trade, climate, resources, demographics and institutional-regime analysis.

### Release 12 — Country books and historical regime atlas — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 42–43. Output target: country/region analysis and point-in-time historical analogue system.

### Release 13 — Measurement, reconstruction and institutional synthesis — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 44–51. Output target: forecasting, causal research, current analysis, historical reconstruction, day-horizon, multi-day, scenario and portfolio outputs.

### Release 14 — Retrieval, source canon, integration and final adversarial audit — completed in Mega Canon v7.0

Stages 52–57. Output target: complete analysis-output architecture, source and citation canon, cross-link consistency, contradiction audit and final 10/10 scoring.

## Acceptance rule for every release

A release is complete only when:

- every material analysis object can retrieve its domain knowledge;
- all required cross-domain links exist;
- output templates can be populated without generic filler;
- historical and current interpretations are separated;
- contradictions and residual gaps are explicit;
- the ten-of-ten scorecard is passed by evidence, not assertion.
