# Mission, Scope and the Ten-of-Ten Knowledge Standard

## Mission

Build a fundamental-only research canon that can support comprehensive institutional analysis across economic systems, financial systems, markets, countries, companies, commodities, events and portfolios.

The mission is **not** to maximize instructional completeness. The Vault must function as a research intelligence base whose content can be retrieved, challenged and synthesized into an answer at the required horizon and information set.

## Scope

The canon includes all knowledge required to explain and assess:

- macroeconomic states and transitions;
- accounting identities and sector balance sheets;
- institutions, incentives and policy reaction functions;
- money, rates, credit, liquidity and global funding;
- corporate economics, financial statements and valuation;
- physical commodity balances and infrastructure constraints;
- market-implied expectations, positioning and risk premia;
- cross-country and cross-asset transmission;
- geopolitics, structural forces and regime change;
- current analysis, historical reconstruction and scenario intelligence;
- portfolio-level hidden drivers and contingent risk.

## Ten-of-ten standard

Ten-of-ten is an **analysis capability claim**, not a writing-quality or teaching claim. It requires:

1. exhaustive object coverage without generic filler;
2. explicit definitions, units, timing and boundary conditions;
3. integrated accounting, institutional, behavioral and causal layers;
4. rival models and discriminating evidence;
5. measurement construction and data limitations;
6. historical, cross-country and regime evidence;
7. expectations, valuation and payoff mapping;
8. horizon-specific interpretation;
9. scenario, nonlinear and tail analysis;
10. contradiction, uncertainty and unknown management;
11. claim-level source lineage;
12. retrieval and synthesis readiness for institutional outputs.

A domain fails ten-of-ten if it is merely named, summarized, repeated from a template, detached from its transmission channels, or unable to answer object-specific questions.

## Clarity versus pedagogy

Clarity remains mandatory. Beginner-oriented sequencing, exercises and exams are optional. They may expose gaps but cannot substitute for analytical depth.

## Completion evidence

Every domain must publish:

- a coverage map;
- an object and variable ontology;
- model and mechanism maps;
- measurement and source maps;
- historical and regime matrices;
- cross-asset and horizon maps;
- contradiction and unknown registers;
- an institutional analysis-output checklist;
- an adversarial gap audit.
