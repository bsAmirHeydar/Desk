## Base Effects

Year-over-year change depends on the comparison month:

\[
\text{YoY}_t = \frac{X_t}{X_{t-12}} - 1
\]

A low or high base can move YoY inflation/growth even when current monthly momentum is unchanged.

Track:

- month-over-month;
- three-month annualized;
- six-month annualized;
- year-over-year;
- contribution of base effects.

## Seasonal Adjustment

Seasonal adjustment estimates recurring patterns. Problems can occur when:

- the seasonal pattern changes;
- holidays move;
- shocks distort historical relationships;
- annual factors are updated;
- sample sizes are small;
- residual seasonality remains.

## Annualization

A monthly change annualized assumes repetition:

\[
(1+m)^{12}-1
\]

This can exaggerate a volatile month. Use it as a momentum lens, not a forecast.

## Composition Effects

Aggregate inflation, wages, productivity, or employment can change because the composition of workers, sectors, or products changes. Ask whether the average moved because each component changed or because weights changed.
