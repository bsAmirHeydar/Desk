## Structural Context

### Questions

- What is potential growth?
- What are demographic and productivity trends?
- Is fiscal capacity improving or deteriorating?
- How is the energy system changing?
- What is the currency’s institutional role?
- How are supply chains and geopolitical blocs evolving?

### Use

Structural context sets long-run priors and identifies assets with persistent tailwinds/headwinds. It rarely provides a day-trade trigger.

## Cyclical Context

### Questions

- Is growth accelerating or decelerating?
- Is inflation rising or falling?
- Is labor tightening or cooling?
- Is policy easing or tightening?
- Is credit expanding or contracting?

### Use

Cyclical context defines the regime and the dominant market concern.

## Tactical Context

### Questions

- Is market pricing inconsistent with the cycle?
- Is a policy path about to be revised?
- Are earnings revisions turning?
- Is fiscal supply changing?
- Is positioning crowded?
- Is a catalyst sequence approaching?

### Use

Tactical context supports swings and sets the weekly bias.

## Daily Context

### Questions

- What changed overnight?
- What is today’s catalyst?
- Which market moved first?
- Is the weekly thesis being activated or challenged?
- What flow windows matter?

### Use

Daily context grants directional permission.

## Intraday Context

### Questions

- Has the catalyst been accepted?
- Are causal markets confirming?
- Is liquidity improving or deteriorating?
- Is the session continuing or reversing the overnight move?
- Has the fundamental impulse decayed?

### Use

Intraday context adjusts timing and confidence. It does not replace the technical trigger.

## The Context Inheritance Rule

Daily context inherits weekly and cyclical context unless new information materially changes them. The daily note should state:

- inherited thesis;
- new evidence;
- updated probability;
- unchanged assumptions;
- changed assumptions.

## Decay

- Structural: slow, institutional.
- Cyclical: several data waves.
- Tactical: days/weeks or until pricing gap closes.
- Daily: one session, occasionally several.
- Event: minutes/hours unless it changes the tactical path.
