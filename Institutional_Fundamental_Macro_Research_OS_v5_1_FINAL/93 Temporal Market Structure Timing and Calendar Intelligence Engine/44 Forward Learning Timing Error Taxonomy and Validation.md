# Forward Learning, Timing Error Taxonomy and Validation

Use [[93 Temporal Market Structure Timing and Calendar Intelligence Engine/59 Timing Learning Promotion Calibration and Governance]].

## Error taxonomy
`CLOCK_MISSED`, `CLOCK_FALSE_POSITIVE`, `TIME_CONVERSION_ERROR`, `SOURCE_STALE`, `SCHEDULE_REVISION_MISSED`, `WRONG_DOMINANT_ROOT`, `DEPENDENCY_DOUBLE_COUNT`, `HANDOFF_MISREAD`, `EXPIRY_MECHANICS_MISREAD`, `BENCHMARK_MECHANISM_MISREAD`, `MECHANICAL_SIGN_ERROR`, `ROLL_MAPPING_ERROR`, `REFERENCE_MARKET_MAPPING_ERROR`, `EVENT_ASSIMILATION_TOO_FAST`, `EVENT_ASSIMILATION_TOO_SLOW`, `HAZARD_WINDOW_TOO_EARLY`, `HAZARD_WINDOW_TOO_LATE`, `VETO_TOO_EARLY`, `VETO_TOO_LATE`, `VALIDITY_TOO_SHORT`, `VALIDITY_TOO_LONG`, `TIME_BUDGET_ERROR`, `LATENCY_STALENESS_ERROR`, `SCHEDULER_FEASIBILITY_ERROR`, `OVER_VETO`, `UNDER_VETO`, `DATA_UNAVAILABLE_CORRECTLY_CAPPED`, `NO_ERROR`.

## Hindsight firewall
Future market outcome can reveal operational cost but cannot supply evidence that did not exist at the original cutoff.

## Required control
Compare the identical core/Donchian system with and without Timing. Measure whether Timing genuinely improves usable expectancy/drawdown once sample is sufficient, rather than merely reducing trade count.
