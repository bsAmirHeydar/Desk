# Futures Lifecycle, Roll, Expiry, Delivery and Final Settlement

Lifecycle states: `NEWLY_LISTED`, `BACK_MONTH`, `ACTIVE_FRONT`, `ROLL_BUILDUP`, `LIQUIDITY_MIGRATION`, `EXPIRY_APPROACH`, `LAST_TRADE`, `FINAL_SETTLEMENT`, and delivery/notice states where relevant.

Calendar dates define contractual boundaries; actual liquidity migration must be observed in volume/open interest or reliable market data. Never roll the analysis proxy solely by calendar convention.

Resolve reference contract, next contract, roll state/confidence, last trade, final settlement, delivery risk, proxy mapping and next roll review.
