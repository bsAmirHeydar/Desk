# Temporal Market Structure, Timing and Calendar Intelligence Engine

## Mission
V15 makes **time itself a causal institutional research object**. It studies when information arrives, which participant groups are active, when benchmarks/auctions/fixings/settlements/expiries/rebalances create mechanical demand, when funding and balance-sheet constraints intensify, and how all of those clocks modify the **validity and tradability** of an already-established fundamental/narrative thesis.

V15 is **not technical price analysis**. It cannot derive BUY/SELL from chart patterns, indicators, support/resistance, market structure, FVG/ICT, momentum, moving averages or day-of-week folklore.

## Authority
- V11 / Module 89: fundamental force, persistence, consumption, remaining pressure, reversal risk.
- V12 / Module 90: attention, narrative validity/dominance/persistence, reflexivity, transitions.
- V13 / Module 91: instrument identity and asset-family routing.
- **V15 / Module 93:** temporal state, institutional clocks, mechanical time-based forces, validity windows, timing vetoes.
- V14.1 / Module 92: final Edge, BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE permission, forward learning, execution/product handoff.

## Core outputs
`temporal_state`, `dominant_clock`, `competing_clocks`, `temporal_forces`, `session_phase`, `valid_from`, `valid_until`, `next_temporal_transition`, `next_review`, `is_true_veto`, `veto_reason`, `confidence`, and `missing_data`.
