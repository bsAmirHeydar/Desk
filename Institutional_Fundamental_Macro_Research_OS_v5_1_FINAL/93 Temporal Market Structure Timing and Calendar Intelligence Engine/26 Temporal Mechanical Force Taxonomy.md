# Temporal Mechanical Force Taxonomy

Force classes: `INFORMATIONAL_FORCE`, `BENCHMARK_FORCE`, `INDEX_IMPLEMENTATION_FORCE`, `DERIVATIVES_EXPIRY_FORCE`, `ROLL_FORCE`, `FUNDING_FORCE`, `SETTLEMENT_FORCE`, `PARTICIPANT_HANDOFF_FORCE`, `LIQUIDITY_FORCE`, `CORPORATE_CALENDAR_FORCE`.

Direction may be `UP`, `DOWN`, `TWO_SIDED`, `NONE`, `UNDETERMINED`; directional sign requires independent causal evidence. Store mechanism, strength, lifespan, affected assets, source, confidence, rival explanation and expiry.

Temporal force can reinforce, oppose, mask or add variance around V11 fundamental force.
