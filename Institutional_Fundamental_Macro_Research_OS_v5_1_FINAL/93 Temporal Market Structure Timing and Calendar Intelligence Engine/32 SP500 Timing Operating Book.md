# SP500 Timing Operating Book

Core clocks: ES/MES Globex, cash open/close auctions, SPX/SPXW and ETF options with exact AM/PM rules, quarterly futures expiry/SOQ, S&P 500 index maintenance/rebalance, broad earnings clusters, macro/FOMC/Treasury, month/quarter-end passive flows.

SP500 is broader than Nasdaq; timing should aggregate sector/constituent event clusters rather than over-weight one company. Closing mechanical flow must be separated from a fundamental narrative change.
