# Unscheduled Shock, Temporal Hijack and Recovery

Not all material clocks are scheduled. Geopolitical headlines, policy leaks, interventions, exchange outages and corporate shocks can immediately replace the expected timing regime.

## State transition
Any credible unscheduled shock can trigger:
`NORMAL -> SHOCK_DETECTION -> SOURCE_VERIFICATION -> LIQUIDITY_REASSESSMENT -> CAUSAL_RESEARCH -> NARRATIVE_TOURNAMENT -> NEW_STATE`.

## Rules
- Source verification precedes directional interpretation.
- Timestamp the first reliable public evidence, not later summaries.
- Distinguish shock time from market reaction time.
- Preserve the prior thesis as a separate object until explicitly invalidated.
- If the shock creates data uncertainty or dislocated liquidity, Permission fails closed.

## Recovery
Return to normal timing only after the dominant information process and liquidity regime are identifiable. A scheduled event that occurs during a shock becomes a **clock collision** and must be re-ranked rather than treated independently.
