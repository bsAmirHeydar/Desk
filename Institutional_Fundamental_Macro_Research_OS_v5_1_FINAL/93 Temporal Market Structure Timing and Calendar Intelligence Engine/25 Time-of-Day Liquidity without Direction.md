# Time-of-Day Liquidity without Direction

Liquidity classes: `DEEP`, `NORMAL`, `THIN`, `FRAGILE`, `DISLOCATED`, `UNKNOWN`. Use direct/proxy evidence such as spread/depth, volume, cross-venue continuity, basis stability, funding liquidity and auction state.

Liquidity may veto a **new entry** or shorten validity. It cannot turn a bullish thesis bearish. Quiet candles or narrow range alone are not proof of institutional liquidity.
