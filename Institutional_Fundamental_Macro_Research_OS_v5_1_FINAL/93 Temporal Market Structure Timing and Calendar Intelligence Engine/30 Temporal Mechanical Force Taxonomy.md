# Temporal Mechanical Force Taxonomy

V15 recognizes temporary causal forces that originate from clocks rather than new fundamentals.

## Classes
1. `INFORMATIONAL_FORCE` — scheduled/unscheduled information release changes beliefs.
2. `BENCHMARK_FORCE` — fixing/benchmark execution.
3. `INDEX_IMPLEMENTATION_FORCE` — rebalance/reconstitution/passive replication.
4. `DERIVATIVES_EXPIRY_FORCE` — option/futures expiry, settlement and hedging.
5. `ROLL_FORCE` — futures contract migration/basis trading.
6. `FUNDING_FORCE` — quarter/year-end funding, basis, balance-sheet constraints.
7. `SETTLEMENT_FORCE` — cash/collateral/value-date obligations.
8. `PARTICIPANT_HANDOFF_FORCE` — change in dominant liquidity/information providers.
9. `LIQUIDITY_FORCE` — temporal withdrawal/addition of intermediation capacity.
10. `CORPORATE_CALENDAR_FORCE` — earnings/dividend/corporate-action implementation.

## Required attributes
`direction` may be `UP`, `DOWN`, `TWO_SIDED`, `NONE`, `UNDETERMINED`. Sign is allowed only with independent causal evidence. Also store strength ordinal, expected lifespan, affected instruments, source, confidence, mechanism, rival explanation and expiry.

## Fundamental interaction
A temporal force can reinforce, oppose, mask or merely add variance around V11 force. When a temporary opposing force expires, the system must reassess whether the original fundamental pressure re-emerges rather than assuming continuation.
