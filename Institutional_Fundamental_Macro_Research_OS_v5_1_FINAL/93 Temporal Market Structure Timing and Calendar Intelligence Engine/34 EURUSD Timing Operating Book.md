# EURUSD Timing Operating Book

Core clocks: Asia -> London -> London/NY overlap -> New York; WMR 4pm London benchmark; ECB decision/press conference; Fed; Euro-area/US macro; TARGET/US banking holidays; month-end hedge/rebalance; quarter-end cross-currency basis/funding; CME EUR futures roll/expiry as proxy where relevant.

Every event is mapped bilaterally to EUR and USD calendars. ECB reference rates are information-only, not treated as WMR transactional flow.
