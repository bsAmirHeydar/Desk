# Futures Daily Settlement and Trade-Date Clock

Daily settlement, cash close and final expiry are different clocks. Resolve product-specific daily settlement methodology, maintenance break, trade-date rollover, holiday modifications and relationship to the cash close.

Settlement-marker activity can create temporary mechanical trading and accounting/margin effects. Label it `SETTLEMENT_MARKER_FLOW`, not fundamental repricing.
