# Treasury Auction, Fiscal and Government-Funding Clock

Model Treasury supply lifecycle: tentative schedule, announcement, when-issued trading, auction, result, issue/settlement and post-auction positioning. Retrieve current schedule dynamically from U.S. Treasury.

Track tenor, new issue/reopening, size, auction time, settlement date and official result metrics. Relate them causally to nominal/real yields, term premium, USD, gold and equity valuation.

Also track refunding announcements, debt-limit/funding deadlines, major tax-payment dates and Treasury cash-balance mechanics when material. No static directional rule is allowed.
