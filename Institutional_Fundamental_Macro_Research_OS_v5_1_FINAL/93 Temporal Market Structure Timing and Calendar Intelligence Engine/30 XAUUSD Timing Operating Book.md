# XAUUSD Timing Operating Book

Primary clocks: London bullion participation; LBMA AM/PM benchmark auctions; COMEX/CME gold futures settlement/roll/expiry/delivery; US macro/Fed/rates events; London/NY overlap; WMR USD benchmark flows; month/quarter/year-end; London/US holidays.

Important collisions: LBMA PM + US data/Fed; COMEX expiry/roll + benchmark; month-end WMR + gold benchmark; US holiday while London bullion is open; geopolitical shock in thin Asia.

Timing never selects gold direction. It classifies benchmark/event/funding clocks as validity constraint, true veto, temporary force or neutral.
