# Unscheduled Shock Hijack and Recovery

Geopolitical headlines, intervention, policy leaks, exchange outages and corporate shocks can hijack the expected clock.

Transition: `NORMAL -> SHOCK_DETECTION -> SOURCE_VERIFICATION -> LIQUIDITY_REASSESSMENT -> CAUSAL_RESEARCH -> NARRATIVE_TOURNAMENT -> NEW_STATE`.

Timestamp first reliable public evidence separately from market reaction. Preserve the prior thesis until explicitly invalidated. If the shock makes data/liquidity unreliable, permission fails closed.
