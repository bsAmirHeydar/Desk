# Point-in-Time and No-Lookahead Temporal Governance

Historical timing must use the schedule and methodology **known at the historical cutoff**. Never use a later reschedule before it was announced, final constituent changes before announcement, current index rules before their effective date, or later holiday/methodology revisions.

Store schedule version, announcement timestamp, implementation timestamp and actual release time when known. Missing reconstruction quality = `TEMPORAL_RECONSTRUCTION_LIMITED`.
