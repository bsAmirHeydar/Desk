# USDJPY Timing Operating Book

Core clocks: Tokyo/Japan banking day; London/NY handoffs; BoJ decision/press conference and uncertain release times; Fed/US macro; WMR; quarter/year-end funding and basis; CME JPY futures roll/expiry proxy; Japan fiscal-year-end and repatriation/hedging windows when evidenced.

Potential intervention is unscheduled unless officially communicated. Elevated intervention risk increases monitoring intensity but cannot create a fabricated intervention timestamp.
