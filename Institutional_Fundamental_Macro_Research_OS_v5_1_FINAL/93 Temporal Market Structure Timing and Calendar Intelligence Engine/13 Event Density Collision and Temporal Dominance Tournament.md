# Event Density, Collision and Temporal Dominance Tournament

Institutional days often contain overlapping clocks: payrolls plus Treasury supply, month-end plus WMR fixing, option expiry plus index rebalance, earnings plus Fed communication. Independent filters fail because the mechanisms interact.

## Collision engine
For every relevant clock score ordinally:
- causal materiality;
- time proximity;
- affected notional/participant breadth;
- expected mechanical-flow intensity;
- information novelty;
- liquidity sensitivity;
- overlap with the target instrument;
- persistence beyond the window;
- evidence quality.

## Dominance tournament
Classify each clock as `DOMINANT`, `CO_DOMINANT`, `SECONDARY`, `BACKGROUND`, or `DORMANT`. A nearer event does not automatically outrank a more powerful benchmark or funding constraint.

## Interaction types
`REINFORCING`, `OFFSETTING`, `SEQUENTIAL`, `MASKING`, `LIQUIDITY_AMPLIFYING`, `MECHANICAL_OVERRIDE`, `UNDETERMINED`.

## Decision consequence
If clocks conflict and no dominant mechanism can be established, state becomes `TEMPORALLY_FRAGMENTED`, which can justify `EDGE_CONDITIONAL` or `NO_TRADE` without changing the fundamental thesis.
