# Global Sessions, Participant Control and Handoffs

Session science is about **who can price and intermediate risk**, not mystical time windows. Track Sydney/Oceania, Tokyo, Hong Kong/Singapore, London/Europe, New York and Chicago/CME participation.

Session state machine: `PRE_OPEN -> OPEN_DISCOVERY -> POST_OPEN_STABILIZATION -> CONTINUOUS -> MIDDAY_LOWER_PARTICIPATION -> PRE_CLOSE -> CLOSE_AUCTION -> POST_CLOSE -> OVERNIGHT`.

FX additionally tracks `ASIA -> LONDON -> LONDON_NY_OVERLAP -> NY -> LATE_NY`.

At each handoff identify likely participant-control groups: banks/corporates, global macro/asset managers, dealers, passive/index desks, options dealers when observable, official sector, benchmark executors and cash-auction participants.

A thesis surviving Asia does not automatically survive London. A handoff is a review trigger, not an automatic reversal signal.
