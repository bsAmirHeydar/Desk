# Earnings, Guidance, Call and Corporate-Action Clock

For material index constituents store exact IR release time if available, BMO/AMC/intraday state, conference-call time, guidance, Q&A, index weight and sector.

Sequence: `EARNINGS_RELEASE -> GUIDANCE_PARSE -> CALL -> Q&A -> ANALYST_REVISION -> CASH_ADOPTION`. A headline beat can be superseded by guidance/call.

Also track ex-dividend dates, special dividends, splits, mergers, spin-offs and index-treatment effective dates. Do not assume a universal buyback blackout rule.
