# Options Expiry: 0DTE, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, AM/PM

Resolve exact product and series. Distinguish 0DTE, weekly, monthly, quarterly, index vs ETF option, AM vs PM settlement, cash vs deliverable, exercise style, last trading time and holiday-shortened rules.

Dealer gamma/hedging can be used only when positioning data are observable/licensed/proxied explicitly. Never infer gamma from price alone.

Generic `OPEX` is not a valid temporal state.
