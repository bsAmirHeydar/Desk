# Fundamental-Only Boundary and Timing Doctrine

Timing answers **when the thesis is usable**, not **what the thesis is**. A temporal mechanism may reinforce, oppose, mask, delay or fragment transmission, but it cannot create direction from price-only evidence.

Permitted timing evidence: official calendars, venue hours, auctions, benchmark rules, derivatives lifecycle, index implementation, macro/central-bank schedules, corporate release timing, funding/settlement calendars, holidays, DST, documented institutional flows and observable liquidity.

Prohibited direction sources: chart patterns, moving averages, candlestick patterns, RSI/MACD, technical support/resistance, ICT/FVG/order blocks, trend lines, price-only seasonality.

Price may be used only as evidence of realized response, liquidity/execution quality, valuation/discount under a separately governed value layer, or forward-learning outcome.
