# Holidays, Half-Days, Weekends and Thin Liquidity

Track exchange holidays, banking holidays, benchmark holidays, long weekends, early closes and asymmetric holidays between currency blocks.

A US holiday can reduce USD/rates/index participation while global FX/gold remain open. Half-days change auction, options and cash/futures synchronization.

Day-of-week price folklore is non-canonical unless validated point-in-time. The temporal consequence is participant availability, horizon expiry and liquidity risk—not superstition.
