# Year-End, Fiscal-Year, Tax and Regulatory Clocks

Track calendar year-end dealer balance sheets, funding/collateral scarcity, portfolio benchmark rebalance, tax-related cash needs, corporate treasury cash management, holiday staffing/liquidity, annual index reconstitution and central-bank calendar resets.

Fiscal-year boundaries differ across jurisdictions; Japan fiscal year-end is a separate JPY-relevant clock. Tax/payment effects require current official data and cannot be hard-coded directionally.
