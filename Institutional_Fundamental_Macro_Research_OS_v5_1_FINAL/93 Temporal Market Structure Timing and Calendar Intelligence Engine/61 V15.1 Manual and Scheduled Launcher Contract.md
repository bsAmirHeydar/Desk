1. execute full V11/V12/V13 six-market analysis;
2. create core Edge candidates only;
3. execute [[93 Temporal Market Structure Timing and Calendar Intelligence Engine/60 V15.1 Full-Vault Timing Clearance Production Prompt]];

5. write Timing Intelligence and `Why Not Active?` blocks into HTML;
