# Gold Benchmark Auction and Precious-Metals Clock

Track LBMA Gold Price AM/PM auction windows, London local time/DST, benchmark publication, London/New York handoff, US macro/Fed overlaps, COMEX lifecycle, month-end FX translation and official-sector calendar events.

Benchmark auction = physical/benchmark concentration mechanism, not automatic directional signal.

For every XAUUSD run report next benchmark window and classify it `NEUTRAL`, `FLOW_RELEVANT`, `DOMINANT`, or `UNKNOWN`.
