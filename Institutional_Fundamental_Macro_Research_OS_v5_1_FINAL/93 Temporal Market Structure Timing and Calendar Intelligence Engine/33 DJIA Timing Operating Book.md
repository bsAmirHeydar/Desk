# DJIA Timing Operating Book

Core clocks: YM/MYM futures, US opening/closing auctions, DJX/DIA options, DJIA constituent-change announcements, high-price constituent earnings, US macro/Fed/Treasury, month/quarter-end.

Because DJIA is price-weighted, company events in high-priced constituents can have disproportionate index influence. Point-in-time price-weight exposure is required. DJIA constituent changes are announcement/effective-date driven; do not assume a generic quarterly rebalance schedule.
