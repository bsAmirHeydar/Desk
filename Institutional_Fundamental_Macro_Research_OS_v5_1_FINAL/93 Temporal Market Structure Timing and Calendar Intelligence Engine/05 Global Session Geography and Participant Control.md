# Global Session Geography and Participant Control

Sessions matter because the **population able and willing to intermediate risk changes through time**. They are not mystical chart zones.

## Institutional session map
Track at least Sydney/Oceania, Tokyo, Hong Kong/Singapore, London/Europe, New York and Chicago derivatives participation. For the six-market profile, London and New York are especially important for FX; CME/US cash synchronization is critical for US indices; London bullion benchmarks and US rates/data are critical for gold.

BIS research documents that London and New York are the centre of gravity for FX and that liquidity is typically highest around the London open and London/New York overlap (`SRC-BIS-FX-GEO`). This is an institutional participation fact, not a directional rule.

## Participant-control objects
- local banks and corporate hedgers;
- global macro and asset managers;
- dealers/market makers;
- futures CTAs/systematic flows where observable;
- ETF/passive implementation desks;
- options dealers where observable;
- central banks/official sector;
- benchmark executors;
- cash-equity specialists/auction participants.

## Output
For each session phase identify `likely_control_group`, `liquidity_centres_open`, `information_centres_open`, `benchmark_centres_open`, and `handoff_risk`. If participant control is inferred rather than directly observed, mark it as inference and cap confidence.
