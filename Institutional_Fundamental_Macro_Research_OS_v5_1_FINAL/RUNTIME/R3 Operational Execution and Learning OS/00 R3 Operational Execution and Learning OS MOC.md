R3 turns the R1 Universal Run and R2 Prompt Process Graph into an operational, launchable, auditable system. It adds launch compilation, model-host binding, source retrieval bridging, process execution, decision-to-execution handoff, outcome/counterfactual ingestion, D4 forward telemetry, and the analytical warehouse.

## Authority boundary
R3 has **zero market-direction authority** and **zero scientific permission-creation authority**. It may block an expired/invalid/unconfigured execution handoff, but may never flip BUY/SELL, turn NO_TRADE into a trade, fabricate evidence, or mutate a sealed decision.
