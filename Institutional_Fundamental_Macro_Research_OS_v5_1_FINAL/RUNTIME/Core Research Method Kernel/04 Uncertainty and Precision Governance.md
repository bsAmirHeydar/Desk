# Uncertainty and Precision Governance

Uncertainty is decomposed into measurement, data, epistemic, model, parameter, structural, regime, reflexive, narrative, timing, tail and deep uncertainty. No universal uncertainty score is created.

A numeric probability is not rejected merely for being numeric. It must declare whether it is calibrated, estimated, model-implied, a scenario weight, ordinal confidence, expert judgment or unknown. A claim presented as **calibrated probability** requires a calibration identifier.
