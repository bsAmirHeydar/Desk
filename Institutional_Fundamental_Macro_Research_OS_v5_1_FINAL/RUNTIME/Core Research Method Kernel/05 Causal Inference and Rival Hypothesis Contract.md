# Causal Inference and Rival Hypothesis Contract

Causal authority requires temporal compatibility, an explicit mechanism and an identification state that is not `NOT_IDENTIFIED`. Existing V21 hypothesis tournaments, causal graphs, adversarial review and root-channel maps are reused rather than duplicated.

Correlation may remain an empirical association. A mechanism-supported but unidentified effect may remain `MECHANISM_SUPPORTED_NOT_IDENTIFIED`. The method kernel does not force false causal closure.
