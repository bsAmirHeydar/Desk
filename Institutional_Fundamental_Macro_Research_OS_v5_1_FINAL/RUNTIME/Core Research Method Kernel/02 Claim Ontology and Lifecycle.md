# Claim Ontology and Lifecycle

Operational claim classes are defined in `AlphaLab_Method_Claim.schema.json`. They distinguish observations, source reports, measurements, deterministic derivatives, estimates, proxies, model outputs, empirical associations, causal hypotheses, mechanism claims, narratives, regimes, forecasts, conditional forecasts, scenarios, expert judgments, decision-rule outputs, UNKNOWN, UNRESOLVED and NOT_IDENTIFIED.

Material claims can progress through PROPOSED, ACTIVE, SUPPORTED, CHALLENGED, WEAKENED, DEGRADED, SUPERSEDED, FALSIFIED, RETIRED or UNRESOLVED. Historical states are retained; method upgrades do not retroactively rewrite them.
