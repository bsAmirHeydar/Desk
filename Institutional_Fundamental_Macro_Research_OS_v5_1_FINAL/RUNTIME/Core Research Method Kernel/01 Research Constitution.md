# Research Constitution

The invariant core is deliberately small and non-negotiable: strict point-in-time knowledge, provenance, fact/inference/decision separation, first-class UNKNOWN, calibrated precision, causal discipline, contradiction visibility, source-dependency visibility, claim lineage, and auditable exceptions. Flexibility changes the **protocol**, not these invariants.

The kernel reuses existing Alpha Lab science wherever it already exists: D1 fact constitution and source lineage, V21 competing hypotheses and causal graph, D4 multiple-testing/forward-validation governance, R1 temporal firewall and seals, R2 adversarial synthesis, R3 outcome/counterfactual learning, and APL-A shadow perspective audits.
