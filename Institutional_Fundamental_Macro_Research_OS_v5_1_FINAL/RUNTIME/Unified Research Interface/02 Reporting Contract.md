# Reporting Contract — UX3

One canonical scientific result feeds one Report Model. **UX3 changes presentation only** and has zero Direction/permission authority.

The canonical human experience is:

1. **Command Center / نتیجه فوری** — immediate state, action, Force, Consumption, Remaining Pressure, Persistence, Reversal Risk, dominant/opposing forces, invalidation and next review.
2. **Market Workspace / تصویر کامل تحلیل** — one selected market at a time, with Know / Current Interpretation / Unknown and all eight analytical dimensions as a compact Lens Index.
3. **Section-Specific Deep Dive / جزئیات کامل هر بخش** — one reusable drawer populated from structured data; no 48 pre-rendered dialogs and no generic boilerplate.
4. **Scenario Navigator** — one active scenario canvas across base/up/down/reversal/range/event/no-trade/rare-shock states where material; no fake probabilities.
5. **Thinking & Philosophy** — high-level epistemic guidance and fragility perspective, separated from market Direction.
6. **Evidence & Limitations** — human-readable evidence meaning and observability limits, never raw engineering metadata.

The HTML may simplify, translate, reorder, collapse, compare or progressively reveal. It may not create claims, alter Direction/permission, hide UNKNOWN, resolve contradictions, invent probabilities, invent proprietary data, or expose internal runtime/Vault engineering in the visible product.

For repeated hourly reports, meaningful delta from the most recent verified comparable run is first-class. If no comparable run exists, the report says so rather than fabricating change.
