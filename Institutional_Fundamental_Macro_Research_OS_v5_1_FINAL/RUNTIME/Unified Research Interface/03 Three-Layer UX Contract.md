# UI2 Three-Layer UX Contract

Alpha Lab communicates one canonical research snapshot through exactly three disclosure layers: **L1 Instant Answer**, **L2 Complete Simple Overview**, and **L3 Section-Specific Deep Dive**. The scientific engine is unchanged. The UX layer has presentation authority only. Simple Persian must preserve UNKNOWN, contradiction, causal status, horizon, and evidence limitations. Explorer HTML is the canonical interactive surface; PDF is an editorial rendering of the same Report Model.
