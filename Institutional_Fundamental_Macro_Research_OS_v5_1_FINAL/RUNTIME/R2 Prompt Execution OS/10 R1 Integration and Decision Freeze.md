# R1 Integration and Decision Freeze

R2 consumes R1 Universal Runs and writes each process output as an immutable Decision-World artifact. P10 completion resolves inputs; W21 freezes the Snapshot Manifest; W23/G2 freezes Evidence; after G4, R2 transitions to `COGNITION_FROZEN` and invokes the R1 Decision Seal. Outcome World remains inaccessible until that seal exists.
