# Independent Worker Isolation

W30 Temporal, W31 Fundamental, W32 Expectations/Policy/Regime, W33 Narrative/Reflexivity/Consumption, W34 Positioning, W35 Flow, W36 Funding and W37 Mechanics/Capacity are classified independently from the same admitted evidence. The reconciler sees all states afterwards.

This prevents a strong early story from contaminating every downstream classification. Contradictions are preserved as information rather than averaged away.
