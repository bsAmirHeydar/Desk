# V20 D4 Baseline and Gap Audit

Baseline V19 already contained Module 94 forward-calibration concepts and Module 101 D3 telemetry handoff. The remaining gap was governance closure: a canonical modifier-level counterfactual framework, immutable promotion lineage, explicit runtime authority registry, positive-permission promotion law, drift/retirement semantics, and six-market forward-validation books.

V20 fills that gap without adding a second Direction engine or auto-promoting unvalidated hypotheses.

Remaining gaps are empirical, not architectural: live sample size, licensed-data coverage, regime breadth, execution telemetry quality and future selective promotion evidence.
