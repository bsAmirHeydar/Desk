# D2 Authority, Precedence and Shadow-Only Law

## Canonical but not promoted
D2 science is authoritative for **classification and state representation** inside its domains. It is not yet authoritative for **decision modification**.

- Fundamental Direction: Module 89 only.
- Narrative: transmission clearance only.
- Timing: temporal clearance only.
- D2 positioning/flow/funding/mechanics/capacity: canonical shadow-state only in V18.
- Final permission: existing V17 stack, fact-governed by Module 95.

## Forbidden in V18
- majority vote across sciences;
- additive score that quietly changes Direction;
- `crowded -> SELL` rule;
- `flow positive -> BUY` rule;
- `funding tight -> SELL` rule;
- expiry/rebalance -> direction rule;
- missing licensed data -> fabricated proxy confidence.

D3 will define causal modifier semantics only after forward validation.
