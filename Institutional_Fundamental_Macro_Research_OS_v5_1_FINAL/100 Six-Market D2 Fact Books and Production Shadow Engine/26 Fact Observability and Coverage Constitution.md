# Fact Observability and Coverage Constitution — V21.3

## Objective
Maximize **epistemic coverage**, not the illusion of omniscience.

Ten-of-ten coverage means:
1. every decision-material fact family is known;
2. every materially available source is routed when relevant;
3. direct, delayed, aggregate, proxy and model-inference evidence remain distinct;
4. private/licensed gaps are explicit;
5. source latency determines what horizon the observation can describe;
6. no missing fact family is silently replaced by price action or a convenient proxy.

## Sixteen residual observability families
1. swap/SDR transactions;
2. primary-dealer positions/financing/fails;
3. Treasury auction/refunding microstructure;
4. TRACE credit transactions;
5. options/dealer convexity;
6. securities lending/short/borrow;
7. equity auction imbalance;
8. ETF/AP/passive flow;
9. constituent/corporate fundamentals;
10. gold physical/OTC/location state;
11. FX OTC flow;
12. cross-currency/global funding;
13. volatility/convexity/dispersion;
14. alternative high-frequency fundamentals;
15. unscheduled policy/geopolitical facts;
16. depth/impact/capacity.

## Flexibility law
Not every family is read on every run. The six-market observability map declares materiality and applicable horizons. `NOT_MATERIAL` is legitimate; a silent omission is not.

## No-authority rule
This completion creates no new direction or permission authority. Facts route to their existing Fundamental/D2/Cognitive authorities under D1 truth governance.


## Flexible runtime materiality

The six-market map is the **baseline materiality prior**, not an inflexible verdict. A run may raise or lower a family's runtime materiality when context warrants it, but the receipt must preserve `baseline_materiality`, state `runtime_materiality`, and give a `materiality_reason`. A downgrade never permits a family to disappear silently; the item remains receipted as `NOT_MATERIAL`. This keeps the system flexible without making coverage discretionary or unauditable.
