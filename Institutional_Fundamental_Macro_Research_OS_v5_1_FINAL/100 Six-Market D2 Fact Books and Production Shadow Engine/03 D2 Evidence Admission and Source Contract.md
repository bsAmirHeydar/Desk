# D2 Evidence Admission and Source Contract

All evidence is governed by Module 95.

Minimum contract:
- source ID/tier;
- fact class;
- legal/instrument scope;
- participant scope if applicable;
- event/reference/publication/first-seen/retrieval clocks;
- vintage/revision semantics;
- direct/derived/inferred status;
- root-cause ID;
- materiality;
- coverage state.

Licensed source placeholders may be registered, but unless a run actually retrieves admissible evidence they remain `LICENSED_REQUIRED` or `UNAVAILABLE`. Source registration is not evidence availability.
