# D2 Coverage Semantics

Canonical coverage states:

- `FULL_PUBLIC` — material public evidence for this domain/horizon is broadly obtainable under declared scope; not a promise of perfect global visibility.
- `PARTIAL_PUBLIC` — useful public facts exist, but important participant/venue/OTC dimensions are not fully observable.
- `LICENSED_REQUIRED` — material domain visibility requires licensed/proprietary data for strong claims.
- `UNAVAILABLE` — evidence is known not to be available to the current run.
- `UNDETERMINED` — availability/scope cannot be established at cutoff.

Coverage is market × science × horizon specific. The baseline profile is conservative and a live run may downgrade it, never silently upgrade it without source evidence.
