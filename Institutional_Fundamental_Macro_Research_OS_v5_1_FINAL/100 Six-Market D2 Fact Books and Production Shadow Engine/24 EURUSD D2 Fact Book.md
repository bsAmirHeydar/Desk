# EURUSD — D2 Fact Book

## Bilateral rule
Every D2 claim is mapped to EUR block, USD block or cross-currency channel.

## Positioning
EUR futures positioning is a transparent regulated subset. OTC dealer/client positioning is materially incomplete without licensed evidence. Do not call futures positioning “global EURUSD positioning”.

## Actual Flow
Official/cross-border data and public futures activity can inform context, but comprehensive live spot dealer/client flow is `LICENSED_REQUIRED`. Price movement is not a substitute.

## Funding / Plumbing
USD and EUR money-market/funding conditions plus cross-currency basis where sourced. Basis is funding-price evidence, not automatic spot direction.

## Mechanics
FX fixings, futures/options expiries, holidays and settlement conventions. Expected fixing/hedging flow stays modelled until observed.

## Capacity
Public futures activity does not define global OTC spot capacity. Strong claims require venue/LP/order-book or licensed data.

## V21.3 — high-value fact routes

Use public FX/rates SDR transactions as a direct derivatives-transaction subset, not global spot flow. Combine with bilateral Fed/ECB reaction functions, cross-currency/global funding, Treasury/dealer state, futures positioning and options path. If live dealer/client spot flow is unavailable, the observability receipt must retain the `LICENSED_REQUIRED` gap.
