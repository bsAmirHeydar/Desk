# XAUUSD — D2 Fact Book

## Positioning
Public regulated futures positioning and public fund/ETF holdings are valuable subsets, not the full global OTC/physical market. Separate producer/commercial hedging, asset-manager/speculative categories, ETF ownership and official-sector holdings. Options/dealer exposure requires inference lineage unless directly sourced.

## Actual Flow
Separate ETF creations/redemptions or holdings changes from futures transaction activity and from physical/official-sector flows. Price/AUM change is not flow. OTC spot/client flow is `LICENSED_REQUIRED` unless directly available.

## Funding / Plumbing
USD funding and collateral, futures margin, financing/carry channels and relevant basis/lease evidence may affect transmission. Do not collapse these into “liquidity”.

## Mechanics
COMEX expiry/roll/settlement, options mechanics, benchmark/fixing conventions and ETF mechanics. Expected mechanical demand is not realized flow.

## Capacity
COMEX futures/options can inform listed-market capacity only. Do not generalize to global OTC/physical gold. Strong full-market capacity claims require venue-specific/licensed evidence.

## V21.3 — high-value fact routes

### Physical/OTC/location
Check WGC ETF/GDT, COMEX stocks/deliveries, LBMA vault/clearing and, when available, LBMA/OTC trade/liquidity data. Treat each as a subset with its own cadence.

### Derivatives/funding
Use public SDR swap transactions for rates/commodity-derivative context, NY Fed primary-dealer/funding data and Treasury auction clocks when USD-rate transmission is material.

### Capacity
For GC listed execution, prefer CME depth/liquidity evidence when available. Global OTC capacity remains a separate licensed/private layer.
