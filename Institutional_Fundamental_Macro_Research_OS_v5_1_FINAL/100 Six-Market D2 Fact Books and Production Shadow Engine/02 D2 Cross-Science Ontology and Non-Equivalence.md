# D2 Cross-Science Ontology

The following concepts are deliberately non-equivalent:

`OWNERSHIP` — stock of holdings.  
`POSITIONING` — risk state of a participant universe.  
`EXPOSURE` — sensitivity to underlying risk.  
`FLOW` — transactional change.  
`VOLUME` — trading activity.  
`OPEN_INTEREST` — outstanding contracts.  
`FUNDING` — financing conditions/capacity.  
`MECHANICS` — scheduled/structural market process.  
`CAPACITY` — ability to execute under size/venue/horizon/state constraints.

Every D2 claim names exactly which ontology node it belongs to. Ambiguity produces `UNDETERMINED`, not confident prose.
