# D2 Root-Cause Graph and Cross-Science Deduplication

The same economic process can appear in several sciences.

Example:
`Index rebalance announcement (mechanic) -> estimated passive demand (model flow) -> closing auction imbalance (mechanic/observed pressure) -> realized basket transaction (actual flow) -> changed fund holding (ownership)`.

These are not five independent confirmations. They form a linked root-cause chain.

D2 pack records parent/root IDs and `independent_root_count`. Final V18 permission ignores D2 modifiers anyway; D3 must also preserve this deduplication when integration is promoted.
