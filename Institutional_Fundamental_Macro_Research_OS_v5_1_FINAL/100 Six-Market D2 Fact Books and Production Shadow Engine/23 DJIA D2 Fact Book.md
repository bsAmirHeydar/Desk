# DJIA — D2 Fact Book

## Positioning
YM futures, DIA/fund ownership, regulatory holdings and option-derived/modelled exposure where defensible. Price-weighted constituent concentration is structural exposure context, not flow.

## Actual Flow
DIA/fund activity, futures/options transactions, corporate buyback/issuance, index rebalance and auctions. Preserve identified vs modeled actor distinction.

## Funding / Plumbing

## Mechanics
Dow index methodology changes, YM/options expiries, DIA mechanics, opening/closing auctions and corporate/period-end mechanics.

## Capacity
Use instrument-specific liquidity/capacity. A small index basket, ETF, futures and constituent cash market each have different depth and execution properties.
