# D2 Residual Limitations and D3 Handoff

V18 closes the **science-definition and evidence-governance gap** for positioning, flows, plumbing, mechanics and capacity, but intentionally leaves three things unresolved:

1. no universal licensed data entitlement is created by this Vault;
2. no D2 feature has earned final decision authority yet;
3. no causal/fuzzy integration law among the five D2 states and the existing edge has been promoted.

These are D3/D4 responsibilities. D3 designs bounded causal integration; D4 learns whether those modifiers actually add forward value and selectively promotes them.
