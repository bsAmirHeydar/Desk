# V8 Final Scientific Certification Release Notes

Version 8 adds a final scientific red-team and report-certification layer, domain scorecards, regression benchmark suites, current official source contracts and targeted monographs for previously thin domains: municipal finance, securitization, insurance, pensions, specialty finance, prime brokerage, electricity, nuclear fuel, carbon, agriculture, shipping, market-making, securities lending, volatility supply and official-sector flows.

The release does not claim omniscience or perfect prediction. It makes ten-of-ten a reproducible standard for analysis completeness and evidence integrity. Reports must be marked conditional or not certified when information is unavailable or conflicting.
