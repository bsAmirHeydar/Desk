## Preserved subject-specific foundation

This material survived consolidation because it contains subject-specific instruction for **Formula and Identity Sheet**, not the former repeated institutional wrapper.

These are organizing identities, not complete structural models.

## National Income

\[
Y = C + I + G + NX
\]

Consumption + investment + government spending + net exports.

## Nominal and Real Growth Approximation

\[
g_{\text{nominal}} \approx g_{\text{real}} + \pi
\]

Exact compounding includes an interaction term.

## Fisher Relation

\[
i \approx r + \pi^e
\]

Nominal rate ≈ real rate + expected inflation.

## Bond Yield Decomposition

\[
y_n \approx \frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}E(i_k) + TP_n
\]

Long yield ≈ expected short-rate path + term premium.

## Breakeven Inflation Approximation

\[
BE_n = y^{nominal}_n - y^{inflation\ protected}_n
\]

Contains expected inflation, inflation-risk premium, and liquidity effects.

## Equity Valuation Skeleton

\[
P = \sum_{t=1}^{\infty}\frac{E(CF_t)}{(1+r_f+ERP)^t}
\]

Price depends on expected cash flows, risk-free discount rate, and equity risk premium.

## Gordon Growth Model

\[
P = \frac{D_1}{k-g}
\]

Highly sensitive when \(k-g\) is small; useful intuition, not a complete index model.

## Credit Spread

\[
CS = y_{credit} - y_{government}
\]

Includes expected loss, liquidity, risk premium, and implementation effects.

## Current Account Identity

\[
CA = S - I
\]

At the national level, current account relates to saving minus investment.

## Balance of Payments Simplification

\[
CA + KA + FA + \text{Errors} = 0
\]

Conventions vary; understand source definitions.

## Government Debt Dynamics Approximation

\[
\Delta d \approx (r-g)d - pb
\]

Debt-to-GDP change depends on interest-growth differential, starting debt, and primary balance \(pb\) under sign convention.

## Oil Balance

\[
\Delta Inventories = Supply - Demand
\]

Adjust for measurement, trade, processing, and product-level detail.

## Annualization

\[
Annualized(m) = (1+m)^{12}-1
\]

For monthly change \(m\).

## Year-over-Year Change

\[
YoY_t = \frac{X_t}{X_{t-12}} - 1
\]

## Standardized Surprise

\[
z_t = \frac{Actual_t - Consensus_t}{\sigma(Actual-Consensus)}
\]

Enhance with revisions and components.

## Bayesian Update

\[
P(H|D) = \frac{P(D|H)P(H)}{P(D)}
\]

## Expected Value

\[
EV = P(W)\bar W - P(L)\bar L - Costs
\]

## Risk Multiplier

\[
R_{trade}=R_{base}M_{macro}M_{setup}M_{liquidity}
\]

All multipliers should be capped; macro cannot raise hard maximum risk.

## Bias Decay

\[
C_{t+\Delta}=C_t e^{-\lambda \Delta}Q
\]

Conceptual confidence decay with time and evidence quality.

## Event-Study Regression

\[
R_t = \alpha+\beta S_t+\gamma Regime_t+\delta(S_t\times Regime_t)+\epsilon_t
\]

## Realized Volatility Skeleton

\[
RV = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} r_i^2}
\]

Annualization and sampling require explicit conventions.

## Option Put–Call Parity

\[
C-P = S_0e^{-qT}-Ke^{-rT}
\]

Useful for consistency and synthetic exposures.

## Percentage versus Basis Points

- 1 percentage point = 100 basis points.
- A rate moving from 4.00% to 4.25% rises 25 basis points, not 25%.

## Contribution Arithmetic

Aggregate change:

\[
\Delta X = \sum_i w_i \Delta x_i + \text{weight/composition effects}
\]

Always inspect contribution and weights.
