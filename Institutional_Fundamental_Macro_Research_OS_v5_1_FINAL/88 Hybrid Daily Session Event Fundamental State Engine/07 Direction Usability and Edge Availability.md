# Direction Usability and Edge Availability

Direction and practical usability are separate.

## Required state dimensions

- direction and direction score;
- intensity;
- confidence;
- catalyst freshness;
- information absorption;
- repricing completion;
- flow exhaustion;
- narrative saturation;
- remaining pressure;
- persistence;
- move quality;
- path asymmetry;
- reversal risk;
- edge availability.

## Edge-availability codes

- `HIGH_FUNDAMENTAL_EDGE`
- `MODERATE_FUNDAMENTAL_EDGE`
- `LOW_FUNDAMENTAL_EDGE`
- `NO_FUNDAMENTAL_EDGE`
- `BINARY_EVENT_RISK`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

## Interpretation examples

| Direction | Consumption | Confirmation | Remaining pressure | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bullish | low | broad | high | high or moderate |
| bullish | high | weakening | low | low despite bullish direction |
| bearish | partial | broad | high | moderate/high |
| neutral | any | contradictory | low | none |
| directional | unknown | proprietary inputs missing | unknown | insufficient evidence or confidence cap |

## Prohibitions

Do not infer edge from direction alone. Do not label a mature, crowded or nearly completed repricing as high edge merely because the causal story remains valid. Do not present ordinal scores as calibrated probabilities.
