# Nasdaq 100 Fast Medium and Slow Driver Stack

## Fast drivers — minutes to one session

- Fed-path repricing and front-end rates;
- 5-year/10-year real yields and term-premium shocks;
- Treasury auctions, issuance and funding conditions;
- DXY/global-dollar impulse;
- credit spreads and funding stress;
- VIX/VXN, options distribution, expiry and hedging proxies;
- mega-cap and semiconductor headlines, guidance and regulatory/export-control changes;
- NQ/QQQ/cash session dislocations, index and passive-flow events;
- concentration unwind and short-covering proxies.

## Medium drivers — two to ten days

- earnings revisions and guidance breadth;
- AI, cloud, data-centre and hyperscaler capex revisions;
- semiconductor inventory/utilization cycle;
- margins, operating leverage, buybacks and dilution;
- concentration-adjusted valuation and equity-duration sensitivity;
- persistent credit and financial-condition changes.

## Slow drivers — weeks to years

- productivity and technology diffusion;
- secular AI capital cycle;
- fiscal and monetary regime;
- antitrust, regulation and export-control architecture;
- structural profit share, capital intensity and index concentration.

## Mandatory intraday leader order

Test policy pricing/real yields, credit, earnings/semiconductors, dollar and flow mechanics. Do not classify an NQ move as fundamentally led when only instruments inside the Nasdaq complex confirm it.
