# S&P 500 Fast Medium and Slow Driver Stack

## Fast drivers

- U.S. data and Fed-path repricing;
- nominal/real yields and curve changes;
- credit spreads, bank stress and funding;
- sector-specific earnings or guidance;
- oil and commodity shocks affecting sector weights and margins;
- VIX/options, buyback flow, expiry, passive rebalance and breadth;
- equal-weight versus cap-weight confirmation.

## Medium drivers

- earnings-revision breadth;
- revenue growth, margins and operating leverage;
- sector rotation across technology, financials, healthcare, industrials, consumer, energy and materials;
- buybacks, issuance and financing;
- credit cycle and lending standards;
- valuation and equity-risk-premium changes.

## Slow drivers

- productivity, fiscal demand and potential growth;
- profit share and capital allocation regime;
- demographics, institutional structure and policy regime;
- long-run sector composition and index concentration.

## Mandatory confirmation

A broad-market state should distinguish cap-weight leadership from broad earnings/credit confirmation. Stable headline index performance with weakening equal-weight, credit or revision breadth reduces edge availability.
