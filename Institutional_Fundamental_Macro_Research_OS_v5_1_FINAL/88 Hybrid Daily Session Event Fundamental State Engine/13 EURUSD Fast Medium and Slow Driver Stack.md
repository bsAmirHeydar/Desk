# EURUSD Fast Medium and Slow Driver Stack

## Fast drivers

- Fed versus ECB meeting-dated OIS repricing;
- U.S.-German nominal and real-rate differentials;
- DXY/global-dollar funding conditions;
- European sovereign spreads and fragmentation risk;
- U.S./European data surprises;
- options risk reversals, expiries, fixings and month-end flow proxies;
- political, tariff, energy and banking headlines.

## Medium drivers

- relative growth, inflation, labor and productivity revisions;
- current account and portfolio-flow trends;
- energy terms of trade;
- fiscal paths and sovereign-spread persistence;
- banking conditions and cross-currency basis;
- positioning and hedging demand.

## Slow drivers

- reserve-currency structure and global dollar dominance;
- euro-area institutional architecture and fiscal integration;
- productivity, demographics and capital-market depth;
- persistent external-balance and energy structure.

## Mandatory relative framing

EURUSD is a relative price. Every state must specify what changed in the euro side, the dollar side and the differential. DXY and EURUSD are not independent confirmations.
