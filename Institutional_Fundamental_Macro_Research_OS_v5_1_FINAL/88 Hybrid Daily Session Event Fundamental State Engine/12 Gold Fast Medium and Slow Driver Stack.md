# Gold Fast Medium and Slow Driver Stack

## Fast drivers

- real-yield and nominal-yield changes;
- dollar and global funding stress;
- Fed-path repricing and inflation compensation;
- geopolitical or fiscal-credibility headlines;
- safe-haven demand versus forced liquidation;
- COMEX liquidity, futures positioning proxies, options and fixings;
- Asia-London-New York handoffs and physical-premium signals.

## Medium drivers

- ETF flows and public futures positioning;
- central-bank purchase information;
- China/India physical demand and premiums;
- fiscal credibility, reserve diversification and policy uncertainty;
- mine supply, recycling and producer hedging;
- persistent real-rate or dollar regime changes.

## Slow drivers

- reserve-system diversification;
- monetary/fiscal regime and trust in sovereign liabilities;
- structural mine supply and cost curves;
- long-horizon official-sector demand.

## Mandatory conflict test

Gold can fall during stress because of cash raising. Separate safe-haven information from liquidity liquidation. Real yields and dollar are important but cannot mechanically override verified official or physical demand shocks.
