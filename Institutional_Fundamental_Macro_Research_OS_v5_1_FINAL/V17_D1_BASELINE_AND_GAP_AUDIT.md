# V17 D1 Baseline and Gap Audit

Baseline V16.1 already contained bitemporal evidence concepts, root-cause deduplication, decision materiality, retrieval firewall, event-fast-path integrity and production preflight. The gap was constitutional fragmentation: no single canonical ontology governed what counted as a fact across current and future sciences.

D1 closes that gap without claiming new data acquisition. Remaining structural gaps are intentionally moved to D2: dedicated Positioning, Actual Flow, Funding/Plumbing, Institutional Mechanics and Market Capacity sciences with source contracts and asset-specific fact books.
