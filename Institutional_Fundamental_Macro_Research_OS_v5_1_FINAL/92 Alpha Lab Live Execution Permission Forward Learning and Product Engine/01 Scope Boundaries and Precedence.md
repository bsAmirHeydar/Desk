# Scope, Boundaries and Precedence

## Precedence
1. Existing evidence, provenance, source-vintage, no-lookahead and uncertainty standards remain first-order constraints.
2. Module 89 / V11 governs force, consumption, repricing completion, remaining pressure, fact persistence, reversal risk and fundamental asymmetry.
3. Module 90 / V12 governs attention, narrative candidates, narrative validity, dominance, price control, narrative persistence, reflexivity, ignored facts and transitions.
4. Module 91 / V13 governs identity resolution, FX bilateral synthesis, commodity/index adaptation and universal routing.
5. V14 baseline governs live point-in-time, six-horizon state estimation and next-review logic.
6. Module 92 / V14.1 governs Edge, permission, execution handoff, forward learning and product delivery.

## Boundaries
- Direction is not Edge.
- Confidence is not win probability.
- Edge is not entry timing.
- Permission is not an instruction to widen stops, average losses or override the execution engine.
- Technical/microstructure evidence may refine usability and timing but may not rewrite the fundamental causal state.
- Missing licensed/proprietary data must be marked unavailable or proxied transparently; never fabricated.
- Current six-market deployment settings are environment configuration, not limits on V13 universal coverage.
