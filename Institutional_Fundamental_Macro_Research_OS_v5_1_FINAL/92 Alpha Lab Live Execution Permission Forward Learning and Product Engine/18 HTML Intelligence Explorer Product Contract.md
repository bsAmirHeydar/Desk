# HTML Intelligence Explorer Product Contract

Every completed Full-Vault run should create/update `AlphaLab_Intelligence_Explorer.html` as the complete human-facing artifact in chat.

## Required product views
- Overview
- Markets
- Compare
- Market Summary
- Full Analysis
- Sources
- Learning / Prior-Call Review
- `Why Not Active?` for every `EDGE_CONDITIONAL`

## Separation
Direction, Edge Level and Permission must be visibly separate objects. Full causal depth belongs in HTML; machine execution JSON remains minimal; email remains simple.

## Product principle
Consumer-facing, not debug-facing. Do not expose implementation logs, raw engineering terminology or redundant machine-state dumps in the visible product.
