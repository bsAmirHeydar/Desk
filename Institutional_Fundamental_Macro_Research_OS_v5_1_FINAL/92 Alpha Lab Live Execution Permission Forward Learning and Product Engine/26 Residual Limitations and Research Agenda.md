# Residual Limitations and Research Agenda

- Edge classes are not calibrated success probabilities.
- Full-system efficacy requires forward sample accumulation; no performance claim is made in this release.
- Flow, options, dealer, positioning, basis and proprietary data remain availability-dependent and must be confidence-capped when missing.
- The event-proximity veto-versus-expiry rule is still a CANDIDATE.
- The post-NFP observations in the learning ledger are observations/candidates, not universal causal laws.
- Exact MFE/MAE requires sufficiently reliable timestamped price data; unavailable path data must stay unavailable.
- Scheduled ChatGPT workflow cadence may be constrained by the platform; this Vault describes the analytical contract, not a guarantee of trigger frequency.
- A future emergency fundamental invalidation/auto-close layer must be researched separately before it may override the mechanical exit engine.
