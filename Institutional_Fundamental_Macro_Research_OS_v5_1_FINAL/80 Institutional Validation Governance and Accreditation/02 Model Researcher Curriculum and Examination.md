# 02 Model Researcher Curriculum and Examination

## Purpose

Train researchers in statistics, econometrics, numerical methods, software, validation and model risk.

## Required evidence

Derivation, coding, synthetic recovery, pseudo-real-time evaluation and challenger-model examination.

## Records

Maintain candidate or capability ID, version, assessors, materials, scores, dissent, conditions, remediation, expiry and re-review date.

## Independence

The creator of a model, dataset or decision process cannot be the sole approver. Material conflicts are disclosed and escalated.
