# 10 Failure Review and Research Incident Process

## Purpose

Investigate data errors, model failures, process breaches, source mistakes and unexpected losses without hindsight rewriting.

## Required evidence

Root-cause analysis, consumer impact, corrective action, owner and verification.

## Records

Maintain candidate or capability ID, version, assessors, materials, scores, dissent, conditions, remediation, expiry and re-review date.

## Independence

The creator of a model, dataset or decision process cannot be the sole approver. Material conflicts are disclosed and escalated.
