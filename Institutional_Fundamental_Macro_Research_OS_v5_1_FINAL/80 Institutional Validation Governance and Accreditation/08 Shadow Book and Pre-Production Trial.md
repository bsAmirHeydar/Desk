# 08 Shadow Book and Pre-Production Trial

## Purpose

Evaluate decisions without capital while preserving real-time information, latency, constraints and opportunity sets.

## Required evidence

Minimum observation count, regime coverage, calibration and operational reliability before promotion.

## Records

Maintain candidate or capability ID, version, assessors, materials, scores, dissent, conditions, remediation, expiry and re-review date.

## Independence

The creator of a model, dataset or decision process cannot be the sole approver. Material conflicts are disclosed and escalated.
