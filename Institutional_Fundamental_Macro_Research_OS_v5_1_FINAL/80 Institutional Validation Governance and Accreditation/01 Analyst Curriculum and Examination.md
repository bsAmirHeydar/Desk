# 01 Analyst Curriculum and Examination

## Purpose

Train analysts in accounting, macro, sources, point-in-time research, causal reasoning, asset transmission and written decision records.

## Required evidence

Written examinations, source-reconstruction tests, live-release simulations and defended research packages.

## Records

Maintain candidate or capability ID, version, assessors, materials, scores, dissent, conditions, remediation, expiry and re-review date.

## Independence

The creator of a model, dataset or decision process cannot be the sole approver. Material conflicts are disclosed and escalated.
