# 04 Portfolio Manager Curriculum and Examination

## Purpose

Train PMs in scenario utility, portfolio interactions, liquidity, financing, convexity and governance.

## Required evidence

Defend allocation choices across historical and hypothetical stress cases.

## Records

Maintain candidate or capability ID, version, assessors, materials, scores, dissent, conditions, remediation, expiry and re-review date.

## Independence

The creator of a model, dataset or decision process cannot be the sole approver. Material conflicts are disclosed and escalated.
