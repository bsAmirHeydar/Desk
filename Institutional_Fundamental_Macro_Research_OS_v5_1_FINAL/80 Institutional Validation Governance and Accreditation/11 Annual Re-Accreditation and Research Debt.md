# 11 Annual Re-Accreditation and Research Debt

## Purpose

Reassess people, models, sources, books and controls as markets and institutions change.

## Required evidence

Annual examinations, model review, source review, debt backlog and retirement decisions.

## Records

Maintain candidate or capability ID, version, assessors, materials, scores, dissent, conditions, remediation, expiry and re-review date.

## Independence

The creator of a model, dataset or decision process cannot be the sole approver. Material conflicts are disclosed and escalated.
