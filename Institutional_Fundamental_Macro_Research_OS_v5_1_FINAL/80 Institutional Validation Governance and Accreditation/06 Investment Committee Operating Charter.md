# 06 Investment Committee Operating Charter

## Purpose

Define agenda, materials, decision rights, dissent, conflicts, approvals and emergency procedures.

## Required evidence

Committee decisions preserve evidence, alternatives, conditions and accountable owners.

## Records

Maintain candidate or capability ID, version, assessors, materials, scores, dissent, conditions, remediation, expiry and re-review date.

## Independence

The creator of a model, dataset or decision process cannot be the sole approver. Material conflicts are disclosed and escalated.
