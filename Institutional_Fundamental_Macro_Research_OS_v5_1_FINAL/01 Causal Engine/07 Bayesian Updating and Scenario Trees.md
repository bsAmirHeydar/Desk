## The Practical Bayesian Process

You do not need formal probability software. You need disciplined updating.

1. Define mutually distinguishable scenarios.
2. Assign rough prior probabilities.
3. Identify observations expected under each scenario.
4. Observe new evidence.
5. update probabilities in proportion to how diagnostic the evidence is.
6. revise the trade permission, not just the narrative.

## Example Scenario Tree

### Question

Will softer inflation create a sustainable Nasdaq rally?

### Scenarios

**A. Benign disinflation — 45%**

- inflation softens;
- growth remains positive;
- real yields fall;
- credit stable;
- earnings revisions stable;
- Nasdaq supported.

**B. Growth scare — 30%**

- inflation softens because demand is weakening;
- yields fall;
- credit spreads widen;
- cyclicals and small caps weaken;
- Nasdaq rally is narrow or temporary.

**C. Data noise — 25%**

- one soft print;
- policy path changes little;
- yields reverse;
- no persistent equity repricing.

### Diagnostic Evidence

| Observation | A | B | C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2Y yield down and stays down | high likelihood | medium | low |
| credit spreads stable | high | low | medium |
| oil collapses | low/medium | high | medium |
| earnings revisions stable | high | low | medium |
| dollar weakens | medium/high | medium | low |
| move fades within an hour | low | medium | high |

The table prevents a single preferred story from absorbing every observation.

## Prior Quality

A prior should come from:

- macro state;
- market pricing;
- historical sensitivity;
- positioning;
- catalyst structure;
- and the reliability of the data.

Do not set probabilities from mood.

## Update Magnitude

Update strongly when evidence is:

- unexpected;
- reliable;
- directly linked to the scenario;
- confirmed across assets;
- and persistent.

Update weakly when evidence is:

- noisy;
- revised frequently;
- already expected;
- contradicted by other markets;
- or irrelevant to the current dominant constraint.

## Scenario-to-Trade Bridge

Each scenario should define:

- favored instruments;
- disfavored instruments;
- first confirmation;
- activation;
- invalidation;
- likely horizon;
- maximum acceptable chase.

A scenario without a decision bridge is analysis without operational value.

## Avoiding Narrative Capture

Narrative capture occurs when every outcome is explained after the fact.

Prevention:

- write scenarios before the event;
- define observations that would favor each;
- timestamp the note;
- preserve the prior;
- record the update;
- do not rewrite history.
