# Promotion Lifecycle and Scientific States

Canonical lifecycle:

`OBSERVATION → SHADOW → CANDIDATE → VALIDATED → PROMOTED → MONITORED → RETIRED`

Alternative exits:
- `REJECTED`
- `SUSPENDED`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

## Meaning
- **OBSERVATION:** interesting pattern, no authority.
- **SHADOW:** frozen rule is evaluated forward without affecting production.
- **CANDIDATE:** governance floors reached; eligible for formal review.
- **VALIDATED:** development + holdout + stability + multiplicity review passed.
- **PROMOTED:** explicit registry authority granted for a defined scope.
- **MONITORED:** promoted rule remains under drift and harm monitoring.
- **SUSPENDED:** authority temporarily disabled without erasing historical record.
- **RETIRED:** authority permanently removed for new runs; historical record retained.

Promotion is never an automatic side effect of one calibration run.
