# Six-Market D4 Validation Router

D4 keeps market-specific validation books for:
- XAUUSD
- NASDAQ100
- SP500
- DJIA
- EURUSD
- USDJPY

The router enforces common governance while preserving different causal channels, data-coverage limitations, session structures and execution-capacity realities.

Cross-market pooling is descriptive unless the promotion record explicitly authorizes a market-family scope and each included market passes minimum stability checks.
