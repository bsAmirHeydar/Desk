# SP500 D4 Validation Book

S&P 500 validation includes broad earnings breadth, macro/credit transmission, ES/SPY positioning, passive/index flow, buyback/issuance, options/0DTE mechanics, Treasury/funding conditions and broad-market capacity.

The book explicitly tests whether index breadth and broad flow add incremental value beyond mega-cap/Nasdaq-correlated roots rather than counting correlated equity observations as independent support.
