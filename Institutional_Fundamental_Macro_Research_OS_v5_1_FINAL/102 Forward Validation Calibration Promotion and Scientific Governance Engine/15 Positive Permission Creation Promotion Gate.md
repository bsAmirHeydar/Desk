# Positive Permission Creation Promotion Gate

V19 intentionally forbids positive D3 support from creating a new permission. D4 creates the only pathway to change that rule.

A positive rule must demonstrate incremental value against the frozen V19 baseline in forward evidence.

Mandatory conjunctive gates include:
- sufficient independent-root events under the versioned policy;
- positive holdout paired delta expectancy;
- no material lower-tail degradation beyond policy tolerance;
- stable sign across required market/regime slices;
- no evidence that performance is driven by one event cluster;
- multiple-testing disclosure/control;
- independent validator approval;
- explicit registry scope and retirement trigger.

Even after promotion, direction remains Fundamental-only.
