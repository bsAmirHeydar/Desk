# Stability, Drift and Regime Change Monitoring

Promotion is not permanent truth.

D4 monitors:
- effect-sign reversal;
- material expectancy decay;
- tail-loss deterioration;
- source/schema/version changes;
- data-coverage degradation;
- concentration into a single regime/event family;
- execution-profile changes;
- excessive unknown/missing-state rate.

## Fail-closed asymmetry
A permission-creating promotion can automatically enter `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW` on a severe drift condition. A risk-reducing baseline constraint is not automatically removed merely because its recent benefit weakens; loosening exposure requires explicit review.
