# D4 Mission, Scope and Authority Boundary

D4 answers a different question from D3.

D3 asks: **given the current facts, how should Positioning, Flow, Funding, Mechanics and Capacity modulate an already-founded edge?**

D4 asks: **did those modulations improve decisions in forward reality, for this instrument, horizon, regime and execution profile?**

D4 therefore governs *learning authority*, not Fundamental Direction.

## D4 may
- measure modifier value versus a frozen baseline;
- measure veto saves and veto opportunity cost;
- measure delay benefit/harm;
- measure confidence-cap and validity-window usefulness;
- evaluate positive-support candidates that V19 kept shadow-only;
- generate promotion proposals;
- activate only registry-approved promotions;
- suspend permission-creating promotions on severe drift;
- retire invalidated rules with a full audit trail.

## D4 may not
- use future outcomes to alter the original run record;
- silently tune a rule on its holdout set;
- create a market direction not already present in Fundamental state;
- promote a rule from in-sample performance alone;
- treat repeated observations from one causal event as independent;
- use a global aggregate result to override clearly adverse market-specific evidence;
- auto-delete historical failures from the research archive.
