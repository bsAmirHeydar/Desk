# XAUUSD D4 Validation Book

Gold validation must separate COMEX/futures evidence from OTC/physical/global gold claims. Core D4 modifier families include real-yield/USD transmission, CFTC/ETF positioning, identified ETF flow, central-bank/physical-demand proxies, funding/liquidity stress, COMEX/LBMA mechanics and venue-specific capacity.

Key promotion hazards:
- treating COMEX flow as the whole gold market;
- mixing slow central-bank/physical horizons with intraday permissions;
- double-counting USD/rates descendants of one macro root;
- using delayed COT as live positioning.
