# D4 Residual Frontier

D4 closes the original architecture roadmap but leaves empirical frontiers by design:
- forward sample accumulation;
- licensed flow/capacity coverage;
- regime-specific modifier validation;
- execution-profile calibration;
- portfolio-level capital allocation;
- probability calibration if and only if separately validated;
- future data infrastructure scaling.

These are continuing research programs, not missing architectural layers. No further major deployment is required merely to make the D1→D4 architecture complete.
