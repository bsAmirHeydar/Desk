# Capacity Gate Evaluation

Capacity is execution-profile specific. D4 never validates a capacity gate without instrument, venue, size, horizon and market-state scope.

Metrics include:
- observed spread/slippage versus predicted capacity band;
- breach frequency;
- execution failure/partial-fill incidents;
- realized cost R;
- capacity-block opportunity cost;
- stress-regime degradation.

A capacity result from one venue/size bucket cannot be promoted to a different venue or notional by analogy alone.
