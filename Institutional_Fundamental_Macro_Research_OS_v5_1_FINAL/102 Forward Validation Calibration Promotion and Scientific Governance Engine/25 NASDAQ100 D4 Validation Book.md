# NASDAQ100 D4 Validation Book

Nasdaq validation emphasizes duration sensitivity, mega-cap concentration, earnings revision state, NQ/QQQ positioning, options/dealer mechanics, systematic flow, passive flow, Treasury/funding transmission and venue-specific capacity.

D4 must distinguish:
- persistent fundamental growth/earnings edge;
- rates/funding transmission;
- mechanical option/rebalance effects;
- crowded momentum fragility;
- temporary auction/expiry distortion.

Positive-support promotion is evaluated against V19 no-trade cases only when a valid Fundamental directional thesis already existed.
