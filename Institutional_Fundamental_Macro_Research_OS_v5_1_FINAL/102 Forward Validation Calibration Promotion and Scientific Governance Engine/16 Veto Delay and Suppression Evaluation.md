# Veto, Delay and Suppression Evaluation

Risk-reducing rules are evaluated for both benefit and opportunity cost.

For a suppression rule, D4 reports:
- prevented-loss count and R saved;
- prevented-win count and R missed;
- paired delta R;
- false-veto rate;
- regime concentration;
- tail improvement;
- trigger/expiry consequences.

For delay rules, compare the same execution policy at the delayed eligible window—not an ex-post perfect entry.

A veto that avoids small losses but repeatedly removes large positive asymmetry may be rejected or narrowed even if its win rate looks high.
