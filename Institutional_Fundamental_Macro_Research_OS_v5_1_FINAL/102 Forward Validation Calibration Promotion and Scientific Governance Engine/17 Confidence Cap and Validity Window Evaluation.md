# Confidence Cap and Validity Window Evaluation

Confidence caps and shortened validity windows are not judged by narrative plausibility. D4 measures whether they improve realized decision quality.

Typical evaluations:
- survival of the original edge after the cap trigger;
- MFE/MAE before and after expiry;
- opportunity captured within shortened validity;
- avoided stale-trigger losses;
- false-expiry opportunity cost;
- effect by crowding/flow/funding state and regime.

A validity rule can be promoted independently from a suppression rule.
