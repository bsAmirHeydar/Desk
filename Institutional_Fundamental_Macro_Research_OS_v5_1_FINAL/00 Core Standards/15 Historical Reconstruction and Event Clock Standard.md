# 15 Historical Reconstruction and Event Clock Standard

## Reconstruction packet

A historical case is rebuilt using only information available at each decision timestamp.

Store:

- release/event clock;
- official documents and contemporaneous vintages;
- consensus distribution;
- market levels, curves, options, positioning, and liquidity before the event;
- scenario tree written from the pre-event information set;
- event surprise vector;
- leader and confirmation sequence;
- returns at seconds, minutes, close, days, and weeks;
- policy and balance-sheet feedback;
- what a rational desk could and could not have known.

## Counterfactuals

Compare chosen action with no trade, opposite trade, delayed trade, cleaner expression, and baseline technical strategy. Avoid judging the process only by realized outcome.
