# Confidence Calibration and Bayesian Updating

## Standard

Update beliefs according to evidence quality, independence and diagnosticity. Confidence is capped by data quality, identification, horizon and missing information. Numeric probabilities require a model, reference class or explicit judgmental procedure.

## Calibration

Repeated forecasts should be scored and grouped by stated probability. Record overconfidence, underconfidence and domain-specific errors. A new release should move beliefs less when it is noisy, revised heavily or already anticipated.

## Output

Report prior, new evidence, likelihood under primary and rival models, posterior direction, confidence cap and next evidence that would materially update the view.
