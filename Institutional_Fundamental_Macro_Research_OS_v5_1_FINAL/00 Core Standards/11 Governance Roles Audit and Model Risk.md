# 11 Governance Roles Audit and Model Risk

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Protect the research process from incentives, concentration of authority, undocumented changes, and outcome-driven rewriting.

## Roles

- data owner;
- research owner;
- independent reviewer;
- portfolio owner;
- risk owner;
- execution owner;
- model-risk owner;
- incident owner.

One person may fill multiple roles in a small operation, but the records and review gates remain distinct.

## Controls

Require source lineage, code/version hashes, parameter registry, test evidence, approval record, exception log, access control, data retention, and incident response.

## Model risk

Classify models by capital impact, opacity, data dependence, nonlinear behavior, and failure consequence. Validate conceptual soundness, process, data, implementation, performance, and use.

## Committee discipline

Committee minutes record prior probabilities, disagreements, alternatives, action thresholds, and vetoes before the outcome. Seniority does not erase a dissenting probability.

## Incentives

Score process and calibrated forecasts, not only P&L. Penalize hidden tail exposure, unrecorded overrides, and risk transfer to other books. Reward early thesis rejection when evidence changes.

## Audit test

A qualified reviewer should reconstruct the decision using stored data, code, models, and records without interviewing the original analyst.
