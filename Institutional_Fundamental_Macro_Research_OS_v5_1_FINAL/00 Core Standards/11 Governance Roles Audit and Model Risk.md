# Governance, Roles, Audit and Model Risk

## Standard

Separate research ownership, independent challenge, data stewardship, model validation and final decision authority. Maintain model inventory, limitations, approved use, change history and effective challenge. Use current supervisory model-risk principles as a governance reference without treating them as a substitute for domain judgment.

## Vetoes

No self-certification by the model owner, no undocumented overrides, no silent data substitution and no production use beyond the approved scope.
