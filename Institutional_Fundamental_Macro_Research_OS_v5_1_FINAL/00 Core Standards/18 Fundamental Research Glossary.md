# 18 Fundamental Research Glossary

## Core distinctions

- **State:** latent or observed condition at a defined horizon.
- **Impulse:** new information or flow that changes the conditional distribution.
- **Expectation:** belief distribution held by a specified group or implied by prices.
- **Pricing gap:** payoff-weighted difference between desk and market distributions.
- **Reaction function:** conditional policy response, including risk asymmetry.
- **Transmission:** ordered mechanism from shock to economic/market variables.
- **Leader:** variable or market closest to the causal mechanism.
- **Confirmation:** independent evidence expected if the mechanism is correct.
- **Permission:** restriction on allowed execution, not a candle forecast.
- **Invalidation:** evidence that materially lowers the thesis probability.
- **Expiry:** time or catalyst after which the research object must be rebuilt.
- **Vintage:** version of a datum available at a specific time.
- **Term premium:** compensation embedded in longer yields beyond expected short rates.
- **Basis:** price difference between related instruments after convention and carry.
- **Carry/roll:** expected P&L if market conditions and curves evolve mechanically.
- **Liquidity:** ability to transact size with bounded cost and delay.
- **Calibration:** agreement between forecast probabilities and observed frequencies.
- **Resolution:** ability to discriminate high- and low-probability outcomes.
- **Capacity:** scalable capital after impact, cost, financing, and crowding.
- **Counterfactual:** outcome under an alternative action or untreated state.
