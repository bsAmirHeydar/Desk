# Fundamental Research Glossary

## Canonical terms

- **state**: best estimate of current economic, financial, institutional or physical condition;
- **expectation**: belief or market-implied distribution about future state;
- **pricing gap**: difference between evidence-weighted and priced distributions;
- **transmission**: mechanism carrying a shock across balance sheets or markets;
- **risk premium**: compensation for bearing uncertainty, liquidity or tail risk;
- **vintage**: version of data available at a specified time;
- **rival model**: plausible alternative causal explanation;
- **invalidation**: evidence that materially weakens the conclusion;
- **expiry**: time or condition after which the conclusion requires re-estimation;
- **certification**: verdict that the analysis passed the required integrity and completeness gates.
