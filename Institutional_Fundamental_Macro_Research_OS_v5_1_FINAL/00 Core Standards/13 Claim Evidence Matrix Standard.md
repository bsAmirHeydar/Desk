# 13 Claim Evidence Matrix Standard

## Required table

| ID | Claim | Type | Source key | Series/table/page | Published | Available internally | Transformation/model | Uncertainty | Rival evidence | Decision field |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- assign one ID per material claim;
- separate observation from inference;
- cite source location, not only homepage;
- preserve contradictions;
- mark data available after the cutoff as prohibited;
- connect model outputs to model cards;
- connect decisions to claim IDs;
- store document versions or checksums where permitted.

## Strength grading

`A` direct official/contractual observation; `B` reproducible derived measurement; `C` validated model; `D` proxy; `E` anecdote. Grade relevance and reliability separately.
