# Research Validation, Costs, Capacity and Retirement

## Standard

A research conclusion must distinguish explanatory validity from decision value. Validate against appropriate baselines, account for data revisions, costs, liquidity, capacity and opportunity cost, and retire a framework when evidence degrades or the institutional mechanism changes.

## Required audit

- point-in-time evidence;
- benchmark and rival model;
- repeated out-of-sample or historical reconstruction;
- implementation and liquidity constraints;
- failure and drift record;
- retirement or revision rule.
