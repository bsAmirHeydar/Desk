# 10 Research Validation Costs Capacity and Retirement

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Establish a promotion path from idea to production and a disciplined retirement path when evidence deteriorates.

## Lifecycle

1. hypothesis;
2. data and point-in-time feasibility;
3. mechanism and rival models;
4. exploratory analysis;
5. frozen specification;
6. pseudo-real-time validation;
7. paper/shadow deployment;
8. limited production;
9. scaled production;
10. monitoring, modification, or retirement.

## Costs

Include commissions, spread, market impact, delay, financing, borrow, option decay, futures roll, taxes where relevant, data cost, operational overhead, and rejected-trade opportunity cost.

## Capacity

Estimate capacity as a distribution by liquidity state, not a fixed AUM number. Test average participation, stressed participation, exit horizon, crowding, and price impact feedback.

## Multiple research trials

Maintain a trial ledger including failed and abandoned variants. Use out-of-sample tests, economic priors, complexity penalties, and reality-check style procedures. A hidden graveyard inflates apparent discovery quality.

## Monitoring

Track input drift, missingness, forecast error, calibration, decision frequency, realized cost, feature contribution, regime concentration, and analyst overrides.

## Retirement

Predefine conditions: persistent calibration failure, edge below cost, structural source change, capacity collapse, unstable sign, better replacement, or unmaintainable operational dependence. Preserve the retired model and full decision history.
