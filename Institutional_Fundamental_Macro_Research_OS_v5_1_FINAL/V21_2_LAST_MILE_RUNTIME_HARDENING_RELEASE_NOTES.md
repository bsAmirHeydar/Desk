# Alpha Lab Fundamental OS — V21.2.0

## Release purpose
Last-mile semantic/runtime hardening of the V21 cognitive brain with high flexibility and low architectural bloat.

## Main changes
1. Context-sensitive applicability profiles.
2. Upstream/downstream propagation integrity with explicit, auditable exceptions.
3. D1 evidence-pack referential validation for load-bearing evidence.
4. Structured final review/invalidation triggers.
5. Bilateral policy-reaction bundle for FX.
6. Horizon-aware regime scope.
7. Derived/cross-checked hypothesis root and contradiction counts.
8. Expanded cognitive telemetry for D4 learning.
9. D4 calibrator/schema compatibility fixed.
10. D4 calibration record resolution for calibrated scenario probabilities.
11. Focused topic-to-canonical retrieval dependencies.

No new direction authority, no science voting, no unvalidated portfolio enforcement, and no numeric probability without D4 calibration.


## Deliberate non-goals
V21.2 deliberately does **not** create a D5, a new directional science, a master score, uncalibrated numeric probabilities, an enforced portfolio rule, a deterministic live-data ingestion platform, or a production analog engine. Those changes would increase complexity without current forward evidence. Portfolio remains `SHADOW`; analog retrieval remains a governed research capability until a sufficient point-in-time state store exists; deterministic data collectors remain an infrastructure workstream rather than a cognitive claim.

## Flexibility contract
Flexibility means context-sensitive applicability, explicit unknown/unavailable states, qualitative scenario branching proportional to material ambiguity, and auditable propagation exceptions. Flexibility does **not** mean silent omission, self-declared evidence independence, fabricated probabilities, untraceable load-bearing claims, or bypassing causal contradictions.
