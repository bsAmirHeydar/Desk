# 09 Output Contract and Quality Gates

A valid response contains a data cutoff, Vault Reading Ledger, claim-evidence ledger, separate multihorizon states, market-implied baseline, pricing gap, causal and rival models, asset-specific mechanics, portfolio implications, scenarios, decision states, confidence caps, invalidation, expiry and unknowns.

## Failure conditions

- the Vault was not opened or cited in the reading ledger;
- current claims lack current sources;
- historical information leaks past the cutoff;
- facts, estimates and judgments are mixed;
- unavailable licensed data are fabricated;
- a price pattern or indicator is used;
- confidence is presented as calibrated without evidence;
- contradictions are suppressed;
- decision states are issued without pricing, scenarios, costs and risk;
- raw URLs replace source attribution when citations are available.
# V8 SCIENTIFIC CERTIFICATION GATE
Before finalizing, open and apply `81 Final Scientific Red-Team and Analysis Certification/00 Final Scientific Red-Team and Analysis Certification MOC`. Run the relevant domain scorecard and benchmark suite. The answer must begin and end with a certification verdict: CERTIFIED — FULL, CERTIFIED — CONDITIONAL, or NOT CERTIFIED. Do not label the answer FULL if any load-bearing claim lacks an exact source locator, current data are stale, historical evidence crosses the cutoff, a material domain was not retrieved, a serious rival model was ignored, or unavailable proprietary information was invented. Include the 60-point certification scorecard and failed gates.
