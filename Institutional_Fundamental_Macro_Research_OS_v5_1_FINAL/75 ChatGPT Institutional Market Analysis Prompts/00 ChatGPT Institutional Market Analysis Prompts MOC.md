## Core doctrine inherited from the Vault

The prompts enforce the same chain used throughout this operating system:

```text
Point-in-time evidence
→ state distribution
→ expectations and priced distribution
→ causal and rival models
→ cross-asset transmission
→ positioning, liquidity and flow
→ multihorizon scenario/payoff distribution
→ portfolio-aware permission
→ technical execution handoff
→ attribution and calibration
```

The uploaded Vault is a **methodology and research architecture**, not a substitute for live or historical point-in-time data. CURRENT mode must browse for current evidence. HISTORICAL mode must reconstruct only the information that was available by the specified cutoff.
