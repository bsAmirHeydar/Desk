# V21.3 Migration and Precedence Map

## Baseline

`V21.2.0` is the required baseline. V21.3 is a fact-observability/source-coverage completion release; it is not D5 and creates no new Direction or permission authority.

## Precedence

1. **Module 95** still decides whether evidence is admissible as Fact/Proxy/Derived/Inference and enforces point-in-time lineage.
2. **Module 89** remains the only Fundamental Direction authority.
3. **Modules 96–99** remain canonical D2 source-state sciences and do not directly change permission.
4. **Module 100 V21.3 observability contracts** decide what fact families must be explicitly covered, unavailable, delayed, proxy-only, subscription-required or licensed-required for the current instrument/horizon/context.
5. **Module 101** remains the only D2 causal-modulation authority.
6. **Module 102** remains the only forward promotion authority.
7. **Module 103** remains the cognitive orchestrator and keeps all V21.2 semantic-integrity rules.

## Materiality migration

The six-market observability map supplies a **baseline materiality prior**. It is deliberately not an inflexible hard-code. Runtime materiality may be raised or lowered when the actual causal context warrants it, but the Observability Receipt must preserve the baseline, record the runtime value, and explain the change. A downgraded family remains in the receipt as `NOT_MATERIAL`; it never disappears silently.

## Coverage semantics

`PUBLIC_PARTIAL` / `PARTIAL_DIRECT` does not mean complete market observability. Public SDR transactions do not reveal complete OTC client direction; COT and filings are delayed structural evidence; options proxies are not dealer books; futures depth is not OTC depth; London/COMEX gold data are not the full global gold market. Private gaps stay explicit rather than being filled by inference.

## Production migration

The production entrypoint remains Module 103. Before D2 synthesis, create the V21.3 Fact Coverage Plan and Fact Observability Receipt. For D2 pack `1.1.0`, the receipt is mandatory. V21.2 D2 pack `1.0.0` remains readable for historical compatibility only.
