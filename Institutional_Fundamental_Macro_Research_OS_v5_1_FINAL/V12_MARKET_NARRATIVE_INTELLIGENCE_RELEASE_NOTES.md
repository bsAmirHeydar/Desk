# V12 Market Narrative Intelligence Release Notes

## Release

V12.0 adds the Market Attention, Narrative Selection, Reflexivity, Driver Dominance and Daily Intelligence Brain as Module 90.

## Major changes

- separates fact, fundamental state, attention and narrative;
- separates narrative validity from dominance and price control;
- separates fact persistence from narrative persistence;
- creates candidate generation, tournament, ignored-fact and transition engines;
- adds cross-asset narrative graphs and reflexivity controls;
- adds Nasdaq 100, S&P 500, Gold and EURUSD narrative books;
- adds a production daily prompt and six launch modes;
- adds schema, examples, 30 benchmarks and deterministic validators;
- preserves V11 and V10.4 record compatibility.

## Certification

`CERTIFIED — CONDITIONAL`: architecture, patch lifecycle and internal validators pass. Empirical attention shares, proprietary adoption and universal transition probabilities are not certified.
