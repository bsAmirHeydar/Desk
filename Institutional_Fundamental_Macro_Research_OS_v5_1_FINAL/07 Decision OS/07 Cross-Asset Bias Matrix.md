## Reading Divergence

### Confirming divergence
Different assets react at different speeds but share one causal direction.

### Warning divergence
A supposed risk-on equity move occurs while credit widens and breadth narrows.

### Regime-changing divergence
A historically stable relationship breaks repeatedly because the dominant driver changed.

### Flow divergence
Temporary decoupling caused by rebalance, expiry, auction, or localized positioning.
