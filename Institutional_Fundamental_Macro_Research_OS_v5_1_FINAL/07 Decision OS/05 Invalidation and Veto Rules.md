## Preserved subject-specific foundation

This material survived consolidation because it contains subject-specific instruction for **Invalidation and Veto Rules**, not the former repeated institutional wrapper.

Invalidation answers **“What makes the thesis false?”** A veto answers **“What makes trading it unacceptable right now?”**

## Four Types of Invalidation

### State invalidation
The assumed economic/financial condition is wrong.

Example: a disinflation thesis fails because breadth, persistence, and leading price pressures reaccelerate.

### Expectations invalidation
The market has already repriced the thesis.

Example: expected easing is fully embedded; further benign data no longer creates a gap.

### Transmission invalidation
The expected channel does not operate.

Example: yields fall but NQ cannot rally, while credit and breadth deteriorate—suggesting growth/risk-premium damage dominates.

### Horizon invalidation
The thesis is not actionable in the selected window.

Example: a six-month fiscal story cannot justify a five-minute entry without a current transmission catalyst.

## Veto Categories

- imminent binary catalyst;
- unverified headline;
- abnormal spread/liquidity;
- conflicting core markets;
- extreme gap reducing reward-to-risk;
- qualified implementation condition absent;
- predeclared risk limit too wide;
- volatility outside validated operating range;
- correlated portfolio exposure already high;
- thesis expired;
- psychological/process impairment.

## Invalidation Ladder

Define before trading:

1. **Warning:** evidence weakens.
2. **Downgrade:** permission reduces.
3. **Fundamental invalidation:** thesis retired.
4. **implementation invalidation:** position exits.
5. **Emergency veto:** no new trades.

## Cross-Asset Invalidation Examples

### NQ long
Potential invalidation:
- real yields and dollar rise persistently;
- semiconductor/mega-cap leadership fails;
- breadth and credit weaken;
- easing expectations arise from credit stress rather than benign disinflation.

### Gold long
Potential invalidation:
- real yields rise;
- dollar strengthens;
- inflation/tail-risk demand fades;
- price fails despite supportive macro inputs, implying positioning exhaustion.

### Oil long
Potential invalidation:
- physical tightness does not appear in spreads/inventories;
- demand indicators deteriorate;
- geopolitical premium reverses;
- producer response increases supply elasticity.

## Veto Precedence

```text
Safety / market integrity
> hard portfolio risk
> fundamental invalidation
> catalyst/liquidity veto
> implementation invalidation
> directional preference
```

Directional bias is the lowest authority in the risk hierarchy.

## Writing Strong Invalidations

Weak:
- “I am wrong if price goes down.”

Strong:
- “The long thesis is invalid if the expected decline in real yields fails after the catalyst, the dollar strengthens, and NQ leadership narrows, because the assumed benign policy-repricing channel is absent.”

The stop can be price-based. The thesis invalidation must be causal.
