## Fundamental No-Trade Conditions

- The priced baseline is unclear.
- More than one opposing transmission channel has similar strength.
- The dominant driver changes repeatedly within the session.
- Data quality or revisions make the state estimate unstable.
- The thesis requires an unverified political/geopolitical assumption.
- Market prices have already closed the expected gap.
- Cross-asset markets are closed or too illiquid to confirm.
- A high-impact catalyst falls inside the expected holding period.
- The asset is reacting primarily to a driver not yet understood.

## Market-Structure No-Trade Conditions

- no valid fundamental-state persistence;
- no structural temporary counter-move;
- no valid trigger;
- invalidation distance is excessive;
- target offers insufficient expectancy;
- opening gap destroys entry location;
- volatility regime is outside tested conditions.

## Flow and Plumbing No-Trade Conditions

- disorderly funding or spread behavior;
- expiry/auction/rebalance dominates but direction is unknowable;
- liquidation cascade;
- exchange/broker/data integrity issue;
- holiday/thin liquidity;
- material basis divergence between CFD and underlying/futures.

## Cognitive No-Trade Conditions

- thesis created after seeing the desired chart direction;
- desire to recover a prior loss;
- inability to state invalidation;
- excessive research without a clear output;
- narrative attachment;
- sleep/stress condition that impairs execution.

## Ambiguity Test

Set `NO_TRADE` when you cannot complete this sentence:

> “The market is most likely repricing ______ through ______, and I will know this is wrong if ______.”

## Re-entry into Eligibility

A market returns from `NO_TRADE` only when:

1. the information gap resolves;
2. relevant markets confirm;
3. liquidity normalizes;
4. scenario probabilities separate;
5. observable market-state confirmation reappears.

Time alone does not remove a veto.
