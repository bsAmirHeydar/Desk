# Liquidity Resiliency and Stress Capacity

A market can display high normal-time volume yet have low stress capacity. Track separately:
- normal depth/spread;
- shock spread/depth deterioration;
- recovery/replenishment speed;
- discontinuity/gap risk;
- venue outages/limit states;
- cross-venue fragmentation.

Capacity grades are horizon- and regime-dependent and should widen uncertainty during event windows or operational anomalies.
