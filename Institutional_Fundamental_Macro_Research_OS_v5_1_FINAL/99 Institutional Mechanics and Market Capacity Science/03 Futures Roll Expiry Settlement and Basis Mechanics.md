# Futures Roll, Expiry, Settlement and Basis Mechanics

Distinguish:
- contract expiry calendar;
- roll convention;
- first notice/last trade where relevant;
- cash vs physical settlement;
- basis/carry state;
- spread transaction volume;
- open-interest migration;
- actual position transfer.

High roll volume is not fresh directional risk by default. Calendar spread activity can dominate volume while economic exposure remains substantially unchanged.
