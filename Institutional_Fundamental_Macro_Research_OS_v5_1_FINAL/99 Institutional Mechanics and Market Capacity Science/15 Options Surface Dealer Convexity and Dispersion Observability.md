# Options Surface, Dealer Convexity and Dispersion Observability

## Observable layers
Public listed-options statistics can establish volume and open interest; subscription participant/open-close data can improve transaction classification. Implied volatility, skew, term structure, 0DTE share and index-vs-single-name dispersion characterize path risk.

## Dealer boundary
Dealer gamma/vanna/charm are **not observed** from OI alone. A dealer-exposure estimate requires an explicit counterparty/sign model or direct licensed participant data. Store the model and uncertainty.

## Decision role
Vol/convexity/dispersion modify path, fragility, timing and execution risk. They do not create Fundamental Direction.
