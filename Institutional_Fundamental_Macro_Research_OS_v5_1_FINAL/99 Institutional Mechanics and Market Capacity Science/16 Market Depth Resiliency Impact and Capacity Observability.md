# Market Depth, Resiliency, Impact and Capacity Observability

## Capacity tuple
`instrument x venue x order size x execution horizon x volatility state x spread/depth/resiliency`.

## Direct listed-market evidence
CME top-of-book/depth/MBO and exchange liquidity analytics can materially improve NQ/ES/YM/GC/6E/6J execution-capacity estimates.

## What not to do
- volume is not depth;
- displayed depth is not latent liquidity;
- depth is not resiliency;
- futures capacity is not global OTC FX/gold capacity;
- normal-state capacity cannot be copied into event/stress state.

## Production use
If live depth is unavailable, use public analytical/context evidence only and state the capacity gap. A strong OTC-capacity claim requires venue/LP/licensed evidence.
