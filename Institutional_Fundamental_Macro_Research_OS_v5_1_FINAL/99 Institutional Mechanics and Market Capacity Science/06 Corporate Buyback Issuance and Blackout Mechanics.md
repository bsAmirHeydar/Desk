# Corporate Buyback, Issuance and Blackout Mechanics

Keep separate:
- authorized buyback capacity;
- disclosed executed repurchases;
- estimated daily buyback capacity;
- blackout/window eligibility;
- debt/equity issuance calendar;
- realized syndication/issuance.

Authorization does not equal execution. Blackout heuristics are not universal facts unless tied to issuer-specific disclosures/rules.
