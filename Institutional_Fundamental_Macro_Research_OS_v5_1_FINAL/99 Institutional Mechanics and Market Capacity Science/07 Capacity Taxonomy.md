# Market Capacity Taxonomy

Capacity is the notional/risk that can be executed under a declared constraint, not a universal property of a ticker.

Dimensions:
- spread;
- displayed/non-displayed depth where sourced;
- turnover;
- order-book replenishment/resiliency;
- volatility;
- participation rate;
- order size;
- execution horizon;
- venue fragmentation;
- market impact/slippage;
- open interest for derivatives;
- OTC/physical opacity.

Required statement: `capacity estimate applies to <instrument/venue/size/horizon/state>`.
