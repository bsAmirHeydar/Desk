# Index Rebalance and Reconstitution Mechanics

Separate:
1. methodology/eligibility facts;
2. announced constituent/weight changes;
3. effective date/time;
4. estimated passive assets tracking benchmark;
5. modelled expected trade notional;
6. realized auction/transaction evidence.

Expected passive flow is a model inference until transaction evidence confirms execution. Market anticipatory trading may front-run the effective event and is a separate causal/flow observation.
