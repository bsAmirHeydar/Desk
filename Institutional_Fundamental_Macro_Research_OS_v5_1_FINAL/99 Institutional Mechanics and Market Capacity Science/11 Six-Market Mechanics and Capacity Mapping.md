# Six-Market Mechanics and Capacity Mapping

## XAUUSD
COMEX futures/options expiries/rolls, relevant benchmark/fixing/settlement structures, ETF mechanics, physical/OTC venue limitations. Full global capacity is not inferable from COMEX alone.

## NASDAQ100 / SP500 / DJIA
Index methodology/rebalances, index futures/options expiries, ETF mechanics, opening/closing auctions, mega-cap concentration, corporate buybacks/issuance and venue capacity.

## EURUSD / USDJPY
FX fixings, futures expiries, option expiries/cut conventions where directly sourced, central-bank/official settlement mechanics, holiday calendars. OTC market depth/capacity generally needs venue/LP/licensed data for strong claims.
