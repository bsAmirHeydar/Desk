# Mechanics Ontology and Direction Boundary

Canonical mechanic families:
- options expiry / exercise / assignment;
- futures expiry, first notice, roll and settlement;
- benchmark fixings;
- opening/closing auctions;
- index rebalance/reconstitution;
- ETF rebalance/creation mechanics;
- corporate buyback blackout/authorization windows;
- Treasury auctions/settlements where mechanically relevant;
- month/quarter/year-end benchmark and balance-sheet processes;
- margin/clearing changes;
- holiday/half-day and venue microstructure constraints.

A known event can create an expected-flow hypothesis, but not Fundamental Direction.
