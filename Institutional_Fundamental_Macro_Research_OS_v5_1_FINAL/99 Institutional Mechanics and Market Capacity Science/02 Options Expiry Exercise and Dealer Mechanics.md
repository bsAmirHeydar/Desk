# Options Expiry, Exercise and Dealer Mechanics

Preserve:
- venue/product;
- expiry style and timestamp;
- settlement method;
- exercise rules;
- OI/strike evidence cutoff;
- modeled dealer inventory assumptions;
- estimated hedge sensitivity;
- realized transaction confirmation.

Gamma/vanna/charm pressure is a derived/model state unless direct inventory is known. Expiry concentration can matter greatly for intraday path without becoming a macro directional cause.
